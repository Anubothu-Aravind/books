[YLT | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 6 | Verse 6] 'But thou, when thou mayest pray, go into thy chamber, and having shut thy door, pray to thy Father who [is] in secret, and thy Father who is seeing in secret, shall reward thee manifestly.
