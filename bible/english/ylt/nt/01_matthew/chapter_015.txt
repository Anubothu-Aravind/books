[YLT | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 15 | Verse 2] 'Wherefore do thy disciples transgress the tradition of the elders? for they do not wash their hands when they may eat bread.'

[YLT | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 15 | Verse 8] This people doth draw nigh to Me with their mouth, and with the lips it doth honour Me, but their heart is far off from Me;

[YLT | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 15 | Verse 20] these are the things defiling the man; but to eat with unwashen hands doth not defile the man.'

[YLT | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 15 | Verse 29] And Jesus having passed thence, came nigh unto the sea of Galilee, and having gone up to the mountain, he was sitting there,
