[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 1] After these things there was a feast of the Jews, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem,
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 2] and there is in Jerusalem by the sheep -[gate] a pool that is called in Hebrew Bethesda, having five porches,
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 3] in these were lying a great multitude of the ailing, blind, lame, withered, waiting for the moving of the water,
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 4] for a messenger at a set time was going down in the pool, and was troubling the water, the first then having gone in after the troubling of the water, became whole of whatever sickness he was held.
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 5] and there was a certain man there being in ailment thirty and eight years,
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 6] him Jesus having seen lying, and having known that he is already a long time, he saith to him, 'Dost thou wish to become whole?'
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 7] The ailing man answered him, 'Sir, I have no man, that, when the water may be troubled, he may put me into the pool, and while I am coming, another doth go down before me.'
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 8] Jesus saith to him, 'Rise, take up thy couch, and be walking;'
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 9] and immediately the man became whole, and he took up his couch, and was walking, and it was a sabbath on that day,
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 10] the Jews then said to him that hath been healed, 'It is a sabbath; it is not lawful to thee to take up the couch.'
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 11] He answered them, 'He who made me whole — that one said to me, Take up thy couch, and be walking;'
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 12] they questioned him, then, 'Who is the man who is saying to thee, Take up thy couch and be walking?'
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 13] But he that was healed had not known who he is, for Jesus did move away, a multitude being in the place.
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 14] After these things, Jesus findeth him in the temple, and said to him, 'Lo, thou hast become whole; sin no more, lest something worse may happen to thee.'
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 15] The man went away, and told the Jews that it is Jesus who made him whole,
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 16] and because of this were the Jews persecuting Jesus, and seeking to kill him, because these things he was doing on a sabbath.
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 17] And Jesus answered them, 'My Father till now doth work, and I work;'
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 18] because of this, then, were the Jews seeking the more to kill him, because not only was he breaking the sabbath, but he also called God his own Father, making himself equal to God.
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 19] Jesus therefore responded and said to them, 'Verily, verily, I say to you, The Son is not able to do anything of himself, if he may not see the Father doing anything; for whatever things He may do, these also the Son in like manner doth;
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 20] for the Father doth love the Son, and doth shew to him all things that He himself doth; and greater works than these He will shew him, that ye may wonder.
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 21] 'For, as the Father doth raise the dead, and doth make alive, so also the Son doth make alive whom he willeth;
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 22] for neither doth the Father judge any one, but all the judgment He hath given to the Son,
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 23] that all may honour the Son according as they honour the Father; he who is not honouring the Son, doth not honour the Father who sent him.
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 24] 'Verily, verily, I say to you — He who is hearing my word, and is believing Him who sent me, hath life age-during, and to judgment he doth not come, but hath passed out of the death to the life.
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 25] 'Verily, verily, I say to you — There cometh an hour, and it now is, when the dead shall hear the voice of the Son of God, and those having heard shall live;
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 26] for, as the Father hath life in himself, so He gave also to the Son to have life in himself,
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 27] and authority He gave him also to do judgment, because he is Son of Man.
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 28] 'Wonder not at this, because there doth come an hour in which all those in the tombs shall hear his voice,
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 29] and they shall come forth; those who did the good things to a rising again of life, and those who practised the evil things to a rising again of judgment.
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 30] 'I am not able of myself to do anything; according as I hear I judge, and my judgment is righteous, because I seek not my own will, but the will of the Father who sent me.
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 31] 'If I testify concerning myself, my testimony is not true;

[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 33] ye have sent unto John, and he hath testified to the truth.
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 34] 'But I do not receive testimony from man, but these things I say that ye may be saved;

[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 36] 'But I have the testimony greater than John's, for the works that the Father gave me, that I might finish them, the works themselves that I do, they testify concerning me, that the Father hath sent me.
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 37] 'And the Father who sent me Himself hath testified concerning me; ye have neither heard His voice at any time, nor His appearance have ye seen;
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 38] and His word ye have not remaining in you, because whom He sent, him ye do not believe.
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 39] 'Ye search the Writings, because ye think in them to have life age-during, and these are they that are testifying concerning me;
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 40] and ye do not will to come unto me, that ye may have life;
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 41] glory from man I do not receive,

[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 44] how are ye able — ye — to believe, glory from one another receiving, and the glory that [is] from God alone ye seek not?
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 45] 'Do not think that I will accuse you unto the Father; there is who is accusing you, Moses — in whom ye have hoped;
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 46] for if ye were believing Moses, ye would have been believing me, for he wrote concerning me;
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 5 | Verse 47] but if his writings ye believe not, how shall ye believe my sayings?'
