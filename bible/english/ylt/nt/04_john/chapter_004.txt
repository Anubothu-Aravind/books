[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 4 | Verse 2] (though indeed Jesus himself was not baptizing, but his disciples,)
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 4 | Verse 3] he left Judea and went away again to Galilee,

[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 4 | Verse 6] and there was there a well of Jacob. Jesus therefore having been weary from the journeying, was sitting thus on the well; it was as it were the sixth hour;

[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 4 | Verse 9] the Samaritan woman therefore saith to him, 'How dost thou, being a Jew, ask drink from me, being a Samaritan woman?' for Jews have no dealing with Samaritans.
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 4 | Verse 10] Jesus answered and said to her, 'If thou hadst known the gift of God, and who it is who is saying to thee, Give me to drink, thou wouldest have asked him, and he would have given thee living water.'

[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 4 | Verse 14] but whoever may drink of the water that I will give him, may not thirst — to the age; and the water that I will give him shall become in him a well of water, springing up to life age-during.'
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 4 | Verse 15] The woman saith unto him, 'Sir, give me this water, that I may not thirst, nor come hither to draw.'
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 4 | Verse 16] Jesus saith to her, 'Go, call thy husband, and come hither;'

[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 4 | Verse 18] for five husbands thou hast had, and, now, he whom thou hast is not thy husband; this hast thou said truly.'
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 4 | Verse 19] The woman saith to him, 'Sir, I perceive that thou art a prophet;

[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 4 | Verse 24] God [is] a Spirit, and those worshipping Him, in spirit and truth it doth behove to worship.'
[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 4 | Verse 25] The woman saith to him, 'I have known that Messiah doth come, who is called Christ, when that one may come, he will tell us all things;'

[YLT | EN | NT | John | Chapter 4 | Verse 28] The woman then left her water-jug, and went away to the city, and saith to the men,
