[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 1] And Josiah maketh in Jerusalem a passover to Jehovah, and they slaughter the passover-offering on the fourteenth of the first month,
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 2] and he stationeth the priests over their charges, and strengtheneth them for the service of the house of Jehovah,
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 3] and saith to the Levites — who are teaching all Israel — who are sanctified to Jehovah, 'Put the holy ark in the house that Solomon son of David king of Israel built; it is not to you a burden on the shoulder. 'Now, serve Jehovah your God, and His people Israel,
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 4] and prepare, by the house of your fathers, according to your courses, by the writing of David king of Israel, and by the writing of Solomon his son,
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 5] and stand in the sanctuary, by the divisions of the house of the fathers of your brethren, sons of the people, and the portion of the house of a father of the Levites,
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 6] and slaughter the passover-offering and sanctify yourselves, and prepare for your brethren, to do according to the word of Jehovah by the hand of Moses.'
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 7] And Josiah lifteth up to the sons of the people a flock of lambs and young goats, the whole for passover-offerings, for every one who is found, to the number of thirty thousand, and oxen three thousand: these [are] from the substance of the king.
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 8] And his heads, for a willing-offering to the people, to the priests, and to the Levites, have lifted up; Hilkiah, and Zechariah, and Jehiel, leaders in the house of God, to the priests have given for passover-offerings two thousand and six hundred, and oxen three hundred;
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 9] and Conaniah, and Shemaiah, and Nethaneel, his brethren, and Hashabiah, and Jeiel, and Jozabad, heads of the Levites, have lifted up to the Levites, for passover-offerings, five thousand, and oxen five hundred.
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 10] And the service is prepared, and the priests stand on their station, and the Levites on their courses, according to the command of the king,
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 11] and they slaughter the passover-offering, and the priests sprinkle out of their hand, and the Levites are striping;
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 12] and they turn aside the burnt-offering, to put them by the divisions of the house of the fathers of the sons of the people, to bring near to Jehovah, as it is written in the book of Moses — and so to the oxen.
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 13] And they cook the passover with fire, according to the ordinance, and the sanctified things they have cooked in pots, and in kettles, and in pans — for all the sons of the people.
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 14] And afterward they have prepared for themselves, and for the priests: for the priests, sons of Aaron, [are] in the offering up of the burnt-offering and of the fat till night; and the Levites have prepared for themselves, and for the priests, sons of Aaron.
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 15] And the singers, sons of Asaph, [are] on their station, according to the command of David, and Asaph, and Heman, and Jeduthun seer of the king, and the gate keepers [are] at gate and gate; it is not for them to turn aside from off their service, for their brethren the Levites have prepared for them.
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 16] And all the service of Jehovah is prepared on that day, to keep the passover, and to cause to ascend burnt-offering upon the altar of Jehovah, according to the command of king Josiah.
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 17] And the sons of Israel who are found make the passover at that time, and the feast of unleavened things, seven days.
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 18] And there hath not been made a passover like it in Israel from the days of Samuel the prophet, and none of the kings of Israel made such a passover as Josiah hath made, and the priests, and the Levites, and all Judah and Israel who are found, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem.
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 19] In the eighteenth year of the reign of Josiah hath this passover been made.
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 20] After all this, when Josiah hath prepared the house, come up hath Necho king of Egypt, to fight against Carchemish by Phrat, and Josiah goeth forth to meet him;
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 21] and he sendeth unto him messengers, saying, 'What — to me and to thee, O king of Judah? not against thee do I come to-day, but unto the house with which I have war, and God said to haste me; cease for thee from God who [is] with me, and He doth not destroy thee.'
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 22] And Josiah hath not turned round his face from him, but to fight against him hath disguised himself, and hath not hearkened unto the words of Necho, from the mouth of God, and cometh in to fight in the valley of Megiddo;
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 23] and the archers shoot at king Josiah, and the king saith to his servants, 'Remove me, for I have become very sick.'
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 24] And his servants remove him from the chariot, and cause him to ride on the second chariot that he hath, and cause him to go to Jerusalem, and he dieth, and is buried in the graves of his fathers, and all Judah and Jerusalem are mourning for Josiah,
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 25] and Jeremiah lamenteth for Josiah, and all the singers and the songstresses speak in their lamentations of Josiah unto this day, and set them for a statute on Israel, and lo, they are written beside the lamentations.
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 26] And the rest of the matters of Josiah, and his kind acts, according as it is written in the law of Jehovah,
[YLT | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 35 | Verse 27] even his matters, the first and the last, lo, they are written on the book of the kings of Israel and Judah.
