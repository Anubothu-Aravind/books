[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 1] The word that Jehovah hath spoken concerning Babylon, concerning the land of the Chaldeans, by the hand of Jeremiah the prophet:
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 2] 'Declare ye among nations, and sound, And lift up an ensign, sound, do not hide, Say ye: Captured hath been Babylon, Put to shame hath been Bel, Broken hath been Merodach, Put to shame have been her grievous things, Broken have been her idols.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 3] For come up against her hath a nation from the north, It maketh her land become a desolation, And there is not an inhabitant in it. From man even unto beast, They have moved, they have gone.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 4] In those days, and at that time, An affirmation of Jehovah, Come in do sons of Israel, They and sons of Judah together, Going on and weeping they go, And Jehovah their God they seek.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 5] [To] Zion they ask the way, Thitherward [are] their faces: Come in, and we are joined unto Jehovah, A covenant age-during — not forgotten.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 6] A perishing flock hath My people been, Their shepherds have caused them to err, [To] the mountains causing them to go back, From mountain unto hill they have gone, They have forgotten their crouching-place.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 7] All finding them have devoured them, And their adversaries have said: We are not guilty, Because that they sinned against Jehovah, The habitation of righteousness, And the hope of their fathers — Jehovah.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 8] Move ye from the midst of Babylon, And from the land of the Chaldeans go out. And be as he-goats before a flock.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 9] For, lo, I am stirring up, And am causing to come up against Babylon, An assembly of great nations from a land of the north, And they have set in array against her, From thence she is captured, Its arrow — as a skilful hero — returneth not empty,
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 10] And Chaldea hath been for a spoil, All her spoilers are satisfied, An affirmation of Jehovah.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 11] Because thou rejoicest, because thou exultest, O spoilers of Mine inheritance, Because thou increasest as a heifer [at] the tender grass, And dost cry aloud as bulls,
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 12] Ashamed hath been your mother greatly, Confounded hath she been that bare you, Lo, the hindermost of nations [is] a wilderness, A dry land, and a desert.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 13] Because of the wrath of Jehovah it is not inhabited, And it hath been a desolation — all of it. Every passer by at Babylon is astonished, And doth hiss because of all her plagues.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 14] Set yourselves in array against Babylon round about, All ye treading a bow, Shoot at her, have no pity on the arrow, For against Jehovah she hath sinned.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 15] Shout against her round about, She hath given forth her hand, Fallen have her foundations, Thrown down have been her walls, For it [is] the vengeance of Jehovah, Be avenged of her, as she did — do ye to her.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 16] Cut off the sower from Babylon, And him handling the sickle in the time of harvest, Because of the oppressing sword, Each unto his people — they turn, And each to his land — they flee.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 17] A scattered sheep is Israel, lions have driven away, At first, devour him did the king of Asshur, And now, at last, broken his bone Hath Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 18] Therefore thus said Jehovah of Hosts, God of Israel: Lo, I am seeing after the king of Babylon, And after his land, As I have seen after the king of Asshur;
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 19] And I have brought back Israel unto his habitation, And he hath fed on Carmel, and on Bashan. And in mount Ephraim, and on Gilead is his soul satisfied.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 20] In those days, and at that time, An affirmation of Jehovah, Sought is the iniquity of Israel, and it is not, And the sin of Judah, and it is not found, For I am propitious to those whom I leave!
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 21] Against the land of Merathaim: Go up against it, and unto the inhabitants of Pekod, Waste and devote their posterity, An affirmation of Jehovah, And do according to all that I have commanded thee.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 22] A noise of battle [is] in the land, and of great destruction.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 23] How hath it been cut and broken, The hammer of the whole earth! How hath Babylon been for a desolation among nations!
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 24] I have laid a snare for thee, And also — thou art captured, O Babylon, And thou — thou hast known, Thou hast been found, and also art caught, For against Jehovah thou hast stirred thyself up.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 25] Jehovah hath opened His treasury, And He bringeth out the weapons of His indignation, For a work [is] to the Lord Jehovah of Hosts, In the land of the Chaldeans.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 26] Come ye in to her from the extremity, Open ye her storehouses, Raise her up as heaps, and devote her, Let her have no remnant.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 27] Slay all her kine, they go down to slaughter, Woe [is] on them, for come hath their day, The time of their inspection.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 28] A voice of fugitives and escaped ones [Is] from the land of Babylon, To declare in Zion the vengeance of Jehovah our God, The vengeance of His temple.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 29] Summon unto Babylon archers, all treading the bow, Encamp against her round about, Let [her] have no escape; Recompense to her according to her work, According to all that she did — do to her, For unto Jehovah she hath been proud, Unto the Holy One of Israel.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 30] Therefore fall do her young men in her broad places, And all her men of war are cut off in that day, An affirmation of Jehovah.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 31] Lo, I [am] against thee, O pride, An affirmation of the Lord Jehovah of Hosts, For thy day hath come, the time of thy inspection.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 32] And stumbled hath pride, And he hath fallen, and hath no raiser up, And I have kindled a fire in his cities, And it hath devoured all round about him.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 33] Thus said Jehovah of Hosts: Oppressed are the sons of Israel, And the sons of Judah together, And all their captors have kept hold on them, They have refused to send them away.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 34] Their Redeemer [is] strong, Jehovah of Hosts [is] His name, He doth thoroughly plead their cause, So as to cause the land to rest, And He hath given trouble to the inhabitants of Babylon.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 35] A sword [is] for the Chaldeans, An affirmation of Jehovah, And it [is] on the inhabitants of Babylon, And on her heads, and on her wise men;
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 36] A sword [is] on the princes, And they have become foolish; A sword [is] on her mighty ones, And they have been broken down;
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 37] A sword [is] on his horses and on his chariot, And on all the rabble who [are] in her midst, And they have become women; A sword [is] on her treasuries, And they have been spoiled;
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 38] A sword [is] on her waters, and they have been dried up, For it [is] a land of graven images, And in idols they do boast themselves.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 39] Therefore dwell do Ziim with Iim, Yea, dwelt in her have daughters of the ostrich, And it is not inhabited any more for ever, Nor dwelt in unto all generations.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 40] As overthrown by God with Sodom, And with Gomorrah, and with its neighbours, An affirmation of Jehovah, none doth dwell there, Nor sojourn in her doth a son of man.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 41] Lo, a people hath come from the north, Even a great nation, And many kings are stirred up from the sides of the earth.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 42] Bow and halbert they seize, Cruel [are] they, and they have no mercy, Their voice as a sea soundeth, and on horses they ride, Set in array as a man for battle, Against thee, O daughter of Babylon.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 43] Heard hath the king of Babylon their report, And feeble have been his hands, Distress hath seized him; pain as a travailing woman.
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 44] Lo, as a lion he cometh up, Because of the rising of the Jordan, Unto the enduring habitation, But I cause to rest, I cause them to run from off her. And who is chosen? on her I lay a charge, For who [is] like Me? And who doth convene Me? And who [is] this shepherd who standeth before Me?
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 45] Therefore, hear ye the counsel of Jehovah, That He counselled concerning Babylon, And His devices that He hath devised Concerning the land of the Chaldeans; Drag them out do not little ones of the flock, Doth He not make desolate over them the habitation?
[YLT | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 50 | Verse 46] From the voice: Captured was Babylon, Hath the earth been shaken, And a cry among nations hath been heard!
