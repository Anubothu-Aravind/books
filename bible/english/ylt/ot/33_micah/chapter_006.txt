[YLT | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 1] Hear, I pray you, that which Jehovah is saying: 'Rise — strive thou with the mountains, And cause thou the hills to hear thy voice.'
[YLT | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 2] Hear, O mountains, the strife of Jehovah, Ye strong ones — foundations of earth! For a strife [is] to Jehovah, with His people, And with Israel He doth reason.
[YLT | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 3] O My people, what have I done to thee? And what — have I wearied thee? Testify against Me.
[YLT | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 4] For I brought thee up from the land of Egypt, And from the house of servants I have ransomed thee, And I send before thee Moses, Aaron, and Miriam.
[YLT | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 5] O My people, remember, I pray you, What counsel did Balak king of Moab, What answer him did Balaam son of Beor, (From Shittim unto Gilgal,) In order to know the righteous acts of Jehovah.'
[YLT | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 6] With what do I come before Jehovah? Do I bow to God Most High? Do I come before Him with burnt-offerings? With calves — sons of a year?
[YLT | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 7] Is Jehovah pleased with thousands of rams? With myriads of streams of oil? Do I give my first-born [for] my transgression? The fruit of my body [for] the sin of my soul?
[YLT | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 8] He hath declared to thee, O man, what [is] good; Yea, what is Jehovah requiring of thee, Except — to do judgment, and love kindness, And lowly to walk with thy God?
[YLT | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 9] A voice of Jehovah to the city calleth, And wisdom doth fear Thy name, Hear ye the rod, and Him who appointed it.
[YLT | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 10] Are there yet [in] the house of the wicked Treasures of wickedness, And the abhorred scanty ephah?
[YLT | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 11] Do I reckon [it] pure with balances of wickedness? And with a bag of deceitful stones?
[YLT | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 12] Whose rich ones have been full of violence, And its inhabitants have spoken falsehood, And their tongue [is] deceitful in their mouth.
[YLT | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 13] And I also, I have begun to smite thee, To make desolate, because of thy sins.
[YLT | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 14] Thou — thou eatest, and thou art not satisfied, And thy pit [is] in thy midst, And thou removest, and dost not deliver, And that which thou deliverest, to a sword I give.
[YLT | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 15] Thou — thou sowest, and thou dost not reap, Thou — thou treadest the olive, And thou pourest not out oil, And new wine — and thou drinkest not wine.
[YLT | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 16] And kept habitually are the statutes of Omri, And all the work of the house of Ahab, And ye do walk in their counsels, For My giving thee for a desolation, And its inhabitants for a hissing, And the reproach of My people ye do bear!
