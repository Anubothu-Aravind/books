[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 1] After this hath Job opened his mouth, and revileth his day.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 2] And Job answereth and saith: —
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 3] Let the day perish in which I am born, And the night that hath said: 'A man-child hath been conceived.'
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 4] That day — let it be darkness, Let not God require it from above, Nor let light shine upon it.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 5] Let darkness and death-shade redeem it, Let a cloud tabernacle upon it, Let them terrify it as the most bitter of days.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 6] That night — let thick darkness take it, Let it not be united to days of the year, Into the number of months let it not come.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 7] Lo! that night — let it be gloomy, Let no singing come into it.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 8] Let the cursers of day mark it, Who are ready to wake up Leviathan.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 9] Let the stars of its twilight be dark, Let it wait for light, and there is none, And let it not look on the eyelids of the dawn.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 10] Because it hath not shut the doors Of the womb that was mine! And hide misery from mine eyes.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 11] Why from the womb do I not die? From the belly I have come forth and gasp!
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 12] Wherefore have knees been before me? And what [are] breasts, that I suck?
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 13] For now, I have lain down, and am quiet, I have slept — then there is rest to me,
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 14] With kings and counsellors of earth, These building wastes for themselves.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 15] Or with princes — they have gold, They are filling their houses [with] silver.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 16] (Or as a hidden abortion I am not, As infants — they have not seen light.)
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 17] There the wicked have ceased troubling, And there rest do the wearied in power.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 18] Together prisoners have been at ease, They have not heard the voice of an exactor,
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 19] Small and great [are] there the same. And a servant [is] free from his lord.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 20] Why giveth He to the miserable light, and life to the bitter soul?
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 21] Who are waiting for death, and it is not, And they seek it above hid treasures.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 22] Who are glad — unto joy, They rejoice when they find a grave.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 23] To a man whose way hath been hidden, And whom God doth shut up?
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 24] For before my food, my sighing cometh, And poured out as waters [are] my roarings.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 25] For a fear I feared and it meeteth me, And what I was afraid of doth come to me.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 3 | Verse 26] I was not safe — nor was I quiet — Nor was I at rest — and trouble cometh!
