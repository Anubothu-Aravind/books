[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 14 | Verse 2] As a flower he hath gone forth, and is cut off, And he fleeth as a shadow and standeth not.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 14 | Verse 3] Also — on this Thou hast opened Thine eyes, And dost bring me into judgment with Thee.

[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 14 | Verse 8] If its root becometh old in the earth, And its stem doth die in the dust,
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 14 | Verse 9] From the fragrance of water it doth flourish, And hath made a crop as a plant.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 14 | Verse 10] And a man dieth, and becometh weak, And man expireth, and where [is] he?
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 14 | Verse 11] Waters have gone away from a sea, And a river becometh waste and dry.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 14 | Verse 12] And man hath lain down, and riseth not, Till the wearing out of the heavens they awake not, Nor are roused from their sleep.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 14 | Verse 13] O that in Sheol Thou wouldest conceal me, Hide me till the turning of Thine anger, Set for me a limit, and remember me.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 14 | Verse 14] If a man dieth — doth he revive? All days of my warfare I wait, till my change come.

[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 14 | Verse 16] But now, my steps Thou numberest, Thou dost not watch over my sin.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 14 | Verse 17] Sealed up in a bag [is] my transgression, And Thou sewest up mine iniquity.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 14 | Verse 18] And yet, a falling mountain wasteth away, And a rock is removed from its place.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 14 | Verse 19] Stones have waters worn away, Their outpourings wash away the dust of earth, And the hope of man Thou hast destroyed.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 14 | Verse 20] Thou prevailest [over] him for ever, and he goeth, He is changing his countenance, And Thou sendest him away.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 14 | Verse 21] Honoured are his sons, and he knoweth not; And they are little, and he attendeth not to them.
[YLT | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 14 | Verse 22] Only — his flesh for him is pained, And his soul for him doth mourn.'
