[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 137 | Verse 1] By rivers of Babylon — There we did sit, Yea, we wept when we remembered Zion.

[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 137 | Verse 3] For there our captors asked us the words of a song, And our spoilers — joy: 'Sing ye to us of a song of Zion.'
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 137 | Verse 4] How do we sing the song of Jehovah, On the land of a stranger?

[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 137 | Verse 6] My tongue doth cleave to my palate, If I do not remember thee, If I do not exalt Jerusalem above my chief joy.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 137 | Verse 7] Remember, Jehovah, for the sons of Edom, The day of Jerusalem, Those saying, 'Rase, rase to its foundation!'
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 137 | Verse 8] O daughter of Babylon, O destroyed one, O the happiness of him who repayeth to thee thy deed, That thou hast done to us.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 137 | Verse 9] O the happiness of him who doth seize, And hath dashed thy sucklings on the rock!
