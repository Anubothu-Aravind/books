[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 8 | Verse 2] From the mouths of infants and sucklings Thou hast founded strength, Because of Thine adversaries, To still an enemy and a self-avenger.

[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 8 | Verse 5] And causest him to lack a little of Godhead, And with honour and majesty compassest him.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 8 | Verse 6] Thou dost cause him to rule Over the works of Thy hands, All Thou hast placed under his feet.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 8 | Verse 7] Sheep and oxen, all of them, And also beasts of the field,
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 8 | Verse 8] Bird of the heavens, and fish of the sea, Passing through the paths of the seas!
