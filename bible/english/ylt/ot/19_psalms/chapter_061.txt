[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 61 | Verse 1] To the Overseer, on stringed instruments. — By David. Hear, O God, my loud cry, attend to my prayer.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 61 | Verse 2] From the end of the land unto Thee I call, In the feebleness of my heart, Into a rock higher than I Thou dost lead me.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 61 | Verse 3] For Thou hast been a refuge for me, A tower of strength because of the enemy.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 61 | Verse 4] I sojourn in Thy tent to the ages, I trust in the secret place of Thy wings. Selah.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 61 | Verse 5] For Thou, O God, hast hearkened to my vows, Thou hast appointed the inheritance Of those fearing Thy name.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 61 | Verse 6] Days to the days of the king Thou addest, His years as generation and generation.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 61 | Verse 7] He dwelleth to the age before God, Kindness and truth appoint — they keep him.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 61 | Verse 8] So do I praise Thy name for ever, When I pay my vows day by day!
