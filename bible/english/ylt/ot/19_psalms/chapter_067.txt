[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 67 | Verse 1] To the Overseer, with stringed instruments. — A Psalm, a Song. God doth favour us and bless us, Doth cause His face to shine with us. Selah.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 67 | Verse 2] For the knowledge in earth of Thy way, among all nations of Thy salvation.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 67 | Verse 3] Praise Thee do peoples, O God, Praise Thee do peoples, all of them.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 67 | Verse 4] Rejoice and sing do nations, For Thou judgest peoples uprightly, And peoples on earth comfortest. Selah.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 67 | Verse 5] Confess Thee do peoples, O God, Confess Thee do peoples — all of them.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 67 | Verse 6] Earth hath given her increase, God doth bless us — our God,
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 67 | Verse 7] God doth bless us, and all ends of earth fear Him!
