[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 12 | Verse 1] To the Overseer, on the octave. — A Psalm of David. Save, Jehovah, for the saintly hath failed, For the stedfast have ceased From the sons of men:
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 12 | Verse 2] Vanity they speak each with his neighbour, Lip of flattery! With heart and heart they speak.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 12 | Verse 3] Jehovah doth cut off all lips of flattery, A tongue speaking great things,
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 12 | Verse 4] Who said, 'By our tongue we do mightily: Our lips [are] our own; who [is] lord over us?'
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 12 | Verse 5] Because of the spoiling of the poor, Because of the groaning of the needy, Now do I arise, saith Jehovah, I set in safety [him who] doth breathe for it.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 12 | Verse 6] Sayings of Jehovah [are] pure sayings; Silver tried in a furnace of earth refined sevenfold.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 12 | Verse 7] Thou, O Jehovah, dost preserve them, Thou keepest us from this generation to the age.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 12 | Verse 8] Around the wicked walk continually, According as vileness is exalted by sons of men!
