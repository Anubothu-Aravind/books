[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 30 | Verse 1] A Psalm. — A song of the dedication of the house of David. I exalt Thee, O Jehovah, For Thou hast drawn me up, and hast not let mine enemies rejoice over me.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 30 | Verse 2] Jehovah my God, I have cried to Thee, And Thou dost heal me.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 30 | Verse 3] Jehovah, Thou hast brought up from Sheol my soul, Thou hast kept me alive, From going down [to] the pit.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 30 | Verse 4] Sing praise to Jehovah, ye His saints, And give thanks at the remembrance of His holiness,
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 30 | Verse 5] For — a moment [is] in His anger, Life [is] in His good-will, At even remaineth weeping, and at morn singing.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 30 | Verse 6] And I — I have said in mine ease, 'I am not moved — to the age.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 30 | Verse 7] O Jehovah, in Thy good pleasure, Thou hast caused strength to remain for my mountain,' Thou hast hidden Thy face — I have been troubled.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 30 | Verse 8] Unto Thee, O Jehovah, I call, And unto Jehovah I make supplication.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 30 | Verse 9] 'What gain [is] in my blood? In my going down unto corruption? Doth dust thank Thee? doth it declare Thy truth?
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 30 | Verse 10] Hear, O Jehovah, and favour me, O Jehovah, be a helper to me.'
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 30 | Verse 11] Thou hast turned my mourning to dancing for me, Thou hast loosed my sackcloth, And girdest me [with] joy.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 30 | Verse 12] So that honour doth praise Thee, and is not silent, O Jehovah, my God, to the age I thank Thee!
