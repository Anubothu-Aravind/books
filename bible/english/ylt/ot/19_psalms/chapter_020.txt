[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 20 | Verse 1] To the Overseer. — A Psalm of David. Jehovah doth answer thee, In a day of adversity, The name of the God of Jacob doth set thee on high,
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 20 | Verse 2] He doth send thy help from the sanctuary, And from Zion doth support thee,
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 20 | Verse 3] He doth remember all thy presents, And thy burnt-offering doth reduce to ashes. Selah.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 20 | Verse 4] He doth give to thee according to thy heart, And all thy counsel doth fulfil.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 20 | Verse 5] We sing of thy salvation, And in the name of our God set up a banner. Jehovah doth fulfil all thy requests.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 20 | Verse 6] Now I have known That Jehovah hath saved His anointed, He answereth him from His holy heavens, With the saving might of His right hand.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 20 | Verse 7] Some of chariots, and some of horses, And we of the name of Jehovah our God Make mention.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 20 | Verse 8] They — they have bowed and have fallen, And we have risen and station ourselves upright.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 20 | Verse 9] O Jehovah, save the king, He doth answer us in the day we call!
