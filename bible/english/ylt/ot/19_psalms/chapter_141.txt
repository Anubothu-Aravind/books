[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 141 | Verse 1] A Psalm, by David. O Jehovah, I have called Thee, haste to me, Give ear [to] my voice when I call to Thee.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 141 | Verse 2] My prayer is prepared — incense before Thee, The lifting up of my hands — the evening present.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 141 | Verse 3] Set, O Jehovah, a watch for my mouth, Watch Thou over the door of my lips.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 141 | Verse 4] Incline not my heart to an evil thing, To do habitually actions in wickedness, With men working iniquity, Yea, I eat not of their pleasant things.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 141 | Verse 5] The righteous doth beat me [in] kindness. And doth reprove me, Oil of the head my head disalloweth not, For still my prayer [is] about their vexations.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 141 | Verse 6] Their judges have been released by the sides of a rock, And they have heard my sayings, For they have been pleasant.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 141 | Verse 7] As one tilling and ripping up in the land, Have our bones been scattered at the command of Saul.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 141 | Verse 8] But to Thee, O Jehovah, my Lord, [are] mine eyes, In Thee I have trusted, Make not bare my soul.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 141 | Verse 9] Keep me from the gin they laid for me, Even snares of workers of iniquity.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 141 | Verse 10] The wicked fall in their nets together, till I pass over!
