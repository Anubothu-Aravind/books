[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 112 | Verse 1] Praise ye Jah! O the happiness of one fearing Jehovah, In His commands he hath delighted greatly.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 112 | Verse 2] Mighty in the earth is his seed, The generation of the upright is blessed.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 112 | Verse 3] Wealth and riches [are] in his house, And his righteousness is standing for ever.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 112 | Verse 4] Light hath risen in darkness to the upright, Gracious, and merciful, and righteous.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 112 | Verse 5] Good [is] the man — gracious and lending, He sustaineth his matters in judgment.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 112 | Verse 6] For — to the age he is not moved; For a memorial age-during is the righteous.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 112 | Verse 7] Of an evil report he is not afraid, Prepared is His heart, confident in Jehovah.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 112 | Verse 8] Sustained is his heart — he feareth not, Till that he look on his adversaries.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 112 | Verse 9] He hath scattered — hath given to the needy, His righteousness is standing for ever, His horn is exalted with honour.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 112 | Verse 10] The wicked seeth, and hath been angry, His teeth he gnasheth, and hath melted, The desire of the wicked doth perish!
