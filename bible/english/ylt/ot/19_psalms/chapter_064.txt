[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 1] To the Overseer. — A Psalm of David. Hear, O God, my voice, in my meditation, From the fear of an enemy Thou keepest my life,
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 2] Hidest me from the secret counsel of evil doers, From the tumult of workers of iniquity.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 3] Who sharpened as a sword their tongue, They directed their arrow — a bitter word.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 4] To shoot in secret places the perfect, Suddenly they shoot him, and fear not.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 5] They strengthen for themselves an evil thing, They recount of the hiding of snares, They have said, 'Who doth look at it?'
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 6] They search out perverse things, 'We perfected a searching search,' And the inward part of man, and the heart [are] deep.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 7] And God doth shoot them [with] an arrow, Sudden have been their wounds,
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 8] And they cause him to stumble, Against them [is] their own tongue, Every looker on them fleeth away.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 9] And all men fear, and declare the work of God, And His deed they have considered wisely.
[YLT | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 10] The righteous doth rejoice in Jehovah, And hath trusted in Him, And boast themselves do all the upright of heart!
