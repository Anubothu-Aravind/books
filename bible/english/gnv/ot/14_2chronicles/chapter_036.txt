[GNV | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 36 | Verse 23] Thus saith Cyrus King of Persia, All the kingdomes of the earth hath the Lord God of heauen giuen me, and hath commanded me to build him an house in Ierusalem, that is in Iudah.
