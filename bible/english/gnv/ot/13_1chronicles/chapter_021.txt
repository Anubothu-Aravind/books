[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 21 | Verse 13] And Dauid said vnto Gad, I am in a wonderfull strait. let me nowe fall into the hande of the Lord: for his mercies are exceeding great, and let me not fall into the hande of man.

[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 21 | Verse 16] And Dauid lift vp his eyes, and sawe the Angel of the Lord stande betweene the earth and the heauen with his sworde drawen in his hand, and stretched out towarde Ierusalem. [GNV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 21 | Verse 17] And Dauid said vnto God, Is it not I that commanded to nomber the people? O Lord my God, I beseech thee, let thine hande be on me and on my fathers house, and not on thy people for their destruction.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 21 | Verse 18] Then the Angel of the Lord commanded Gad to say to Dauid, that Dauid should goe vp, and set vp an altar vnto the Lord in the thresshing floore of Ornan the Iebusite.

[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 21 | Verse 24] And King Dauid saide to Ornan, Not so: but I will bye it for sufficient money: for I wil not take that which is thine for the Lord, nor offer burnt offrings without cost.
