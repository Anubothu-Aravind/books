[GNV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 46 | Verse 8] Remember this, and be ashamed: bring it againe to minde, O you transgressers.

[GNV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 46 | Verse 12] Heare me, ye stubburne hearted, that are farre from iustice.
