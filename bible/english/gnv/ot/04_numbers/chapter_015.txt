[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 15 | Verse 7] And for a drinke-offering, thou shalt offer the third part of an Hin of wine, for a sweete sauour vnto the Lord.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 15 | Verse 8] And when thou preparest a bullocke for a burnt offring, or for a sacrifice to fulfill a vowe or a peace offring to the Lord,

[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 15 | Verse 11] Thus shall it be done for a bullocke, or for a ram, or for a lambe, or for a kid.

[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 15 | Verse 14] And if a stranger soiourne with you, or whosoeuer bee among you in your generations, and will make an offring by fire of a sweete sauour vnto the Lord, as ye do, so hee shall doe.

[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 15 | Verse 16] One Lawe and one maner shall serue both for you and for the stranger that soiourneth with you.

[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 15 | Verse 22] And if ye haue erred, and not obserued all these commandements, which the Lord hath spoken vnto Moses,

[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 15 | Verse 24] And if so be that ought be committed ignorantly of the Congregation, then all ye Congregatio shall giue a bullocke for a burnt offring, for a sweete sauour vnto the Lord, with the meat offring and drinke offring thereto, according to the maner, and an hee goate for a sinne offring.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 15 | Verse 25] And the Priest shall make an atonement for al the Congregation of the children of Israel, and it shalbe forgiuen them: for it is ignorance: and they shall bring their offring for an offring made by fire vnto the Lord, and their sinne offering before the Lord for their ignorance.
