[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 1] When King Arad the Canaanite, which dwelt toward the South, heard tel that Israel came by the way of the spies, then fought hee against Israel, and tooke of them prysoners.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 2] So Israel vowed a vowe vnto the Lord, and said, If thou wilt deliuer and giue this people into mine hand, then I wil vtterly destroy their cities.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 3] And the Lord heard the voyce of Israel, and deliuered them the Canaanites: and they vtterly destroied them and their cities, and called ye name of the place Hormah.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 4] After, they departed from the mount Hor by the way of the red Sea, to compasse the land of Edom: and the people were sore grieued because of the way.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 5] And the people spake against God and against Moses, saying, Wherefore haue ye brought vs out of Egypt, to die in the wildernesse? for here is neither bread nor water, and our soule lotheth this light bread.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 6] Wherefore the Lord sent fierie serpents among ye people, which stung the people: so that many of the people of Israel died.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 7] Therefore the people came to Moses and said, We haue sinned: for wee haue spoken against the Lord, and against thee: pray to the Lord, that he take away the serpents from vs: and Moses prayed for the people.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 8] And the Lord said vnto Moses, Make thee a fiery serpent, and set it vp for a signe, that as many as are bitten, may looke vpon it, and liue.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 9] So Moses made a serpent of brasse, and set it vp for a signe: and when a serpent had bitten a man, then he looked to the serpent of brasse, and liued.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 10] And ye children of Israel departed thence, and pitched in Oboth.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 11] And they departed from Oboth, and pitched in lie-abarim, in the wildernesse, which is before Moab on the Eastside.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 12] They remoued thence, and pitched vpon the riuer of Zared.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 13] Thence they departed, and pitched on the other side of Arnon, which is in the wildernesse, and commeth out of the coasts of the Amorites: (for Arnon is the border of Moab, betweene the Moabites and the Amorites)
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 14] Wherefore it shall be spoken in the booke of the battels of the Lord, what thing he did in the red sea, and in the riuers of Arnon,
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 15] And at the streame of the riuers that goeth downe to the dwelling of Ar, and lieth vpon the border of Moab.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 16] And from thence they turned to Beer: the same is the well where the Lord said vnto Moses, Assemble the people, and I wil giue them water.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 17] Then Israel sang this song, Rise vp well, sing ye vnto it.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 18] The princes digged this well, the captaines of the people digged it, euen the lawe giuer, with their staues. And from the wildernesse they came to Mattanah,
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 19] And from Mattanah to Nahaliel, and from Nahaliel to Bamoth,
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 20] And from Bamoth in the valley, that is in the plaine of Moab, to the top of Pisgah that looketh toward Ieshimon.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 21] Then Israel sent messengers vnto Sihon, King of the Amorites, saying,
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 22] Let me goe through thy land: we wil not turne aside into the fieldes, nor into the vineyardes, neither drinke of the waters of ye welles: we will goe by the kings way, vntill we be past thy countrey.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 23] But Sihon gaue Israel no licence to passe through his countrey, but Sihon assembled all his people, and went out against Israel into the wildernesse: and he came to Iahoz, and fought against Israel.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 24] But Israel smote him with the edge of the sword, and conquered his land, from Arnon vnto Iabok, euen vnto ye children of Ammon: for the border of the children of Ammon was strong.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 25] And Israel tooke al these cities, and dwelt in all the cities of the Amorites in Heshbon and in all the villages thereof.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 26] For Heshbon was the citie of Sihon the king of the Amorites, which had fought beforetime against the king of the Moabites, and had taken al his land out of his hand, euen vnto Arnon.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 27] Wherefore they that speake in prouerbes, say, Come to Heshbon, let the citie of Sihon bee built and repaired:
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 28] For a fire is gone out of Heshbon, and a flame from the citie of Sihon, and hath consumed Ar of the Moabites, and the lords of Bamoth in Arnon.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 29] Wo be to thee, Moab: O people of Chemosh, thou art vndone: he hath suffered his sonnes to be pursued, and his daughters to be in captiuitie to Sihon the king of the Amorites.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 30] Their empire also is lost from Heshbon vnto Dibon, and wee haue destroyed them vnto Nophah, which reacheth vnto Medeba.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 31] Thus Israel dwelt in the lande of the Amorites.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 32] And Moses sent to searche out Iaazer, and they tooke the townes belonging thereto, and rooted out the Amorites that were there.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 33] And they turned and went vp toward Bashan: and Og the King of Bashan came out against them, hee, and all his people, to fight at Edrei.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 34] Then the Lord said vnto Moses, Feare him not: for I haue deliuered him into thine hand and all his people, and his land: and thou shalt do to him as thou diddest vnto Sihon the king of the Amorites, which dwelt at Heshbon.
[GNV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 21 | Verse 35] They smote him therefore, and his sonnes, and all his people, vntill there was none left him: so they conquered his land.
