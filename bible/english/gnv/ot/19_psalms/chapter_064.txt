[GNV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 1] To him that excelleth. A Psalme of David. Heare my voyce, O God, in my prayer: preserue my life from feare of the enemie.
[GNV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 2] Hide me from the conspiracie of the wicked, and from the rage of the workers of iniquitie.
[GNV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 3] Which haue whette their tongue like a sword, and shot for their arrowes bitter wordes.
[GNV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 4] To shoote at the vpright in secrete: they shoote at him suddenly, and feare not.
[GNV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 5] They encourage themselues in a wicked purpose: they commune together to lay snares priuilie, and say, Who shall see them?
[GNV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 6] They haue sought out iniquities, and haue accomplished that which they sought out, euen euery one his secret thoughtes, and the depth of his heart.
[GNV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 7] But God will shoote an arrowe at them suddenly: their strokes shalbe at once.
[GNV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 8] They shall cause their owne tongue to fall vpon them: and whosoeuer shall see them, shall flee away.
[GNV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 9] And all men shall see it, and declare the worke of God, and they shall vnderstand, what he hath wrought.
[GNV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 10] But the righteous shall be glad in the Lord, and trust in him: and all that are vpright of heart, shall reioyce.
