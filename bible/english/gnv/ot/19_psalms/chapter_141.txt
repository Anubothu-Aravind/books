[GNV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 141 | Verse 1] A Psalme of David. O Lord, I call vpon thee: haste thee vnto me: heare my voyce, when I cry vnto thee.
[GNV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 141 | Verse 2] Let my prayer be directed in thy sight as incense, and the lifting vp of mine hands as an euening sacrifice.
[GNV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 141 | Verse 3] Set a watch, O Lord, before my mouth, and keepe the doore of my lips.
[GNV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 141 | Verse 4] Incline not mine heart to euill, that I should commit wicked workes with men that worke iniquitie: and let me not eate of their delicates.
[GNV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 141 | Verse 5] Let the righteous smite me: for that is a benefite: and let him reprooue me, and it shalbe a precious oyle, that shall not breake mine head: for within a while I shall euen pray in their miseries.
[GNV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 141 | Verse 6] When their iudges shall be cast downe in stonie places, they shall heare my wordes, for they are sweete.
[GNV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 141 | Verse 7] Our bones lye scattered at the graues mouth, as he that heweth wood or diggeth in the earth.
[GNV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 141 | Verse 8] But mine eyes looke vnto thee, O Lord God: in thee is my trust: leaue not my soule destitute.
[GNV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 141 | Verse 9] Keepe me from the snare, which they haue layde for me, and from the grennes of the workers of iniquitie.
[GNV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 141 | Verse 10] Let the wicked fall into his nettes together, whiles I escape.
