[GNV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 138 | Verse 1] A Psalme of David. I will praise thee with my whole heart: euen before the gods will I praise thee.
[GNV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 138 | Verse 2] I will worship toward thine holy Temple and praise thy Name, because of thy louing kindenesse and for thy trueth: for thou hast magnified thy Name aboue all things by thy word.

[GNV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 138 | Verse 7] Though I walke in the middes of trouble, yet wilt thou reuiue me: thou wilt stretch foorth thine hand vpon the wrath of mine enemies, and thy right hand shall saue me.
