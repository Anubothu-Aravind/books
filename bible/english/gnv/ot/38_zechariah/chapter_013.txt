[GNV | EN | OT | Zechariah | Chapter 13 | Verse 3] And when any shall yet prophesie, his father and his mother that begate him, shall say vnto him, Thou shalt not liue: for thou speakest lies in the name of the Lord: and his father and his mother that begat him, shall thrust him through, when he prophesieth.
[GNV | EN | OT | Zechariah | Chapter 13 | Verse 4] And in that day shall the prophetes be ashamed euery one of his vision, when he hath prophesied: neither shall they weare a rough garment to deceiue.
[GNV | EN | OT | Zechariah | Chapter 13 | Verse 5] But he shall say, I am no Prophet: I am an husbandman: for man taught me to be an heardman from my youth vp.
[GNV | EN | OT | Zechariah | Chapter 13 | Verse 6] And one shall say vnto him, What are these woundes in thine hands? Then he shall answere, Thus was I wounded in the house of my friendes.
[GNV | EN | OT | Zechariah | Chapter 13 | Verse 7] Arise, O sword, vpon my shepheard, and vpon the man, that is my fellow, sayth the Lord of hostes: smite the shepheard, and the sheepe shall be scattered: and I will turne mine hand vpon the litle ones.
[GNV | EN | OT | Zechariah | Chapter 13 | Verse 8] And in all the land, sayeth the Lord, two partes therein shall be cut off, and die: but the third shall be left therein.
[GNV | EN | OT | Zechariah | Chapter 13 | Verse 9] And I will bring that third part thorowe the fire, and will fine them as the siluer is fined, and will trye them as golde is tryed: they shall call on my Name, and I will heare them: I will say, It is my people, and they shall say, The Lord is my God.
