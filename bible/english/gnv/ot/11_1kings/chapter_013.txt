[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 1] And beholde, there came a man of God out of Iudah (by the commandement of the Lord) vnto Beth-el, and Ieroboam stoode by the altar to offer incense.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 2] And he cryed against the altar by the comandement of the Lord, and said, O altar, altar, thus saith the Lord, Beholde, a child shalbe borne vnto the house of Dauid, Iosiah by name, and vpon thee shall he sacrifice the Priestes of the hie places that burne incense vpon thee, and they shall burne mens bones vpon thee.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 3] And he gaue a signe the same time, saying, This is the signe, that the Lord hath spoken, Behold, the altar shall rent, and the ashes that are vpon it, shall fall out.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 4] And when the King had heard the saying of the man of God, which he had cryed against the altar in Beth-el, Ieroboam stretched out his hand from the altar, saying, Lay holde on him: but his hande which he put foorth against him, dryed vp, and he could not pull it in againe to him.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 5] The altar also claue asunder, and the ashes fell out from the altar, according to the signe, which the man of God had giuen by the commandement of the Lord.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 6] Then the King answered, and saide vnto the man of God, I beseeche thee, pray vnto ye Lord thy God, and make intercession for me, that mine hand may bee restored vnto me. And the man of God besought the Lord, and the Kings hand was restored, and became as it was afore.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 7] Then the King sayde vnto the man of God, Come home with mee, that thou mayest dyne, and I will giue thee a reward.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 8] But the man of God saide vnto the King, If thou wouldest giue me halfe thine house, I would not goe in with thee, neither woulde I eate bread nor drinke water in this place.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 9] For so was it charged mee by the worde of the Lord, saying, Eate no bread nor drinke water, nor turne againe by the same way that thou camest.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 10] So he went another way and returned not by the way that he came to Beth-el.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 11] And an olde Prophet dwelt in Beth-el, and his sonnes came and tolde him all ye woorkes, that the man of God had done that day in Beth-el, and the wordes which he had spoken vnto the King, tolde they their father.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 12] And their father sayde vnto them, What way went he? and his sonnes shewed him what waye the man of God went, which came from Iudah.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 13] And hee saide vnto his sonnes, Saddle mee the asse. Who sadled him the asse, and hee rode thereon,
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 14] And went after the man of God, and found him sitting vnder an oke: and he saide vnto him, Art thou the man of God that camest from Iudah? And he sayd, Yea.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 15] Then he said vnto him, Come home with me, and eate bread.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 16] But he answered, I may not returne with thee, nor go in with thee, neither wil I eate bread nor drinke water with thee in this place.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 17] For it was charged me by the word of the Lord, saying, Thou shalt eate no bread, nor drinke water there, nor turne againe to goe by the way that thou wentest.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 18] And he said vnto him, I am a Prophet also as thou art, and an Angel spake vnto me by the worde of the Lord, saying, Bring him againe with thee into thine house, that hee may eate bread and drinke water: but he lyed vnto him.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 19] So he went againe with him, and did eate bread in his house, and dranke water.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 20] And as they sate at the table, the worde of the Lord came vnto the Prophet, that brought him againe.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 21] And hee cried vnto the man of God that came from Iudah, saying, Thus sayeth the Lord, Because thou hast disobeyed the mouth of the Lord, and hast not kept the commandement which the Lord thy God commanded thee,
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 22] But camest backe againe, and hast eaten bread and drunke water in the place (whereof he did say vnto thee, Thou shalt eate no bread nor drinke any water) thy carkeis shall not come vnto the sepulchre of thy fathers.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 23] And when he had eaten bread and drunke, he sadled him the asse, to wit, to the Prophet whome he had brought againe.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 24] And when hee was gone, a lion met him by the way, and slewe him, and his body was cast in the way, and the asse stoode thereby: the lion stood by the corps also.

[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 26] And when the Prophet that brought him backe againe from the waye, hearde thereof, hee sayde, It is the man of God, who hath bene disobedient vnto the commandement of the Lord: therefore the Lord hath deliuered him vnto the lion, which hath rent him and slayne him, according to the worde of the Lord, which hee spake vnto him.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 27] And he spake to his sonnes, saying, Saddle me the asse. And they sadled him.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 28] And he went and founde his body cast in the way, and the asse and the lion stoode by the corps: and the lion had not eaten the bodie, nor torne the asse.
[GNV | EN | OT | 1 Kings | Chapter 13 | Verse 29] And the Prophet tooke vp the body of the man of God, and layed it vpon the asse, and brought it againe, and the olde Prophet came to the citie, to lament and burie him.
