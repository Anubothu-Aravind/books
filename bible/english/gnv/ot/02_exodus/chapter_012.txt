[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 1] Then the Lord spake to Moses and to Aaron in the land of Egypt, saying,
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 2] This moneth shalbe vnto you the beginning of moneths: it shalbe to you the first moneth of the yere.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 3] Speake ye vnto all the congregation of Israel, saying, In the tenth of this moneth let euery man take vnto him a lambe, according to the house of the fathers, a lambe for an house.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 4] And if the housholde be too litle for the lambe, he shall take his neighbour, which is next vnto his house, according to the nomber of the persons: euery one of you, according to his eating shall make your count for the lambes,
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 5] Your lambe shalbe without blemish, a male of a yeere olde: ye shall take it of the lambes, or of the kiddes.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 6] And yee shall keepe it vntill the fourteenth day of this moneth: then al the multitude of the Congregation of Israel shall kill it at euen.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 7] After, they shall take of the blood, and strike it on the two postes, and on the vpper doore post of the houses where they shall eate it.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 8] And they shall eate the flesh the same night, roste with fire, and vnleauened bread: with sowre herbes they shall eate it.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 9] Eate not thereof rawe, boyled nor sodden in water, but rost with fire, both his head, his feete, and his purtenance.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 10] And ye shall reserue nothing of it vnto the morning: but that, which remaineth of it vnto the morowe, shall ye burne with fire.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 11] And thus shall yee eate it, Your loynes girded, your shoes on your feete, and your staues in your handes, and yee shall eate it in haste: for it is the Lords Passeouer.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 12] For I will passe through the lande of Egypt the same night, and will smite all the first borne in the land of Egypt, both man and beast, and I will execute iudgement vpon all the gods of Egypt. I am the Lord.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 13] And the blood shalbe a token for you vpon the houses where ye are: so when I see the blood, I will passe ouer you, and the plague shall not be vpon you to destruction, when I smite the lande of Egypt.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 14] And this day shalbe vnto you a remembrance: and ye shall keepe it an holie feast vnto the Lord, throughout your generations: yee shall keepe it holie by an ordinance for euer.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 15] Seuen daies shall ye eat vnleauened bread, and in any case ye shall put away leauen the first day out of your houses: for whosoeuer eateth leauened bread from the first daie vntill the seuenth day, that person shalbe cut off from Israel.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 16] And in the first day shalbe an holie assemblie: also in the seuenth day shalbe an holy assemblie vnto you: no worke shalbe done in them, saue about that which euery man must eate: that onely may ye do.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 17] Ye shall keepe also the feast of vnleauened bread: for that same daye I will bring your armies out of the lande of Egypt: therefore ye shall obserue this day, throughout your posteritie, by an ordinance for euer.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 18] In the first moneth and the fourteenth day of the moneth at euen, yee shall eate vnleauened bread vnto the one and twentieth day of the moneth at euen.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 19] Seuen daies shall no leauen be founde in your houses: for whosoeuer eateth leauened bread, that person shalbe cut off from the Congregation of Israel: whether he bee a stranger, or borne in the land.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 20] Ye shall eate no leauened bread: but in all your habitations shall ye eate vnleauened bread.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 21] Then Moses called all the Elders of Israel, and saide vnto them, Choose out and take you for euerie of your housholdes a lambe, and kill the Passeouer.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 22] And take a bunch of hyssop, and dip it in the blood that is in the basen, and strike the lintell, and the doore cheekes with the blood that is in the basen, and let none of you goe out at the doore of his house, vntill the morning.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 23] For the Lord will passe by to smite the Egyptians: and when he seeth the blood vpon the lintel and on the two doore cheekes, the Lord wil passe ouer the doore, and wil not suffer the destroyer to come into your houses to plague you.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 24] Therefore shall ye obserue this thing as an ordinance both for thee and thy sonnes for euer.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 25] And when ye shall come into the land, which the Lord will giue you as hee hath promised, then ye shall keepe this seruice.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 26] And when your children aske you, What seruice is this ye keepe?
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 27] Then ye shall saye, It is the sacrifice of the Lordes Passeouer, which passed ouer the houses of the children of Israel in Egypt, when he smote the Egyptians, and preserued our houses. Then the people bowed them selues, and worshipped.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 28] So the children of Israel went, and did as the Lord had commanded Moses and Aaron: so did they.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 29] Nowe at midnight, the Lord smote all the first borne in the lande of Egypt, from the first borne of Pharaoh that sate on his throne, vnto the first borne of the captiue that was in prison, and all the first borne of beastes.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 30] And Pharaoh rose vp in the night, he, and all his seruants and all the Egyptians: and there was a great crye in Egypt: for there was no house where there was not one dead.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 31] And hee called to Moses and to Aaron by night, and saide, Rise vp, get you out from among my people, both yee, and the children of Israel, and goe serue the Lord as ye haue sayde.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 32] Take also your sheepe and your cattell as yee haue sayde, and depart, and blesse me also.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 33] And the Egyptians did force the people, because they would send them out of the land in haste: for they said, We die all.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 34] Therfore the people tooke their dough before it was leauened, euen their dough bound in clothes vpon their shoulders.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 35] And the children of Israel did according to the saying of Moses, and they asked of ye Egyptians iewels of siluer and iewels of gold, and raiment.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 36] And the Lord gaue the people fauour in the sight of the Egyptians: and they graunted their request: so they spoyled the Egyptians.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 37] Then the children of Israel tooke their iourney from Rameses to Succoth about sixe hundreth thousand men of foote, beside children.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 38] And a great multitude of sundrie sortes of people went out with them, and sheepe, and beeues, and cattel in great abundance.
[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 39] And they baked the dough which they brought out of Egypt, and made vnleauened cakes: for it was not leauened, because they were thrust out of Egypt, neither coulde they tarie, nor yet prepare themselues vitailes.

[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 42] It is a night to be kept holie to the Lord, because he brought them out of the lande of Egypt: this is that night of the Lord, which all the children of Israel must keepe throughout their generations.

[GNV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 12 | Verse 50] Then all the children of Israel did as the Lord commanded Moses and Aaron: so did they.
