[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 3] And he entred into one of the ships, which was Simons, and required him that he would thrust off a litle from the land: and he sate downe, and taught the people out of the ship.

[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 7] And they beckened to their parteners, which were in the other ship, that they shoulde come and helpe them, who came then, and filled both the ships, that they did sinke.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 8] Now when Simon Peter saw it, he fel down at Iesus knees, saying, Lord, go from me: for I am a sinfull man.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 9] For he was vtterly astonied, and all that were with him, for the draught of fishes which they tooke.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 10] And so was also Iames and Iohn the sonnes of Zebedeus, which were companions with Simon. Then Iesus sayde vnto Simon, Feare not: from henceforth thou shalt catch men.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 11] And when they had brought the ships to land, they forsooke all, and followed him.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 12] Nowe it came to passe, as he was in a certaine citie, beholde, there was a man full of leprosie, and when he sawe Iesus, he fell on his face, and besought him, saying, Lord, if thou wilt, thou canst make me cleane.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 13] So he stretched forth his hand, and touched him, saying, I will, be thou cleane. And immediately the leprosie departed from him.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 14] And he commanded him that hee should tell it no man: but Go, sayth he, and shew thy selfe to the Priest, and offer for thy clensing, as Moses hath commanded, for a witnes vnto them.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 15] But so much more went there a fame abroad of him, and great multitudes came together to heare, and to be healed of him of their infirmities.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 16] But he kept himselfe apart in the wildernes, and prayed.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 17] And it came to passe, on a certaine day, as he was teaching, that the Pharises and doctours of the Law sate by, which were come out of euery towne of Galile, and Iudea, and Hierusalem, and the power of the Lord was in him to heale them.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 18] Then beholde, men brought a man lying in a bed, which was taken with a palsie, and they sought meanes to bring him in, and to lay him before him.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 19] And when they could not finde by what way they might bring him in, because of the preasse, they went vp on the house, and let him downe through the tyling, bed and all, in the middes before Iesus.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 20] And when he sawe their faith, he sayd vnto him, Man, thy sinnes are forgiuen thee.

[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 22] But when Iesus perceiued their reasoning, he answered, and sayd vnto them, What reason ye in your hearts?

[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 25] And immediatly he rose vp before them, and tooke vp his bed whereon he lay, and departed to his owne house, praysing God.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 26] And they were all amased, and praysed God, and were filled with feare, saying, Doutlesse we haue seene strange things to day.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 27] And after that, he went foorth and sawe a Publicane named Leui, sitting at the receite of custome, and sayd vnto him, Follow me.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 28] And he left all, rose vp, and folowed him.

[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 31] Then Iesus answered, and sayd vnto them, They that are whole, neede not the Physician, but they that are sicke.

[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 33] Then they said vnto him, Why do the disciples of Iohn fast often, and pray, and the disciples of the Pharises also, but thine eate and drinke?

[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 35] But the dayes will come, euen when the bridegrome shalbe taken away from them: then shall they fast in those dayes.

[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 38] But newe wine must be powred into newe vessels: so both are preserued.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 5 | Verse 39] Also no man that drinketh olde wine, straightway desireth newe: for he sayth, The olde is more profitable.
