[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 1] And it came to passe, that on one of those dayes, as he taught the people in the Temple, and preached the Gospel, the hie Priests and the Scribes came vpon him with the Elders,
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 2] And spake vnto him, saying, Tell vs by what authoritie thou doest these things, or who is hee that hath giuen thee this authoritie?
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 3] And he answered, and sayde vnto them, I also will aske you one thing: tell me therefore:
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 4] The baptisme of Iohn, was it from heauen, or of men?
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 5] And they reasoned within themselues, saying, If we shall say, From heauen, he will say, Why then beleeued ye him not?
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 6] But if we shall say, Of men, all the people will stone vs: for they be perswaded that Iohn was a Prophet.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 7] Therefore they answered, that they could not tell whence it was.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 8] Then Iesus sayd vnto them, Neither tell I you, by what authoritie I doe these things.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 9] Then began he to speake to ye people this parable, A certaine man planted a vineyarde, and let it forth to husbandmen: and went into a strange countrey, for a great time.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 10] And at the time conuenient he sent a seruant to the husbandmen, that they should giue him of the fruite of the vineyard: but the husbandmen did beate him, and sent him away emptie.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 11] Againe he sent yet another seruant: and they did beate him, and foule entreated him, and sent him away emptie.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 12] Moreouer he sent the third, and him they wounded, and cast out.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 13] Then sayd the Lord of the vineyard, What shall I doe? I will send my beloued sonne: it may be that they will doe reuerence, when they see him.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 14] But when the husbandmen sawe him, they reasoned with themselues, saying, This is the heire: come, let vs kill him, that the inheritance may be ours.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 15] So they cast him out of the vineyarde, and killed him. What shall the Lord of the vineyarde therefore doe vnto them?
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 16] He will come and destroy these husbandmen, and wil giue out his vineyard to others. But when they heard it, they sayd, God forbid.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 17] And he beheld them, and said, What meaneth this then that is written, The stone that the builders refused, that is made the head of the corner?
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 18] Whosoeuer shall fall vpon that stone, shall be broken: and on whomsoeuer it shall fall, it will grinde him to pouder.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 19] Then the hie Priests, and the Scribes the same houre went about to lay hands on him: (but they feared the people) for they perceiued that he had spoken this parable against them.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 20] And they watched him, and sent forth spies, which should faine themselues iust men, to take him in his talke, and to deliuer him vnto the power and authoritie of the gouernour.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 21] And they asked him, saying, Master, we know that thou sayest, and teachest right, neither doest thou accept mans person, but teachest the way of God truely.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 22] Is it lawfull for vs to giue Cesar tribute or no?
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 23] But he perceiued their craftines, and sayd vnto them, Why tempt ye me?
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 24] Shew me a penie. Whose image and superscription hath it? They answered, and sayd, Cesars.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 25] Then he sayd vnto them, Giue then vnto Cesar the things which are Cesars, and to God those which are Gods.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 26] And they could not reproue his saying before the people: but they marueiled at his answere, and helde their peace.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 27] Then came to him certaine of the Sadduces (which denie that there is any resurrection) and they asked him,
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 28] Saying, Master, Moses wrote vnto vs, If any mans brother die hauing a wife, and hee die without children, that his brother should take his wife, and raise vp seede vnto his brother.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 29] Now there were seuen brethren, and the first tooke a wife, and he dyed without children.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 30] And the second tooke the wife, and he dyed childelesse.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 31] Then the third tooke her: and so likewise the seuen dyed, and left no children.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 32] And last of all the woman dyed also.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 33] Therefore at the resurrection, whose wife of them shall she be? for seuen had her to wife.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 34] Then Iesus answered, and sayd vnto them, The children of this world marry wiues, and are married.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 35] But they which shalbe counted worthy to enioy that world, and the resurrection from the dead, neither marry wiues, neither are married.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 36] For they can die no more, forasmuch as they are equall vnto the Angels, and are the sonnes of God, since they are the children of the resurrection.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 37] And that the dead shall rise againe, euen Moses shewed it besides the bush, when he said, The Lord is the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Iacob.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 38] For he is not the God of the dead, but of them which liue: for all liue vnto him.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 39] Then certaine of the Scribes answered, and sayd, Master, thou hast well sayd.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 40] And after that, durst they not aske him any thing at all.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 41] Then sayd he vnto them, Howe say they that Christ is Dauids sonne?
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 42] And Dauid himselfe sayth in the booke of the Psalmes, The Lord sayd vnto my Lord, Sit at my right hand,
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 43] Till I shall make thine enemies thy footestoole.
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 44] Seeing Dauid called him Lord, howe is he then his sonne?
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 45] Then in the audience of all the people he sayd vnto his disciples,
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 46] Beware of the Scribes, which willingly go in long robes, and loue salutations in the markets, and the highest seates in the assemblies, and the chiefe roomes at feastes:
[GNV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 20 | Verse 47] Which deuoure widowes houses, and in shewe make long prayers: These shall receiue greater damnation.
