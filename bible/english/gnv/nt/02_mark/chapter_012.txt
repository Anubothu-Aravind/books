[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 5] And againe he sent another, and him they slew, and many other, beating some, and killing some.

[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 7] But ye husbandmen said among themselues, This is the heire: come, let vs kill him, and the inheritance shalbe ours.
[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 8] So they tooke him, and killed him, and cast him out of the vineyard.

[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 10] Haue ye not read so much as this Scripture? The stone which the builders did refuse, is made the head of the corner.
[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 11] This was done of the Lord, and it is marueilous in our eyes.
[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 12] Then they went about to take him, but they feared the people: for they perceiued that he spake that parable against them: therefore they left him, and went their way.
[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 13] And they sent vnto him certaine of the Pharises, and of ye Herodians that they might take him in his talke.
[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 14] And when they came, they saide vnto him, Master, we know that thou art true, and carest for no man: for thou considerest not the person of men, but teachest the way of God truely, Is it lawfull to giue tribute to Cesar, or not?
[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 15] Should we giue it, or should we not giue it? but he knew their hypocrisie, and said vnto them, Why tempt ye me? Bring me a peny, that I may see it.

[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 17] Then Iesus answered, and saide vnto them, Giue to Cesar the things that are Cesars, and to God, those that are Gods: and they marueiled at him.

[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 19] Master, Moses wrote vnto vs, If any mans brother die, and leaue his wife, and leaue no children, that his brother should take his wife, and rayse vp seede vnto his brother.
[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 20] There were seuen brethren, and the first tooke a wife, and when he died, left no issue.
[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 21] Then the seconde tooke her, and he died, neither did he yet leaue issue, and the third likewise:
[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 22] So those seuen had her, and left no yssue: last of all the wife died also.
[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 23] In the resurrection then, when they shall rise againe, whose wife shall she be of them? for seuen had her to wife.
[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 24] Then Iesus answered, and saide vnto them, Are ye not therefore deceiued, because ye knowe not the Scriptures, neither the power of God?

[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 26] And as touching the dead, that they shall rise againe, haue ye not read in the booke of Moses, howe in the bush God spake vnto him, saying, I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Iacob?
[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 27] God is not ye God of the dead, but the God of the liuing. Ye are therefore greatly deceiued.
[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 28] Then came one of the Scribes that had heard them disputing together, and perceiuing that he had answered them well, he asked him, Which is the first commandement of all?

[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 30] Thou shalt therefore loue the Lord thy God with all thine heart, and with all thy soule, and with all thy minde, and with all thy strength: this is the first commandement.
[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 31] And the second is like, that is, Thou shalt loue thy neighbour as thy selfe. There is none other commandement greater then these.
[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 32] Then that Scribe said vnto him, Well, Master, thou hast saide the trueth, that there is one God, and that there is none but he,
[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 33] And to loue him with all the heart, and with all the vnderstanding, and with all the soule, and with all the strength, and to loue his neighbour as himselfe, is more then all whole burnt offerings and sacrifices.
[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 34] Then when Iesus saw that he answered discreetely, he saide vnto him, Thou art not farre from the kingdome of God. And no man after that durst aske him any question.

[GNV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 44] For they all did cast in of their superfluitie: but she of her pouertie did cast in all that she had, euen all her liuing.
