[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 16] And of his fulnesse haue all we receiued, and grace for grace.

[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 18] No man hath seene God at any time: that onely begotten Sonne, which is in the bosome of the Father, he hath declared him.

[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 20] And he confessed and denied not, and said plainely, I am not that Christ.

[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 23] He said, I am the voyce of him that cryeth in the wildernesse, Make straight the way of the Lord, as said the Prophet Esaias.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 24] Nowe they which were sent, were of the Pharises.

[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 26] Iohn answered them, saying, I baptize with water: but there is one among you, whom ye knowe not.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 27] He it is that commeth after me, which was before me, whose shoe latchet I am not worthie to vnloose.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 28] These things were done in Bethabara beyond Iordan, where Iohn did baptize.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 29] The next day Iohn, seeth Iesus comming vnto him, and saith, Beholde that Lambe of God, which taketh away the sinne of the world.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 30] This is he of whom I saide, After me commeth a man, which was before me: for he was better then I.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 31] And I knewe him not: but because he should be declared to Israel, therefore am I come, baptizing with water.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 32] So Iohn bare recorde, saying, I behelde that Spirit come downe from heauen, like a doue, and it abode vpon him,
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 33] And I knewe him not: but he that sent me to baptize with water, he saide vnto me, Vpon whom thou shalt see that Spirit come downe, and tary still on him, that is he which baptizeth with the holy Ghost.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 34] And I sawe, and bare record that this is that Sonne of God.
