[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 2] And the souldiers platted a crowne of thornes, and put it on his head, and they put on him a purple garment,
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 3] And saide, Haile, King of the Iewes. And they smote him with their roddes.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 4] Then Pilate went foorth againe, and said vnto them, Behold, I bring him forth to you, that ye may knowe, that I finde no fault in him at all.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 5] Then came Iesus foorth wearing a crowne of thornes, and a purple garment. And Pilate said vnto them, Beholde the man.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 6] Then when the hie Priests and officers sawe him, they cried, saying, Crucifie, crucifie him. Pilate said vnto them, Take yee him and crucifie him: for I finde no fault in him.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 7] The Iewes answered him, We haue a lawe, and by our law he ought to die, because he made himselfe the Sonne of God.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 8] When Pilate then heard that woorde, he was the more afraide,
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 9] And went againe into the common hall, and saide vnto Iesus, Whence art thou? But Iesus gaue him none answere.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 10] Then saide Pilate vnto him, Speakest thou not vnto me? Knowest thou not that I haue power to crucifie thee, and haue power to loose thee?
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 11] Iesus answered, Thou couldest haue no power at all against me, except it were giuen thee from aboue: therefore he that deliuered me vnto thee, hath the greater sinne.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 12] From thence foorth Pilate sought to loose him, but the Iewes cried, saying, If thou deliuer him, thou art not Cesars friende: for whosoeuer maketh himselfe a King, speaketh against Cesar.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 13] When Pilate heard this woorde, hee brought Iesus foorth, and sate downe in the iudgement seate in a place called the Pauement, and in Hebrewe, Gabbatha.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 14] And it was the Preparation of the Passeouer, and about the sixt houre: and hee sayde vnto the Iewes, Beholde your King.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 15] But they cried, Away with him, away with him, crucifie him. Pilate sayde vnto them, Shall I crucifie your King? The high Priestes answered, We haue no King but Cesar.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 16] Then deliuered he him vnto them, to be crucified. And they tooke Iesus, and led him away.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 17] And he bare his owne crosse, and came into a place named of dead mens Skulles, which is called in Hebrewe, Golgotha:
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 18] Where they crucified him, and two other with him, on either side one, and Iesus in the middes.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 19] And Pilate wrote also a title, and put it on the crosse, and it was written, JESUS OF NAZARETH THE KING OF THE JEWES.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 20] This title then read many of the Iewes: for the place where Iesus was crucified, was neere to the citie: and it was written in Hebrewe, Greeke and Latine.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 21] Then saide the hie Priests of the Iewes to Pilate, Write not, The King of the Iewes, but that he sayd, I am King of the Iewes.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 22] Pilate answered, What I haue written, I haue written.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 23] Then the souldiers, when they had crucified Iesus, tooke his garments (and made foure partes, to euery souldier a part) and his coat: and the coat was without seame wouen from the toppe throughout.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 24] Therefore they sayde one to another, Let vs not deuide it, but cast lots for it, whose it shall be. This was that the Scripture might be fulfilled, which sayth, They parted my garments among them, and on my coate did cast lots. So the souldiers did these things in deede.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 25] Then stoode by the crosse of Iesus his mother, and his mothers sister, Marie the wife of Cleopas, and Marie Magdalene.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 26] And when Iesus sawe his mother, and the disciple standing by, whom he loued, he said vnto his mother, Woman, beholde thy sonne.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 27] Then saide he to the disciple, Beholde thy mother: and from that houre, the disciple tooke her home vnto him.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 28] After, when Iesus knew that all things were performed, that the Scripture might be fulfilled, he said, I thirst.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 29] And there was set a vessell full of vineger: and they filled a spondge with vineger: and put it about an Hyssope stalke, and put it to his mouth.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 30] Nowe when Iesus had receiued of the vineger, he saide, It is finished, and bowed his head, and gaue vp the ghost.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 31] The Iewes then (because it was the Preparation, that the bodies should not remaine vpon the crosse on the Sabbath day: for that Sabbath was an hie day) besought Pilate that their legges might be broken, and that they might be taken downe.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 32] Then came the souldiers and brake the legges of the first, and of the other, which was crucified with Iesus.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 33] But when they came to Iesus, and saw that he was dead alreadie, they brake not his legges.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 34] But one of the souldiers with a speare pearced his side, and foorthwith came there out blood and water.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 35] And he that sawe it, bare recorde, and his record is true: and he knoweth that he saith true, that ye might beleeue it.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 36] For these things were done, that the Scripture shoulde be fulfilled, Not a bone of him shalbe broken.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 37] And againe an other Scripture saith, They shall see him whom they haue thrust through.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 38] And after these things, Ioseph of Arimathea (who was a disciple of Iesus, but secretly for feare of the Iewes) besought Pilate that he might take downe the bodie of Iesus. And Pilate gaue him licence. He came then and tooke Iesus body.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 39] And there came also Nicodemus (which first came to Iesus by night) and brought of myrrhe and aloes mingled together about an hundreth pound.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 40] Then tooke they the body of Iesus, and wrapped it in linnen clothes with the odours, as the maner of the Iewes is to burie.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 41] And in that place where Iesus was crucified, was a garden, and in the garden a newe sepulchre, wherein was neuer man yet laid.
[GNV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 42] There then laide they Iesus, because of the Iewes Preparation day, for the sepulchre was neere.
