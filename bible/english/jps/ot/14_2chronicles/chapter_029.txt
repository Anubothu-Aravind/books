[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 2] And he did that which was right in the eyes of the LORD, according to all that David his father had done.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 3] He in the first year of his reign, in the first month, opened the doors of the house of the LORD, and repaired them.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 4] And he brought in the priests and the Levites, and gathered them together into the broad place on the east;
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 5] and said unto them: 'Hear me, ye Levites: now sanctify yourselves, and sanctify the house of the LORD, the God of your fathers, and carry forth the filthiness out of the holy place.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 6] For our fathers have acted treacherously, and done that which was evil in the sight of the LORD our God, and have forsaken Him, and have turned away their faces from the habitation of the LORD, and turned their backs.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 7] Also they have shut up the doors of the porch, and put out the lamps, and have not burned incense nor offered burnt-offerings in the holy place unto the God of Israel.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 8] Wherefore the wrath of the LORD was upon Judah and Jerusalem, and He hath delivered them to be a horror, an astonishment, and a hissing, as ye see with your eyes.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 9] For, lo, our fathers have fallen by the sword, and our sons and our daughters and our wives are in captivity for this.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 10] Now it is in my heart to make a covenant with the LORD, the God of Israel, that His fierce anger may turn away from us.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 11] My sons, be not now negligent; for the LORD hath chosen you to stand before Him, to minister unto Him, and that ye should be His ministers, and offer unto Him.'
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 12] Then the Levites arose, Mahath the son of Amasai, and Joel the son of Azariah, of the sons of the Kohathites; and of the sons of Merari, Kish the son of Abdi, and Azariah the son of Jehallelel; and of the Gershonites, Joah the son of Zimmah, and Eden the son of Joah;
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 13] and of the sons of Elizaphan, Shimri and Jeiel; and of the sons of Asaph, Zechariah and Mattaniah;
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 14] and of the sons of Heman, Jehiel and Shimei; and of the sons of Jeduthun, Shemaiah and Uzziel.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 15] And they gathered their brethren, and sanctified themselves, and went in, according to the commandment of the king by the words of the LORD, to cleanse the house of the LORD.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 16] And the priests went in unto the inner part of the house of the LORD, to cleanse it, and brought out all the uncleanness that they found in the temple of the LORD into the court of the house of the LORD. And the Levites took it, to carry it out abroad to the brook Kidron.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 17] Now they began on the first day of the first month to sanctify, and on the eighth day of the month came they to the porch of the LORD; and they sanctified the house of the LORD in eight days; and on the sixteenth day of the first month they made an end.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 18] Then they went in to Hezekiah the king within the palace, and said: 'We have cleansed all the house of the LORD, even the altar of burnt-offering, with all the vessels thereof, and the table of showbread, with all the vessels thereof.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 19] Moreover all the vessels, which king Ahaz in his reign did cast away when he acted treacherously, have we prepared and sanctified; and, behold, they are before the altar of the LORD.'
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 20] Then Hezekiah the king arose early, and gathered the princes of the city, and went up to the house of the LORD.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 21] And they brought seven bullocks, and seven rams, and seven lambs, and seven he-goats, for a sin-offering for the kingdom and for the sanctuary and for Judah. And he commanded the priests the sons of Aaron to offer them on the altar of the LORD.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 22] So they killed the bullocks, and the priests received the blood, and dashed it against the altar; and they killed the rams, and dashed the blood against the altar; they killed also the lambs, and dashed the blood against the altar.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 23] And they brought near the he-goats for the sin-offering before the king and the congregation, and they laid their hands upon them;
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 24] and the priests killed them, and they made a sin-offering with their blood upon the altar, to make atonement for all Israel; for the king commanded that the burnt-offering and the sin-offering should be made for all Israel.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 25] And he set the Levites in the house of the LORD with cymbals, with psalteries, and with harps, according to the commandment of David, and of Gad the king's seer, and Nathan the prophet; for the commandment was of the LORD by His prophets.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 26] And the Levites stood with the instruments of David, and the priests with the trumpets.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 27] And Hezekiah commanded to offer the burnt-offering upon the altar. And when the burnt-offering began, the song of the LORD began also, and the trumpets, together with the instruments of David king of Israel.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 28] And all the congregation prostrated themselves, and the singers sang, and the trumpeters sounded; all this continued until the burnt-offering was finished.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 29] And when they had made an end of offering, the king and all that were present with him bowed themselves and prostrated themselves.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 30] Moreover Hezekiah the king and the princes commanded the Levites to sing praises unto the LORD with the words of David, and of Asaph the seer. And they sang praises with gladness, and they bowed their heads and prostrated themselves.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 31] Then Hezekiah answered and said: 'Now ye have consecrated yourselves unto the LORD, come near and bring sacrifices and thank-offerings into the house of the LORD.' And the congregation brought in sacrifices and thank-offerings; and as many as were of a willing heart brought burnt-offerings.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 32] And the number of the burnt-offerings, which the congregation brought, was threescore and ten bullocks, a hundred rams, and two hundred lambs; all these were for a burnt-offering to the LORD.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 33] And the consecrated things were six hundred oxen and three thousand sheep.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 34] But the priests were too few, so that they could not flay all the burnt-offerings; wherefore their brethren the Levites did help them, till the work was ended, and until the priests had sanctified themselves; for the Levites were more upright in heart to sanctify themselves than the priests.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 35] And also the burnt-offerings were in abundance, with the fat of the peace-offerings, and the drink-offerings for every burnt-offering. So the service of the house of the LORD was firmly established.
[JPS | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 29 | Verse 36] And Hezekiah rejoiced, and all the people, because of that which God had prepared for the people; for the thing was done suddenly.
