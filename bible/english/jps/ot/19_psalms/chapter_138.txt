[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 138 | Verse 1] A Psalm of David. I will give Thee thanks with my whole heart, in the presence of the mighty will I sing praises unto Thee.
[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 138 | Verse 2] I will bow down toward Thy holy temple, and give thanks unto Thy name for Thy mercy and for Thy truth; for Thou hast magnified Thy word above all Thy name.
[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 138 | Verse 3] In the day that I called, Thou didst answer me; Thou didst encourage me in my soul with strength.
[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 138 | Verse 4] All the kings of the earth shall give Thee thanks, O LORD, for they have heard the words of Thy mouth.
[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 138 | Verse 5] Yea, they shall sing of the ways of the LORD; for great is the glory of the LORD.
[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 138 | Verse 6] For though the LORD be high, yet regardeth He the lowly, and the haughty He knoweth from afar.
[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 138 | Verse 7] Though I walk in the midst of trouble, Thou quickenest me; Thou stretchest forth Thy hand against the wrath of mine enemies, and Thy right hand doth save me.
[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 138 | Verse 8] The LORD will accomplish that which concerneth me; Thy mercy, O LORD, endureth for ever; forsake not the work of Thine own hands.
