[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 82 | Verse 1] A Psalm of Asaph. God standeth in the congregation of God; in the midst of the judges He judgeth:
[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 82 | Verse 2] 'How long will ye judge unjustly, and respect the persons of the wicked? Selah
[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 82 | Verse 3] Judge the poor and fatherless; do justice to the afflicted and destitute.
[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 82 | Verse 4] Rescue the poor and needy; deliver them out of the hand of the wicked.
[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 82 | Verse 5] They know not, neither do they understand; they go about in darkness; all the foundations of the earth are moved.
[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 82 | Verse 6] I said: Ye are godlike beings, and all of you sons of the Most High.
[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 82 | Verse 7] Nevertheless ye shall die like men, and fall like one of the princes.'
[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 82 | Verse 8] Arise, O God, judge the earth; for Thou shalt possess all nations.
