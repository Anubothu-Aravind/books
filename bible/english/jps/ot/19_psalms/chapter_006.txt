[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 6 | Verse 1] (6-1) For the Leader; with string-music; on the Sheminith. A Psalm of David. (6-2) O LORD, rebuke me not in Thine anger, neither chasten me in Thy wrath.
[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 6 | Verse 2] (6-3) Be gracious unto me, O LORD, for I languish away; heal me, O LORD, for my bones are affrighted.
[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 6 | Verse 3] (6-4) My soul also is sore affrighted; and Thou, O LORD, how long?
[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 6 | Verse 4] (6-5) Return, O LORD, deliver my soul; save me for Thy mercy's sake.
[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 6 | Verse 5] (6-6) For in death there is no remembrance of Thee; in the nether-world who will give Thee thanks?
[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 6 | Verse 6] (6-7) I am weary with my groaning; every night make I my bed to swim; I melt away my couch with my tears.
[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 6 | Verse 7] (6-8) Mine eye is dimmed because of vexation; it waxeth old because of all mine adversaries.
[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 6 | Verse 8] (6-9) Depart from me, all ye workers of iniquity; for the LORD hath heard the voice of my weeping.
[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 6 | Verse 9] (6-10) The LORD hath heard my supplication; the LORD receiveth my prayer.
[JPS | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 6 | Verse 10] (6-11) All mine enemies shall be ashamed and sore affrighted; they shall turn back, they shall be ashamed suddenly.
