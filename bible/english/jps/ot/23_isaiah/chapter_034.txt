[JPS | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 34 | Verse 1] Come near, ye nations, to hear, and attend, ye peoples; let the earth hear, and the fulness thereof, the world, and all things that come forth of it.
[JPS | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 34 | Verse 2] For the LORD hath indignation against all the nations, and fury against all their host; He hath utterly destroyed them, He hath delivered them to the slaughter.
[JPS | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 34 | Verse 3] Their slain also shall be cast out, and the stench of their carcasses shall come up, and the mountains shall be melted with their blood.
[JPS | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 34 | Verse 4] And all the host of heaven shall moulder away, and the heavens shall be rolled together as a scroll; and all their host shall fall down, as the leaf falleth off from the vine, and as a falling fig from the fig-tree.
[JPS | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 34 | Verse 5] For My sword hath drunk its fill in heaven; behold, it shall come down upon Edom, and upon the people of My ban, to judgment.
[JPS | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 34 | Verse 6] The sword of the LORD is filled with blood, it is made fat with fatness, with the blood of lambs and goats, with the fat of the kidneys of rams; for the LORD hath a sacrifice in Bozrah, and a great slaughter in the land of Edom.
[JPS | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 34 | Verse 7] And the wild-oxen shall come down with them, and the bullocks with the bulls; and their land shall be drunken with blood, and their dust made fat with fatness.
[JPS | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 34 | Verse 8] For the LORD hath a day of vengeance, a year of recompense for the controversy of Zion.
[JPS | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 34 | Verse 9] And the streams thereof shall be turned into pitch, and the dust thereof into brimstone, and the land thereof shall become burning pitch.
[JPS | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 34 | Verse 10] It shall not be quenched night nor day, the smoke thereof shall go up for ever; from generation to generation it shall lie waste: none shall pass through it for ever and ever.
[JPS | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 34 | Verse 11] But the pelican and the bittern shall possess it, and the owl and the raven shall dwell therein; and He shall stretch over it the line of confusion, and the plummet of emptiness.
[JPS | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 34 | Verse 12] As for her nobles, none shall be there to be called to the kingdom; and all her princes shall be nothing.
[JPS | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 34 | Verse 13] And thorns shall come up in her palaces, nettles and thistles in the fortresses thereof; and it shall be a habitation of wild-dogs, an enclosure for ostriches.
[JPS | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 34 | Verse 14] And the wild-cats shall meet with the jackals, and the satyr shall cry to his fellow; yea, the night-monster shall repose there, and shall find her a place of rest.
[JPS | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 34 | Verse 15] There shall the arrowsnake make her nest, and lay, and hatch, and brood under her shadow; yea, there shall the kites be gathered, every one with her mate.
[JPS | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 34 | Verse 16] Seek ye out of the book of the LORD, and read; no one of these shall be missing, none shall want her mate; for My mouth it hath commanded, and the breath thereof it hath gathered them.
[JPS | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 34 | Verse 17] And He hath cast the lot for them, and His hand hath divided it unto them by line; they shall possess it for ever, from generation to generation shall they dwell therein.
