[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 1] Now it came to pass on a second-first sabbath, that he was going through the grainfields; and his disciples plucked the ears, and did eat, rubbing them in their hands.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 2] But certain of the Pharisees said unto them, Why do ye that which it is not lawful to do on the sabbath day?
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 3] And Jesus answered them and said, Have ye not read even this, what David did, when he was hungry, he, and they that were with him;
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 4] how he entered into the house of God, and took and ate the showbread, and gave also to them that were with him; which it is not lawful to eat save for the priests alone?
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 5] And he said unto them, The Son of man is lord even of the sabbath.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 6] And it came to pass also on another sabbath, that he entered into the synagogue and taught: and there was a man there, and his right hand was withered.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 7] And the scribes and the Pharisees watched, whether he would heal on the sabbath; that they might find an accusation against him.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 8] But he knew their thoughts; and he said to the man that had his hand withered, Rise up, and stand forth in the midst. And he arose and stood forth.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 9] Then Jesus said unto them, I will ask you something, Is it lawful on the sabbath to do good, or to do harm? to save a life, or to kill?
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 10] And he looked round about on them all, and said unto him, Stretch forth thy hand. And he did so: and his hand was restored whole as the other.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 11] But they were filled with madness; and communed one with another what they might do to Jesus.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 12] And it came to pass in these days, that he went out into the mountain to pray; and he continued all night in prayer to God.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 13] And when it was day, he called his disciples; and he chose from them twelve, whom also he named apostles:
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 14] Simon, whom he also named Peter, and Andrew his brother, James and John, Philip and Bartholomew,
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 15] Matthew and Thomas, James the son of Alphaeus, and Simon who was called the Zealot,
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 16] Judas the son of James, and Judas Iscariot, who also became a traitor;
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 17] and he came down with them, and stood on a level place, and a multitude of his disciples, and a great number of the people from all Judea and Jerusalem, and the seacoast of Tyre and Sidon, who came to hear him, and to be healed of their diseases;
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 18] and they that were vexed by unclean spirits: and they were healed.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 19] And all the multitude sought to touch him; for power came forth from him, and healed them all.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 20] And he lifted up his eyes on his disciples, and said, Blessed are ye poor: for yours is the kingdom of God.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 21] Blessed are ye that hunger now: for ye shall be filled. Blessed are ye that weep now: for ye shall laugh.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 22] Blessed are ye, when men shall hate you, and when they shall separate you from their company, and reproach you, and cast out your name as evil, for the Son of man’s sake.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 23] Rejoice in that day, and leap for joy: for behold, your reward is great in heaven; for in the same manner did their fathers unto the prophets.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 24] But woe unto you that are rich! for ye have received your consolation.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 25] Woe unto you, ye that are full! for ye shall hunger. Woe unto you, ye that laugh now! for ye shall mourn and weep.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 26] Woe, when men shall speak well of you! for in the same manner did their fathers to the false prophets.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 27] But I say unto you that hear, Love your enemies, do good to them that hate you,
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 28] bless them that curse you, pray for them that despitefully use you.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 29] To him that smiteth thee on the one cheek offer also the other; and from him that taketh away thy cloak withhold not thy coat also.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 30] And give to everyone that asketh thee; and of him that taketh away thy goods ask them not again.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 31] And as ye would that men should do to you, do ye also to them likewise.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 32] And if ye love them that love you, what thank have ye? for even sinners love those that love them.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 33] And if ye do good to them that do good to you, what thank have ye? for even sinners do the same.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 34] And if ye lend to them of whom ye hope to receive back, what thank have ye? for even sinners lend to sinners, to receive again as much.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 35] But love your enemies, and do them good, and lend, never despairing; and your reward shall be great, and ye shall be sons of the Most High: for he is kind toward the unthankful and evil.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 36] Be ye therefore merciful, even as your Father also is merciful.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 37] And judge not, and ye shall not be judged: condemn not, and ye shall not be condemned: release, and ye shall be released:
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 38] give, and it shall be given unto you; good measure, pressed down, and shaken together, and running over, shall they give into your bosom. For with the same measure ye mete it shall be measured to you again.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 39] And he spake a parable unto them, Can the blind guide the blind? shall they not both fall into a pit?
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 40] The disciple is not above his teacher: but everyone when he is perfected shall be as his teacher.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 41] And why beholdest thou the mote that is in thy brother’s eye, but considerest not the beam that is in thine own eye?
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 42] Or how canst thou say to thy brother, Brother, let me cast out the mote that is in thine eye, when thou thyself beholdest not the beam that is in thine own eye? Thou hypocrite, cast out first the beam out of thine own eye, and then shalt thou see clearly to cast out the mote that is in thy brother’s eye.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 43] For there is no good tree that bringeth forth corrupt fruit; nor a corrupt tree that bringeth forth good fruit.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 44] For each tree is known by its own fruit. For of thorns men do not gather figs, nor of a bramble bush gather they grapes.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 45] The good man out of the good treasure of his heart bringeth forth that which is good; and the evil man out of the evil treasure of his heart bringeth forth that which is evil: for out of the abundance of the heart his mouth speaketh.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 46] And why call ye me, Lord, Lord, and do not the things which I say?
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 47] Everyone that cometh unto me, and heareth my words, and doeth them, I will show you to whom he is like:
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 48] he is like a man building a house, who dug and went deep, and laid a foundation upon the rock: and when a flood arose, the stream brake against that house, and could not shake it: for it had been founded upon the rock.
[ASV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 6 | Verse 49] But he that heareth, and doeth not, is like a man that built a house upon the earth without a foundation; against which the stream brake, and straightaway it fell; and the ruin of that house was great.
