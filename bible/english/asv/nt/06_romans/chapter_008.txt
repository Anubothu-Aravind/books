[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 1] There is therefore now no condemnation to them that are in Christ Jesus, who walk not after the flesh, but after the Spirit.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 2] For the law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus made me free from the law of sin and of death.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 3] For what the law could not do, in that it was weak through the flesh, God, sending his own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh and for sin, condemned sin in the flesh:
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 4] that the ordinance of the law might be fulfilled in us, who walk not after the flesh, but after the Spirit.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 5] For they that are after the flesh mind the things of the flesh; but they that are after the Spirit the things of the Spirit.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 6] For the mind of the flesh is death; but the mind of the Spirit is life and peace:
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 7] because the mind of the flesh is enmity against God; for it is not subject to the law of God, neither indeed can it be:
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 8] and they that are in the flesh cannot please God.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 9] But ye are not in the flesh but in the Spirit, if so be that the Spirit of God dwelleth in you. But if any man hath not the Spirit of Christ, he is none of his.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 10] And if Christ is in you, the body is dead because of sin; but the spirit is life because of righteousness.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 11] But if the Spirit of him that raised up Jesus from the dead dwelleth in you, he that raised up Christ from the dead shall give life also to your mortal bodies because of his Spirit that dwelleth in you.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 12] So then, brethren, we are debtors, not to the flesh, to live after the flesh:
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 13] for if ye live after the flesh, ye must die; but if by the Spirit ye put to death the deeds of the body, ye shall live.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 14] For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, these are sons of God.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 15] For ye received not the spirit of bondage again unto fear; but ye received the spirit of adoption, whereby we cry, Abba, Father.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 16] The Spirit himself beareth witness with our spirit, that we are children of God:
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 17] and if children, then heirs; heirs of God, and joint heirs with Christ; if so be that we suffer with him, that we may be also glorified with him.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 18] For I reckon that the sufferings of this present time are not worthy to be compared with the glory which shall be revealed to us-ward.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 19] For the earnest expectation of the creation waiteth for the revealing of the sons of God.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 20] For the creation was subjected to vanity, not of its own will, but by reason of him who subjected it in hope;
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 21] because the creation itself also shall be delivered from the bondage of corruption into the liberty of the glory of the children of God.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 22] For we know that the whole creation groaneth and travaileth in pain together until now.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 23] And not only so, but ourselves also, who have the firstfruits of the Spirit, even we ourselves groan within ourselves, waiting for our adoption, to wit, the redemption of our body.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 24] For in hope were we saved: but hope that is seen is not hope: for what a man seeth, why doth he yet hope for?
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 25] But if we hope for that which we see not, then do we with patience wait for it.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 26] And in like manner the Spirit also helpeth our infirmities: for we know not how to pray as we ought; but the Spirit himself maketh intercession for us with groanings which cannot be uttered;
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 27] and he that searcheth the hearts knoweth what is the mind of the Spirit, because he maketh intercession for the saints according to the will of God.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 28] And we know that to them that love God all things work together for good, even to them that are called according to his purpose.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 29] For whom he foreknew, he also foreordained to be conformed to the image of his Son, that he might be the firstborn among many brethren:
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 30] and whom he foreordained, them he also called: and whom he called, them he also justified: and whom he justified, them he also glorified.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 31] What then shall we say to these things? If God is for us, who is against us?
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 32] He that spared not his own Son, but delivered him up for us all, how shall he not also with him freely give us all things?
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 33] Who shall lay anything to the charge of God’s elect? It is God that justifieth;
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 34] who is he that condemneth? It is Christ that died, yea rather, that was also raised, who is also at the right hand of God, who also maketh intercession for us.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 35] Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? shall tribulation, or anguish, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or peril, or sword?
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 36] Even as it is written, For thy sake we are killed all the day long; We were accounted as sheep for the slaughter.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 37] Nay, in all these things we are more than conquerors through him that loved us.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 38] For I am persuaded, that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor powers, nor things present, nor things to come,
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 8 | Verse 39] nor height, nor depth, nor any other creature, shall be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.
