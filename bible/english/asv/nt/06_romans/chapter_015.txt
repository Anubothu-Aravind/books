[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 2] Let each one of us please his neighbor for that which is good, unto edifying.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 3] For Christ also pleased not himself; but, as it is written, The reproaches of them that reproached thee fell upon me.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 4] For whatsoever things were written aforetime were written aforetime for our learning, that through patience and through comfort of the scriptures we might have hope.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 5] Now the God of patience and of comfort grant you to be of the same mind one with another according to Christ Jesus:
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 6] that with one accord ye may with one mouth glorify the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 7] Wherefore receive ye one another, even as Christ also received you, to the glory of God.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 8] Now I say that Christ Jesus hath been made a minister of the circumcision for the truth of God, that he might confirm the promises given unto the fathers,
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 9] and that the Gentiles might glorify God for his mercy; as it is written, Therefore will I give praise unto thee among the Gentiles, And sing unto thy name.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 10] And again he saith, Rejoice, ye Gentiles, with his people.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 11] And again, Praise the Lord, all ye Gentiles; And praise him, all ye peoples.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 12] And again, Isaiah saith, There shall be the root of Jesse, And he that ariseth to rule over the Gentiles; On him shall the Gentiles hope.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 13] Now the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, that ye may abound in hope, in the power of the Holy Spirit.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 14] And I myself also am persuaded of you, my brethren, that ye yourselves are full of goodness, filled with all knowledge, able also to admonish others.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 15] But I write the more boldly unto you, brethren, in some measure, as putting you again in remembrance, because of the grace that was given me of God,
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 16] that I should be a minister of Jesus Christ unto the Gentiles, ministering the gospel of God, that the offering up of the Gentiles might be made acceptable, being sanctified by the Holy Spirit.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 17] I have therefore my glorying in Christ Jesus in things pertaining to God.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 18] For I will not dare to speak of any things save those which Christ wrought through me, for the obedience of the Gentiles, by word and deed,
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 19] in the power of signs and wonders, in the power of the Spirit of God; so that from Jerusalem, and round about even unto Illyricum, I have fully preached the gospel of Christ;
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 20] yea, making it my aim so to preach the gospel, not where Christ was already named, that I might not build upon another man’s foundation;
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 21] but, as it is written, To whom no tidings of him came, they shall see, And they who have not heard shall understand.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 22] Wherefore also I was hindered these many times from coming to you:
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 23] but now, having no more any place in these regions, and having these many years a longing to come unto you,
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 24] whensoever I go unto Spain, I will come to you (for I hope to see you in my journey, and to be brought on my way thitherward by you, if first in some measure I shall have been satisfied with your company)—
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 25] but now, I say, I go unto Jerusalem, ministering unto the saints.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 26] For it hath been the good pleasure of Macedonia and Achaia to make a certain contribution for the poor among the saints that are at Jerusalem.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 27] Yea, it hath been their good pleasure; and their debtors they are. For if the Gentiles have been made partakers of their spiritual things, they owe it to them also to minister unto them in carnal things.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 28] When therefore I have accomplished this, and have sealed to them this fruit, I will go on by you unto Spain.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 29] And I know that, when I come unto you, I shall come in the fullness of the blessing of the gospel of Christ.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 30] Now I beseech you, brethren, by our Lord Jesus Christ, and by the love of the Spirit, that ye strive together with me in your prayers to God for me;
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 31] that I may be delivered from them that are disobedient in Judea, and that my ministration which I have for Jerusalem may be acceptable to the saints;
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 32] that I may come unto you in joy through the will of God, and together with you find rest.
[ASV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 33] Now the God of peace be with you all. Amen.
