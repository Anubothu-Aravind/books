[ASV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 22] and the seven took her, and left no seed. Last of all the woman also died.

[ASV | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 12 | Verse 27] He is not the God of the dead, but the God of the living: ye therefore do greatly err.
