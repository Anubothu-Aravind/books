[ASV | EN | NT | Revelation | Chapter 8 | Verse 10] And the third angel sounded, and there fell from heaven a great star, burning as a torch, and it fell upon the third part of the rivers, and upon the fountains of the waters;

[ASV | EN | NT | Revelation | Chapter 8 | Verse 13] And I saw, and I heard an eagle, flying in midheaven, saying with a great voice, Woe, woe, woe, to them that dwell on the earth, by reason of the other voices of the trumpet of the three angels, who are yet to sound.
