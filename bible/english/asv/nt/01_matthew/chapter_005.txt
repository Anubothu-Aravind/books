[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 5 | Verse 29] And if thy right eye causeth thee to stumble, pluck it out, and cast it from thee: for it is profitable for thee that one of thy members should perish, and not thy whole body be cast into hell.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 5 | Verse 30] And if thy right hand causeth thee to stumble, cut it off, and cast it from thee: for it is profitable for thee that one of thy members should perish, and not thy whole body be cast into hell.
