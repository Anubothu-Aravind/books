[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 1] Then spake Jesus to the multitudes and to his disciples,
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 2] saying, The scribes and the Pharisees sit on Moses’ seat:
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 3] all things therefore whatsoever they bid you observe, these observe and do: but do not ye after their works; for they say, and do not.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 4] For they bind heavy burdens and grievous to be borne, and lay them on men’s shoulders; but they will not move them with their finger.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 5] But all their works they do to be seen of men: and they make broad their phylacteries, and enlarge the borders of their garments,
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 6] and love the chief place at feasts, and the chief seats in the synagogues,
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 7] and the salutations in the marketplaces, and to be called of men, Rabbi, Rabbi.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 8] But be not ye called Rabbi: for one is your master, even Christ, and all ye are brethren.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 9] And call no man your father on the earth: for one is your Father, even he who is in heaven.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 10] Neither be ye called masters: for one is your master, even the Christ.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 11] But he that is greatest among you shall be your servant.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 12] And whosoever shall exalt himself shall be humbled; and whosoever shall humble himself shall be exalted.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 13] But woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye devour widows’ houses, even while for a pretense ye make long prayers: therefore ye shall receive greater condemnation.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 14] Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye shut the kingdom of heaven against men: for ye enter not in yourselves, neither suffer ye them that are entering in to enter.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 15] Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye compass sea and land to make one proselyte; and when he is become so, ye make him twofold more a son of hell than yourselves.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 16] Woe unto you, ye blind guides, that say, Whosoever shall swear by the temple, it is nothing; but whosoever shall swear by the gold of the temple, he is a debtor.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 17] Ye fools and blind: for which is greater, the gold, or the temple that sanctifieth the gold?
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 18] And, Whosoever shall swear by the altar, it is nothing; but whosoever shall swear by the gift that is upon it, he is a debtor.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 19] Ye fools and blind: for which is greater, the gift, or the altar that sanctifieth the gift?
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 20] He therefore that sweareth by the altar, sweareth by it, and by all things thereon.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 21] And he that sweareth by the temple, sweareth by it, and by him that hath dwelt therein.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 22] And he that sweareth by the heaven, sweareth by the throne of God, and by him that sitteth thereon.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 23] Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye tithe mint and anise and cumin, and have left undone the weightier matters of the law, justice, and mercy, and faith: these ye ought to have done, and not to leave the other undone.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 24] Ye blind guides, that strain out the gnat, and swallow the camel!
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 25] Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye cleanse the outside of the cup and of the platter, but within they are full from extortion and unrighteousness.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 26] Thou blind Pharisee, cleanse first the inside of the cup and of the platter, that the outside of them may become clean also.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 27] Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye are like unto whited sepulchers, which outwardly appear beautiful, but inwardly are full of dead men’s bones, and of all uncleanness.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 28] Even so ye also outwardly appear righteous unto men, but inwardly ye are full of hypocrisy and iniquity.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 29] Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye build the sepulchers of the prophets, and garnish the tombs of the righteous,
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 30] and say, If we had been in the days of our fathers, we should not have been partakers with them in the blood of the prophets.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 31] Wherefore ye witness to yourselves, that ye are sons of them that slew the prophets.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 32] Fill ye up then the measure of your fathers.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 33] Ye serpents, ye offspring of vipers, how shall ye escape the judgment of hell?
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 34] Therefore, behold, I send unto you prophets, and wise men, and scribes: and some of them shall ye kill and crucify; and some of them shall ye scourge in your synagogues, and persecute from city to city:
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 35] that upon you may come all the righteous blood shed on the earth, from the blood of Abel the righteous unto the blood of Zechariah son of Barachiah, whom ye slew between the sanctuary and the altar.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 36] Verily I say unto you, All these things shall come upon this generation.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 37] O Jerusalem, Jerusalem, that killeth the prophets, and stoneth them that are sent unto her! how often would I have gathered thy children together, even as a hen gathereth her chicks under her wings, and ye would not!
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 38] Behold, your house is left unto you desolate.
[ASV | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 23 | Verse 39] For I say unto you, Ye shall not see me henceforth, till ye shall say, Blessed is he that cometh in the name of the Lord.
