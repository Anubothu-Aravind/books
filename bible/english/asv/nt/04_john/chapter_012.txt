[ASV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 12 | Verse 8] For the poor ye have always with you; but me ye have not always.

[ASV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 12 | Verse 14] And Jesus, having found a young ass, sat thereon; as it is written,

[ASV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 12 | Verse 24] Verily, verily, I say unto you, Except a grain of wheat fall into the earth and die, it abideth by itself alone; but if it die, it beareth much fruit.

[ASV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 12 | Verse 26] If any man serve me, let him follow me; and where I am, there shall also my servant be: and if any man serve me, him will the Father honor.
