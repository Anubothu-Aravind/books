[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 33 | Verse 9] Some of them he exalted and hallowed, And some of them hath he made ordinary days.
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 33 | Verse 10] And all men are from the ground, And Adam was created of earth.

[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 33 | Verse 12] Some of them he blessed and exalted, And some of them he hallowed and brought nigh to himself: Some of them he cursed and brought low, And overthrew them from their place.

[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 33 | Verse 15] And thus look upon all the works of the Most High; Two and two, one against another.

[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 33 | Verse 18] Hear me, ye great men of the people, And hearken with your ears, ye rulers of the congregation.
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 33 | Verse 19] To son and wife, to brother and friend, Give not power over thee while thou livest; And give not thy goods to another, Lest thou repent and make supplication for them again.

[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 33 | Verse 21] For better it is that thy children should supplicate thee, Than that thou shouldest look to the hand of thy sons.
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 33 | Verse 22] In all thy works keep the upper hand; Bring not a stain on thine honor.
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 33 | Verse 23] In the day that thou endest the days of thy life, And in the time of death, distribute thine inheritance.

[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 33 | Verse 26] Yoke and thong will bow the neck: And for an evil servant there are racks and tortures.

[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 33 | Verse 28] Set him to work, as is fit for him; And if he obey not, make his fetters heavy.
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 33 | Verse 29] And be not excessive toward any; And without judgment do nothing.
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 33 | Verse 30] If thou hast a servant, let him be as thyself, Because thou hast bought him with blood.
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 33 | Verse 31] If thou hast a servant, treat him as thyself; For as thine own soul wilt thou have need of him: If thou treat him ill, and he depart and run away, Which way wilt thou go to seek him?
