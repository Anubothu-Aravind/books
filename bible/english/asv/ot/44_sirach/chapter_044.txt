[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 44 | Verse 1] Let us now praise famous men, And our fathers that begat us.
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 44 | Verse 2] The Lord manifested in them great glory, Even his mighty power from the beginning.
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 44 | Verse 3] Such as did bear rule in their kingdoms, And were men renowned for their power, Giving counsel by their understanding, Such as have brought tidings in prophecies:
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 44 | Verse 4] Leaders of the people by their counsels, And by their understanding men of learning for the people; Wise were their words in their instruction:
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 44 | Verse 5] Such as sought out musical tunes, And set forth verses in writing:
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 44 | Verse 6] Rich men furnished with ability, Living peaceably in their habitations:
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 44 | Verse 7] All these were honored in their generations, And were a glory in their days.
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 44 | Verse 8] There are of them, that have left a name behind them, To declare their praises.
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 44 | Verse 9] And some there are, who have no memorial; Who are perished as though they had not been, And are become as though they had not been born; And their children after them.
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 44 | Verse 10] But these were men of mercy, Whose righteous deeds have not been forgotten.
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 44 | Verse 11] With their seed shall remain continually a good inheritance; Their children are within the covenants.
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 44 | Verse 12] Their seed standeth fast, And their children for their sakes.
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 44 | Verse 13] Their seed shall remain forever, And their glory shall not be blotted out.
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 44 | Verse 14] Their bodies were buried in peace, And their name liveth to all generations.
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 44 | Verse 15] Peoples will declare their wisdom, And the congregation telleth out their praise.
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 44 | Verse 16] Enoch pleased the Lord, and was translated, Being an example of repentance to all generations.
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 44 | Verse 17] Noah was found perfect and righteous; In the season of wrath he was taken in exchange for the world; Therefore was there left a remnant unto the earth, When the flood came.
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 44 | Verse 18] Everlasting covenants were made with him, That all flesh should no more be blotted out by a flood.
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 44 | Verse 19] Abraham was a great father of a multitude of nations; And there was none found like him in glory;
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 44 | Verse 20] Who kept the law of the Most High, And was taken into covenant with him: In his flesh he established the covenant; And when he was proved, he was found faithful.
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 44 | Verse 21] Therefore he assured him by an oath, That the nations should be blessed in his seed; That he would multiply him as the dust of the earth, And exalt his seed as the stars, And cause them to inherit from sea to sea, And from the River unto the utmost part of the earth.
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 44 | Verse 22] In Isaac also did he establish likewise, for Abraham his father’s sake, The blessing of all men, and the covenant:
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 44 | Verse 23] And he made it rest upon the head of Jacob; He acknowledged him in his blessings, And gave to him by inheritance, And divided his portions; Among twelve tribes did he part them.
