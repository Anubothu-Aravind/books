[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 22 | Verse 14] What shall be heavier than lead? And what is the name thereof, but a fool?

[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 22 | Verse 19] He that pricketh the eye will make tears to fall; And he that pricketh the heart maketh it to show feeling.

[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 22 | Verse 21] If thou hast drawn a sword against a friend, despair not; For there may be a returning.

[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 22 | Verse 25] I will not be ashamed to shelter a friend; And I will not hide myself from his face:
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 22 | Verse 26] And if any evil happen unto me because of him, Everyone that heareth it will beware of him.
[ASV | EN | OT | Sirach | Chapter 22 | Verse 27] Who shall set a watch over my mouth, And a seal of shrewdness upon my lips, That I fall not from it, and that my tongue destroy me not?
