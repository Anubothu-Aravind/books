[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 3] and say, Thus saith the Lord Jehovah unto Jerusalem: Thy birth and thy nativity is of the land of the Canaanite; the Amorite was thy father, and thy mother was a Hittite.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 4] And as for thy nativity, in the day thou wast born thy navel was not cut, neither wast thou washed in water to cleanse thee; thou wast not salted at all, nor swaddled at all.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 5] No eye pitied thee, to do any of these things unto thee, to have compassion upon thee; but thou wast cast out in the open field, for that thy person was abhorred, in the day that thou wast born.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 6] And when I passed by thee, and saw thee weltering in thy blood, I said unto thee, Though thou art in thy blood, live; yea, I said unto thee, Though thou art in thy blood, live.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 7] I caused thee to multiply as that which groweth in the field, and thou didst increase and wax great, and thou attainedst to excellent ornament; thy breasts were fashioned, and thy hair was grown; yet thou wast naked and bare.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 8] Now when I passed by thee, and looked upon thee, behold, thy time was the time of love; and I spread my skirt over thee, and covered thy nakedness: yea, I sware unto thee, and entered into a covenant with thee, saith the Lord Jehovah, and thou becamest mine.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 9] Then washed I thee with water; yea, I thoroughly washed away thy blood from thee, and I anointed thee with oil.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 10] I clothed thee also with broidered work, and shod thee with sealskin, and I girded thee about with fine linen, and covered thee with silk.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 11] And I decked thee with ornaments, and I put bracelets upon thy hands, and a chain on thy neck.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 12] And I put a ring upon thy nose, and earrings in thine ears, and a beautiful crown upon thy head.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 13] Thus wast thou decked with gold and silver; and thy raiment was of fine linen, and silk, and broidered work; thou didst eat fine flour, and honey, and oil; and thou wast exceeding beautiful, and thou didst prosper unto royal estate.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 14] And thy renown went forth among the nations for thy beauty; for it was perfect, through my majesty which I had put upon thee, saith the Lord Jehovah.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 15] But thou didst trust in thy beauty, and playedst the harlot because of thy renown, and pouredst out thy whoredoms on everyone that passed by; his it was.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 16] And thou didst take of thy garments, and madest for thee high places decked with diverse colors, and playedst the harlot upon them: the like things shall not come, neither shall it be so.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 17] Thou didst also take thy fair jewels of my gold and of my silver, which I had given thee, and madest for thee images of men, and didst play the harlot with them;
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 18] and thou tookest thy broidered garments, and coveredst them, and didst set mine oil and mine incense before them.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 19] My bread also which I gave thee, fine flour, and oil, and honey, wherewith I fed thee, thou didst even set it before them for a sweet savor; and thus it was, saith the Lord Jehovah.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 20] Moreover thou hast taken thy sons and thy daughters, whom thou hast borne unto me, and these hast thou sacrificed unto them to be devoured. Were thy whoredoms a small matter,
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 21] that thou hast slain my children, and delivered them up, in causing them to pass through the fire unto them?
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 22] And in all thine abominations and thy whoredoms thou hast not remembered the days of thy youth, when thou wast naked and bare, and wast weltering in thy blood.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 23] And it is come to pass after all thy wickedness (woe, woe unto thee! saith the Lord Jehovah),
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 24] that thou hast built unto thee a vaulted place, and hast made thee a lofty place in every street.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 25] Thou hast built thy lofty place at the head of every way, and hast made thy beauty an abomination, and hast opened thy feet to everyone that passed by, and multiplied thy whoredom.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 26] Thou hast also committed fornication with the Egyptians, thy neighbors, great of flesh; and hast multiplied thy whoredom, to provoke me to anger.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 27] Behold therefore, I have stretched out my hand over thee, and have diminished thine ordinary food, and delivered thee unto the will of them that hate thee, the daughters of the Philistines, that are ashamed of thy lewd way.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 28] Thou hast played the harlot also with the Assyrians, because thou wast insatiable; yea, thou hast played the harlot with them, and yet thou wast not satisfied.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 29] Thou hast moreover multiplied thy whoredom unto the land of traffic, unto Chaldea; and yet thou wast not satisfied herewith.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 30] How weak is thy heart, saith the Lord Jehovah, seeing thou doest all these things, the work of an impudent harlot;
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 31] in that thou buildest thy vaulted place at the head of every way, and makest thy lofty place in every street, and hast not been as a harlot, in that thou scornest hire.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 32] A wife that committeth adultery! that taketh strangers instead of her husband!
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 33] They give gifts to all harlots; but thou givest thy gifts to all thy lovers, and bribest them, that they may come unto thee on every side for thy whoredoms.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 34] And thou art different from other women in thy whoredoms, in that none followeth thee to play the harlot; and whereas thou givest hire, and no hire is given unto thee, therefore thou art different.

[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 37] therefore behold, I will gather all thy lovers, with whom thou hast taken pleasure, and all them that thou hast loved, with all them that thou hast hated; I will even gather them against thee on every side, and will uncover thy nakedness unto them, that they may see all thy nakedness.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 38] And I will judge thee, as women that break wedlock and shed blood are judged; and I will bring upon thee the blood of wrath and jealousy.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 39] I will also give thee into their hand, and they shall throw down thy vaulted place, and break down thy lofty places; and they shall strip thee of thy clothes, and take thy fair jewels; and they shall leave thee naked and bare.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 40] They shall also bring up a company against thee, and they shall stone thee with stones, and thrust thee through with their swords.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 41] And they shall burn thy houses with fire, and execute judgments upon thee in the sight of many women; and I will cause thee to cease from playing the harlot, and thou shalt also give no hire anymore.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 42] So will I cause my wrath toward thee to rest, and my jealousy shall depart from thee, and I will be quiet, and will be no more angry.
[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 43] Because thou hast not remembered the days of thy youth, but hast raged against me in all these things; therefore, behold, I also will bring thy way upon thy head, saith the Lord Jehovah: and thou shalt not commit this lewdness with all thine abominations.

[ASV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 53] And I will turn again their captivity, the captivity of Sodom and her daughters, and the captivity of Samaria and her daughters, and the captivity of thy captives in the midst of them;
