[ASV | EN | OT | Wisdom of Solomon | Chapter 4 | Verse 1] Better than this is childishness with virtue; For in the memory of virtue is immortality: Because it is recognized both before God and before men.
[ASV | EN | OT | Wisdom of Solomon | Chapter 4 | Verse 2] When it is present, men imitate it; And they long after it when it is departed: And throughout all time it marcheth crowned in triumph, Victorious in the strife for the prizes that are undefiled.
[ASV | EN | OT | Wisdom of Solomon | Chapter 4 | Verse 3] But the multiplying brood of the ungodly shall be of no profit, And with bastard slips they shall not strike deep root, Nor shall they establish a sure hold.
[ASV | EN | OT | Wisdom of Solomon | Chapter 4 | Verse 4] For even if these put forth boughs and flourish for a season, Yet, standing unsure, they shall be shaken by the wind, And by the violence of winds they shall be rooted out.
[ASV | EN | OT | Wisdom of Solomon | Chapter 4 | Verse 5] Their branches shall be broken off before they come to maturity, And their fruit shall be useless, Never ripe to eat, and fit for nothing.
[ASV | EN | OT | Wisdom of Solomon | Chapter 4 | Verse 6] For children unlawfully begotten are witnesses of wickedness Against parents when God searcheth them out.
[ASV | EN | OT | Wisdom of Solomon | Chapter 4 | Verse 7] But a righteous man, though he die before his time, shall be at rest.
[ASV | EN | OT | Wisdom of Solomon | Chapter 4 | Verse 8] (For honorable old age is not that which standeth in length of time, Nor is its measure given by number of years:
[ASV | EN | OT | Wisdom of Solomon | Chapter 4 | Verse 9] But understanding is gray hairs unto men, And an unspotted life is ripe old age.)
[ASV | EN | OT | Wisdom of Solomon | Chapter 4 | Verse 10] Being found well-pleasing unto God he was beloved of him, And while living among sinners he was translated:
[ASV | EN | OT | Wisdom of Solomon | Chapter 4 | Verse 11] He was caught away, lest wickedness should change his understanding, Or guile deceive his soul.
[ASV | EN | OT | Wisdom of Solomon | Chapter 4 | Verse 12] (For the bewitching of naughtiness obscureth the things which are good, And the giddy whirl of desire perverteth an innocent mind.)
[ASV | EN | OT | Wisdom of Solomon | Chapter 4 | Verse 13] Being made perfect in a little while, he fulfilled long years;
[ASV | EN | OT | Wisdom of Solomon | Chapter 4 | Verse 14] For his soul was pleasing unto the Lord: Therefore hasted he out of the midst of wickedness.
[ASV | EN | OT | Wisdom of Solomon | Chapter 4 | Verse 15] But as for the peoples, seeing and understanding not, Neither laying this to heart, That grace and mercy are with his chosen, And that he visiteth his holy ones—
[ASV | EN | OT | Wisdom of Solomon | Chapter 4 | Verse 16] But a righteous man that is dead shall condemn the ungodly that are living, And youth that is quickly perfected the many years of an unrighteous man’s old age;
[ASV | EN | OT | Wisdom of Solomon | Chapter 4 | Verse 17] For the ungodly shall see a wise man’s end, And shall not understand what the Lord purposed concerning him, And for what he safely kept him—
[ASV | EN | OT | Wisdom of Solomon | Chapter 4 | Verse 18] They shall see, and they shall despise; But them the Lord shall laugh to scorn. And after this they shall become a dishonored carcass, And a reproach among the dead forever:
[ASV | EN | OT | Wisdom of Solomon | Chapter 4 | Verse 19] Because he shall dash them speechless to the ground, And shall shake them from the foundations, And they shall lie utterly waste, and they shall be in anguish, And their memory shall perish.
[ASV | EN | OT | Wisdom of Solomon | Chapter 4 | Verse 20] They shall come, when their sins are reckoned up, with coward fear; And their lawless deeds shall convict them to their face.
