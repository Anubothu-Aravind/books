[ASV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 98 | Verse 1] Oh sing unto Jehovah a new song; For he hath done marvelous things: His right hand, and his holy arm, hath wrought salvation for him.
[ASV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 98 | Verse 2] Jehovah hath made known his salvation: His righteousness hath he openly shown in the sight of the nations.
[ASV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 98 | Verse 3] He hath remembered his lovingkindness and his faithfulness toward the house of Israel: All the ends of the earth have seen the salvation of our God.
[ASV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 98 | Verse 4] Make a joyful noise unto Jehovah, all the earth: Break forth and sing for joy, yea, sing praises.
[ASV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 98 | Verse 5] Sing praises unto Jehovah with the harp; With the harp and the voice of melody.
[ASV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 98 | Verse 6] With trumpets and sound of cornet Make a joyful noise before the King, Jehovah.
[ASV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 98 | Verse 7] Let the sea roar, and the fullness thereof; The world, and they that dwell therein;
[ASV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 98 | Verse 8] Let the floods clap their hands; Let the hills sing for joy together
[ASV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 98 | Verse 9] Before Jehovah; for he cometh to judge the earth: He will judge the world with righteousness, And the peoples with equity.
