[ASV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 137 | Verse 7] Remember, O Jehovah, against the children of Edom The day of Jerusalem; Who said, Raze it, raze it, Even to the foundation thereof.
[ASV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 137 | Verse 8] O daughter of Babylon, that art to be destroyed, Happy shall he be, that rewardeth thee As thou hast served us.
