[ASV | EN | OT | 3 Maccabees | Chapter 7 | Verse 1] King Ptolemy Philopator to the commanders throughout Egypt, and to all who are set over affairs, joy and strength.
[ASV | EN | OT | 3 Maccabees | Chapter 7 | Verse 2] We, too, and our children are well; and God hath directed our affairs as we wish.
[ASV | EN | OT | 3 Maccabees | Chapter 7 | Verse 3] Certain of our friends did of malice vehemently urge us to punish the Jews of our realm in a body, with the infliction of a monstrous punishment.
[ASV | EN | OT | 3 Maccabees | Chapter 7 | Verse 4] They pretended that our affairs would never be in a good state till this took place. Such, they said, was the hatred borne by the Jews to all other people.
[ASV | EN | OT | 3 Maccabees | Chapter 7 | Verse 5] They brought them fettered in grievous chains as slaves, nay, as traitors. Without inquiry or examination they endeavored to annihilate them. They buckled themselves with a savage cruelty, worse than Scythian custom.
[ASV | EN | OT | 3 Maccabees | Chapter 7 | Verse 6] For this cause we severely threatened them; yet, with the clemency which we are wont to extend to all men, we at length permitted them to live. Finding that the God of heaven cast a shield of protection over the Jews so as to preserve them, and that he fought for them as a father always fighteth for his sons;
[ASV | EN | OT | 3 Maccabees | Chapter 7 | Verse 7] and taking into consideration their constancy and fidelity toward us and toward our ancestors, we have, as we ought, acquitted them of every sort of charge.
[ASV | EN | OT | 3 Maccabees | Chapter 7 | Verse 8] And we have dismissed them to their several homes; bidding all men everywhere to do them no wrong, or unrighteously revile them about the past.
[ASV | EN | OT | 3 Maccabees | Chapter 7 | Verse 9] For know ye, that should we conceive any evil design against, or in any manner aggrieve them, we shall ever have as our opposite, not man, but the highest God, the ruler of all might. From him there will be no escape, as the avenger of such deeds. Fare ye well.
[ASV | EN | OT | 3 Maccabees | Chapter 7 | Verse 10] When they had received this letter, they were not forward to depart immediately. They petitioned the king to be allowed to inflict fitting punishment upon those of their race who had willingly transgressed the holy God, and the law of God.
[ASV | EN | OT | 3 Maccabees | Chapter 7 | Verse 11] They alleged that men who had for their bellies’ sake transgressed the ordinances of God, would never be faithful to the interests of the king.
[ASV | EN | OT | 3 Maccabees | Chapter 7 | Verse 12] The king admitted the truth of this reasoning, and commended them. Full power was given them, without warrant or special commission, to destroy those who had transgressed the law of God boldly in every part of the king’s dominions.
[ASV | EN | OT | 3 Maccabees | Chapter 7 | Verse 13] Their priests, then, as it was meet, saluted him with good wishes, and all the people echoed with the Hallelujah. They then joyfully departed.
[ASV | EN | OT | 3 Maccabees | Chapter 7 | Verse 14] Then they punished and destroyed with ignominy every polluted Jew that fell in their way;
[ASV | EN | OT | 3 Maccabees | Chapter 7 | Verse 15] slaying thus, in that day, above three hundred men, and esteeming this destruction of the wicked a season of joy.
[ASV | EN | OT | 3 Maccabees | Chapter 7 | Verse 16] They themselves having held fast their God unto death, and having enjoyed a full deliverance, departed from the city garlanded with sweet-flowered wreaths of every kind. Uttering exclamations of joy, with songs of praise, and melodious hymns they thanked the God of their fathers, the eternal Savior of Israel.
[ASV | EN | OT | 3 Maccabees | Chapter 7 | Verse 17] Having arrived at Ptolemais, called from the specialty of that district Rose-bearing, where the fleet, in accordance with the general wish, waited for them seven days,
[ASV | EN | OT | 3 Maccabees | Chapter 7 | Verse 18] they partook of a banquet of deliverance, for the king generously granted them severally the means of securing a return home.
[ASV | EN | OT | 3 Maccabees | Chapter 7 | Verse 19] They were accordingly brought back in peace, while they gave utterance to becoming thanks; and they determined to keep these days during their sojourn as days of joyfulness.
[ASV | EN | OT | 3 Maccabees | Chapter 7 | Verse 20] These they registered as sacred upon a pillar, when they had dedicated the place of their festivity to be one of prayer. They departed unharmed, free, abundant in joy, preserved by the king’s command, by land, by sea, and by river, each to his own home.
[ASV | EN | OT | 3 Maccabees | Chapter 7 | Verse 21] They had more weight than before among their enemies; and were honored and feared, and no one in any way robbed them of their goods.
[ASV | EN | OT | 3 Maccabees | Chapter 7 | Verse 22] Every man received back his own, according to inventory; those who had obtained their goods, giving them up with the greatest terror. For the greatest God wrought with perfectness wonders for their salvation.
[ASV | EN | OT | 3 Maccabees | Chapter 7 | Verse 23] Blessed be the Redeemer of Israel unto everlasting. Amen.
