[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 47 | Verse 1] The word of Jehovah that came to Jeremiah the prophet concerning the Philistines, before that Pharaoh smote Gaza.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 47 | Verse 2] Thus saith Jehovah: Behold, waters rise up out of the north, and shall become an overflowing stream, and shall overflow the land and all that is therein, the city and them that dwell therein; and the men shall cry, and all the inhabitants of the land shall wail.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 47 | Verse 3] At the noise of the stamping of the hoofs of his strong ones, at the rushing of his chariots, at the rumbling of his wheels, the fathers look not back to their children for feebleness of hands;

[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 47 | Verse 6] O thou sword of Jehovah, how long will it be ere thou be quiet? put up thyself into thy scabbard; rest, and be still.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 47 | Verse 7] How canst thou be quiet, seeing Jehovah hath given thee a charge? Against Ashkelon, and against the seashore, there hath he appointed it.
