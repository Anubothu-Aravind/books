[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 1] Of the children of Ammon. Thus saith Jehovah: Hath Israel no sons? hath he no heir? why then doth Malcam possess Gad, and his people dwell in the cities thereof?
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 2] Therefore, behold, the days come, saith Jehovah, that I will cause an alarm of war to be heard against Rabbah of the children of Ammon; and it shall become a desolate heap, and her daughters shall be burned with fire: then shall Israel possess them that did possess him, saith Jehovah.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 3] Wail, O Heshbon, for Ai is laid waste; cry, ye daughters of Rabbah, gird you with sackcloth: lament, and run to and fro among the fences; for Malcam shall go into captivity, his priests and his princes together.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 4] Wherefore gloriest thou in the valleys, thy flowing valley, O backsliding daughter? that trusted in her treasures, saying, Who shall come unto me?
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 5] Behold, I will bring a fear upon thee, saith the Lord, Jehovah of hosts, from all that are round about thee; and ye shall be driven out every man right forth, and there shall be none to gather together the fugitives.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 6] But afterward I will bring back the captivity of the children of Ammon, saith Jehovah.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 7] Of Edom. Thus saith Jehovah of hosts: Is wisdom no more in Teman? is counsel perished from the prudent? is their wisdom vanished?
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 8] Flee ye, turn back, dwell in the depths, O inhabitants of Dedan; for I will bring the calamity of Esau upon him, the time that I shall visit him.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 9] If grape-gatherers came to thee, would they not leave some gleaning grapes? if thieves by night, would they not destroy till they had enough?
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 10] But I have made Esau bare, I have uncovered his secret places, and he shall not be able to hide himself: his seed is destroyed, and his brethren, and his neighbors; and he is not.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 11] Leave thy fatherless children, I will preserve them alive; and let thy widows trust in me.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 12] For thus saith Jehovah: Behold, they to whom it pertained not to drink of the cup shall assuredly drink; and art thou he that shall altogether go unpunished? thou shalt not go unpunished, but thou shalt surely drink.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 13] For I have sworn by myself, saith Jehovah, that Bozrah shall become an astonishment, a reproach, a waste, and a curse; and all the cities thereof shall be perpetual wastes.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 14] I have heard tidings from Jehovah, and an ambassador is sent among the nations, saying, Gather yourselves together, and come against her, and rise up to the battle.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 15] For, behold, I have made thee small among the nations, and despised among men.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 16] As for thy terribleness, the pride of thy heart hath deceived thee, O thou that dwellest in the clefts of the rock, that holdest the height of the hill: though thou shouldest make thy nest as high as the eagle, I will bring thee down from thence, saith Jehovah.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 17] And Edom shall become an astonishment: everyone that passeth by it shall be astonished, and shall hiss at all the plagues thereof.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 18] As in the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah and the neighbor cities thereof, saith Jehovah, no man shall dwell there, neither shall any son of man sojourn therein.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 19] Behold, he shall come up like a lion from the pride of the Jordan against the strong habitation: for I will suddenly make them run away from it; and whoso is chosen, him will I appoint over it: for who is like me? and who will appoint me a time? and who is the shepherd that will stand before me?
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 20] Therefore hear ye the counsel of Jehovah, that he hath taken against Edom; and his purposes, that he hath purposed against the inhabitants of Teman: Surely they shall drag them away, even the little ones of the flock; surely he shall make their habitation desolate over them.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 21] The earth trembleth at the noise of their fall; there is a cry, the noise whereof is heard in the Red Sea.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 22] Behold, he shall come up and fly as the eagle, and spread out his wings against Bozrah: and the heart of the mighty men of Edom at that day shall be as the heart of a woman in her pangs.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 23] Of Damascus. Hamath is confounded, and Arpad; for they have heard evil tidings, they are melted away: there is sorrow on the sea; it cannot be quiet.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 24] Damascus is waxed feeble, she turneth herself to flee, and trembling hath seized on her: anguish and sorrows have taken hold of her, as of a woman in travail.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 25] How is the city of praise not forsaken, the city of my joy?
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 26] Therefore her young men shall fall in her streets, and all the men of war shall be brought to silence in that day, saith Jehovah of hosts.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 27] And I will kindle a fire in the wall of Damascus, and it shall devour the palaces of Ben-hadad.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 28] Of Kedar, and of the kingdoms of Hazor, which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon smote. Thus saith Jehovah: Arise ye, go up to Kedar, and destroy the children of the east.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 29] Their tents and their flocks shall they take; they shall carry away for themselves their curtains, and all their vessels, and their camels; and they shall cry unto them, Terror on every side!
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 30] Flee ye, wander far off, dwell in the depths, O ye inhabitants of Hazor, saith Jehovah; for Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon hath taken counsel against you, and hath conceived a purpose against you.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 31] Arise, get you up unto a nation that is at ease, that dwelleth without care, saith Jehovah; that have neither gates nor bars, that dwell alone.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 32] And their camels shall be a booty, and the multitude of their cattle a spoil: and I will scatter unto all winds them that have the corners of their hair cut off; and I will bring their calamity from every side of them, saith Jehovah.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 33] And Hazor shall be a dwelling place of jackals, a desolation forever: no man shall dwell there, neither shall any son of man sojourn therein.

[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 36] And upon Elam will I bring the four winds from the four quarters of heaven, and will scatter them toward all those winds; and there shall be no nation whither the outcasts of Elam shall not come.

[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 38] and I will set my throne in Elam, and will destroy from thence king and princes, saith Jehovah.
[ASV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 49 | Verse 39] But it shall come to pass in the latter days, that I will bring back the captivity of Elam, saith Jehovah.
