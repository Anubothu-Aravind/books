[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 149 | Verse 7] on the heathen to execute vengeance, and chastisement sore on the nations,
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 149 | Verse 8] binding their kings with chains, and their nobles with fetters of iron,
