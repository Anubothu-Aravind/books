[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 138 | Verse 1] I will thank you, O Lord, with all my heart: in the sight of the gods I will sing your praise,
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 138 | Verse 2] and prostrate before your holy temple, will praise your name for your constant love, for you have exulted your promise above all.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 138 | Verse 3] When I called you, you answered; you gave me strength, you inspired me.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 138 | Verse 4] All the kings of the earth shall praise you, O Lord, when they shall have heard the words you have uttered;
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 138 | Verse 5] and they shall sing of the ways of the Lord, and tell of the Lord’s transcendent glory.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 138 | Verse 6] For, high though the Lord is, he looks on the lowly, and strikes down the haughty from far away.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 138 | Verse 7] Though my way be distressful, yet you preserve me: you lay your hand on my angry foes, and your right hand gives me victory.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 138 | Verse 8] The Lord will accomplish all that which concerns me. Your kindness, O Lord, endures forever. O do not abandon the work of your hands.
