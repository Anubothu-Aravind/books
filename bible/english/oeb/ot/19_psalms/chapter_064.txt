[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 1] Hear, O my God, the voice of my lament: guard my life from the dread of the foe.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 2] Hide me from villains who secretly plot, from the blustering throng of the workers of evil,
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 3] who have sharpened their tongue like a sword, and aimed bitter words like arrows,
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 4] which from ambush they launch at the blameless, shooting swiftly and unafraid.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 5] They strengthen their wicked purpose, they tell of the snares they have hidden, they say to themselves, ‘Who can see?’
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 6] They think out their crimes full cunningly hidden deep in their crafty hearts.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 7] But God with his arrow will shoot them, swiftly shall they be smitten.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 8] For their tongue he will bring them to ruin, all will shudder with horror at the sight of them.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 9] Then every person, touched to awe, as they ponder what God has wrought, will tell the tale of his deeds.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 10] In the Lord shall the righteous rejoice, in him shall they take refuge; and all the true-hearted shall glory.
