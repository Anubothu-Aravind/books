[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 1] Why glory in mischief, you hero? God’s kindness is all the day.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 2] Engulfing ruin you plot, your tongue like a razor sharpened, you practiser of deceit.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 3] Evil, not good, you love, and falsehood, not words of truth. Selah
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 4] But you love all words that devour, and a tongue that is given to deceit.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 5] But God, on his part, shall destroy you forever, grasp you and pluck you out of your tent, and root you out of the land of the living. Selah
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 6] Smitten with awe at the sight, the righteous shall laugh at you.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 7] ‘Look’ (they will say) ‘at the hero who did not make God his stronghold, but trusted in his great wealth and in the strength of his riches.’
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 8] But I am like a fresh olive-tree in the house of God. I trust in the kindness of God for ever and evermore.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 9] I will render you thanks for ever for what you have done. I will tell how good you are in the presence of those who love you.
