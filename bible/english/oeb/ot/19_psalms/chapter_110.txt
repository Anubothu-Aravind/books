[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 110 | Verse 1] This said the Lord concerning my lord, ‘Sit at my right hand, till I set your foot on the neck of your foes.’
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 110 | Verse 2] On Zion the Lord is wielding your sceptre of might, and charges you to rule over the foes that surround you.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 110 | Verse 3] The day that you march to battle your people will follow you gladly young warriors in holy array, like dew-drops, born of the morning.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 110 | Verse 4] The Lord has sworn and will not repent, ‘As for you, you are priest for ever as Melchizedek was.’
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 110 | Verse 5] By your side will the Lord shatter kings on the day of his wrath.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 110 | Verse 6] He will execute judgment filling the valleys with dead, the broad fields with shattered heads.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 110 | Verse 7] He will drink of the brook by the way, and march onward with uplifted head.
