[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 43 | Verse 1] Right me, defend my cause against a pitiless people. From the crafty and crooked, O God, deliver me.

[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 43 | Verse 3] Send forth your light and your truth, let them be my guides: to your holy hill let them bring me, to the place where you live.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 43 | Verse 4] Then will I go to God’s altar, to God my rejoicing; and with joy on the lyre I will praise you, O God, my God.
