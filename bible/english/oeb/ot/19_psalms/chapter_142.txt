[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 142 | Verse 1] Loudly I cry to the Lord: to the Lord plead loudly for mercy,
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 142 | Verse 2] I pour my complaint before him, I tell my troubles to him.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 142 | Verse 3] When my spirit is faint within me, my path is known to you. In the way I am wont to walk in, they have hidden a trap for me.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 142 | Verse 4] I look to the right and the left; but not a friend have I. No place of refuge is left me, not a man to care for me.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 142 | Verse 5] So I cry to you, O Lord: I say, ‘My refuge are you, all I have in the land of the living.’
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 142 | Verse 6] Attend to my piercing cry, for very weak am I. Save me from those who pursue me, for they are too strong for me.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 142 | Verse 7] Free me from prison, that I may give thanks to your name, for the righteous are patiently waiting till you show your bounty to me.
