[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 101 | Verse 1] If kindness and justice I sing, making melody to you, Lord.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 101 | Verse 2] I would look to the way that is blameless, and make it my own. Within my own house I would walk with an innocent heart.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 101 | Verse 3] I would never direct my eyes to a thing that is base. The impulse to stray I abhor it shall not cling to me.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 101 | Verse 4] Far from me be perverseness of heart, or kinship with evil.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 101 | Verse 5] Who slanders their neighbour in secret, I bring them to silence: haughty looks and proud hearts I will not abide.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 101 | Verse 6] I will favour the true in the land, they shall live in my court. Those who walk in a way that is blameless will be my attendant.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 101 | Verse 7] No one will live in my house who practises guile. No one that speaks a lie will abide in my presence.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 101 | Verse 8] Morn by morn I will wholly wipe out all the bad in the land, and cut off from the Lord’s own city all workers of evil.
