[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 112 | Verse 1] Hallelujah. Happy are those who fear the Lord, and greatly delight in his commandments.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 112 | Verse 2] Mighty on earth shall be their seed; a blessing shall rest on the race of the upright.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 112 | Verse 3] Wealth and riches are in their houses, their prosperity stands forever.

[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 112 | Verse 6] For they will never be shaken; the just will be forever remembered.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 112 | Verse 7] They will not be afraid of evil tidings, with steady heart they trust the Lord.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 112 | Verse 8] Their heart is firm and unafraid: they know they will feast their eyes on their enemies.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 112 | Verse 9] With lavish hands they give to the poor, and their prosperity stands forever. They are lifted to heights of triumph and honour.
