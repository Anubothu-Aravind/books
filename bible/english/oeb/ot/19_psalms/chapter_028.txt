[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 28 | Verse 1] Unto you, O Lord, do I cry; my rock, be not deaf to me: lest, through holding your peace, I become like those who go down to the pit.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 28 | Verse 2] Hear my loud entreaty, as I cry for help to you, lifting my hands, O Lord, towards your holy chancel.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 28 | Verse 3] Take me not off with the wicked, nor with the workers of wrong, whose speech to their neighbours is friendly, while evil is in their heart.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 28 | Verse 4] Give them as they have done, as their wicked deeds deserve. As their hands have wrought, so give to them: requite to them their deserts.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 28 | Verse 5] They are blind to all that the Lord does, to all that his hands have wrought; and so he will tear them down, to build them up no more.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 28 | Verse 6] Blest be the Lord, who has heard my voice as I plead for mercy.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 28 | Verse 7] The Lord is my strength and my shield; my heart trusts in him. I was helped: so my heart is exultant, and in my song I will praise him.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 28 | Verse 8] The Lord is the strength of his people, the fortress who saves his anointed.
[OEB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 28 | Verse 9] O save your people, and bless your inheritance. Be their shepherd and carry them forever.
