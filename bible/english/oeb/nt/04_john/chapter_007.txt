[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 1] After this, Jesus went about in Galilee, for he would not do so in Judea, because the religious authorities (in Jerusalem) were eager to put him to death.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 2] When the Jewish Festival of Tabernacles was near,
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 3] his brothers said to him, ‘Leave this part of the country, and go into Judea, so that your disciples, as well as we, may see the work that you are doing.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 4] For no one does a thing privately, if they are seeking to be widely known. Since you do these things, you should show yourself publicly to the world.’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 5] For even his brothers did not believe in him.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 6] ‘My time,’ answered Jesus, ‘is not come yet, but your time is always here.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 7] The world cannot hate you, but it does hate me, because I testify that its ways are evil.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 8] Go yourselves up to the Festival; I am not going to this Festival yet, because my time has not yet come.’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 9] After telling them this, he stayed on in Galilee.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 10] But, when his brothers had gone up to the Festival, Jesus also went up – not publicly, but privately.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 11] The authorities were looking for him at the Festival and asking “Where is he?”;
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 12] and there were many whispers about him among the people, some saying “He is a good man;” others, “No! He is leading the people astray.”
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 13] No one, however, spoke freely about him, because they were afraid of the authorities.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 14] About the middle of the Festival week, Jesus went up into the Temple Courts, and began teaching.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 15] The authorities were astonished. ‘How has this man got his learning,’ they asked, ‘when he has never studied?’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 16] So, in reply, Jesus said, ‘My teaching is not my own; it is his who sent me.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 17] If anyone has the will to do God’s will, they will find out whether my teaching is from God, or whether I speak on my own authority.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 18] The person who speaks on their own authority seeks honour for themselves; but the one who seeks the honour of him who sent him is sincere, and there is nothing false in him.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 19] Was not it Moses who gave you the Law? Yet not one of you obeys it! Why are you seeking to put me to death?’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 20] ‘You must be possessed by a demon!’ the people exclaimed. ‘Who is seeking to put you to death?’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 21] ‘There was one thing I did,’ replied Jesus, ‘at which you are all still wondering.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 22] But that is why Moses has instituted circumcision among you – not, indeed, that it began with him, but with our ancestors – and that is why you circumcise even on a Sabbath.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 23] When a man receives circumcision on a Sabbath to prevent the Law of Moses from being broken, how can you be angry with me for making a man sound and well on a Sabbath?
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 24] Do not judge by appearances; judge justly.’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 25] At this some of the people of Jerusalem exclaimed, ‘Is not this the man who they are seeking to put to death?
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 26] Yet here he is, speaking out boldly, and they say nothing to him! Is it possible that our leading men have really discovered that he is the Christ?
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 27] Yet we know where this man is from; but, when the Christ comes, no one will be able to tell where he is from.’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 28] Therefore, Jesus, as he was teaching in the Temple Courts, raised his voice and said, ‘Yes; you know me and you know where I am from. Yet I have not come on my own authority, but he who sent me may be trusted; and him you do not know.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 29] I do know him, for it is from him that I have come, and he sent me as his messenger.’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 30] So they sought to arrest him; but no one touched him, for his time was not come yet.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 31] Many of the people, however, believed in him. ‘When the Christ comes,’ they said, ‘will he give more signs of his mission than this man has given?’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 32] The Pharisees heard the people whispering about him in this way, and so the chief priests and the Pharisees sent officers to arrest him;
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 33] at which Jesus said, ‘I will be with you but a little longer, and then I am going to him who sent me.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 34] You will look for me, and you will not find me; and you will not be able to come where I will be.’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 35] ‘Where is this man going,’ the people asked one another, ‘that we would not find him? Will he go to our countrymen abroad, and teach foreigners?
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 36] What does he mean by saying “You will look for me, and you will not find me; and you will not be able to come where I will be”?’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 37] On the last and greatest day of the Festival, Jesus, who was standing by, exclaimed, ‘If anyone is thirsty, they should come to me and drink.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 38] From the heart of those who believe in me will flow, as is said in scripture, rivers of living water.’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 39] (By this he meant the Spirit, which those who had believed in him were to receive; for the Spirit had not yet come, because Jesus had not yet been exalted.)
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 40] Some of the people, when they heard these words, said, ‘This is certainly the Prophet!’;
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 41] others said, ‘the Christ!’; but some asked, ‘What! Does the Christ come from Galilee?
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 42] Is not it said in scripture that it is of the descendants of David, and from Bethlehem, the village to which David belonged, that the Christ is to come?’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 43] So there was a sharp division among the people because of Jesus.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 44] Some of them wanted to arrest him, and yet no one touched him.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 45] When the officers returned to the chief priests and Pharisees, they were asked, ‘Why have you not brought him?’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 46] ‘No one ever spoke as he speaks!’ they answered.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 47] ‘What! Have you been led astray too?’ the Pharisees replied.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 48] ‘Have any of our leading men believed in him, or any of the Pharisees?
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 49] As for these people who do not know the Law – they are cursed!’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 50] But one of their number, Nicodemus, who before this had been to see Jesus, said to them,
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 51] ‘Does our Law pass judgment on a person without first giving them a hearing, and finding out what they have been doing?’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 52] ‘Are you also from Galilee?’ they retorted. ‘Search, and you will find that no prophet is to arise in Galilee!’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 7 | Verse 53] And everyone went home
