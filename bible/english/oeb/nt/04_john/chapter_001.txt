[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 1] In the beginning the Word was; and the Word was with God; and the Word was God.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 2] He was in the beginning with God;
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 3] through him all things came into being, and nothing came into being apart from him.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 4] That which came into being in him was life; and the life was the light of humanity;
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 5] and the light shines in the darkness, and the darkness never overpowered it.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 6] There appeared a man sent from God, whose name was John;
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 7] he came as a witness – to bear witness to the light so that through him everyone might believe.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 8] He was not the light, but he came to bear witness to the light.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 9] That was the true light which enlightens everyone coming into the world.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 10] He was in the world; and through him the world came into being – yet the world did not know him.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 11] He came to his own – yet his own did not receive him.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 12] But to all who did receive him he gave power to become children of God – to those who believe in his name.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 13] For not to natural conception, nor to human instincts, nor to human will did they owe the new life, but to God.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 14] And the Word became human, and lived among us, (We saw his glory – the glory of the Only Son sent from the Father), full of love and truth.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 15] (John bears witness to him; he cried aloud – for it was he who spoke – ‘He who is coming after me is now before me, for he was ever first’);
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 16] out of his fullness we have all received gift after gift of love;
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 17] for the Law was given through Moses, love and truth came through Jesus Christ.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 18] No one has ever yet seen God; God the only Son, who is ever with the Father – he has revealed him.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 19] When the religious authorities in Jerusalem sent some Priests and Levites to ask John – ‘Who are you?’,
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 20] he told them clearly and simply, ‘I am not the Christ.’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 21] ‘What then?’ they asked. ‘Are you Elijah?’ ‘No,’ he said, ‘I am not.’ ‘Are you “the prophet”?’ He answered, ‘No.’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 22] ‘Who then are you?’ they continued. ‘Tell us so that we have an answer to give to those who have sent us. What do you say about yourself?’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 23] ‘I,’ he answered, ‘am – “The voice of one crying aloud in the wilderness: make a straight road for the Lord”, as the prophet Isaiah said.’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 24] These men had been sent from the Pharisees;
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 25] and their next question was, ‘Why then do you baptize, if you are not the Christ or Elijah or “the prophet”?’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 26] John’s answer was – ‘I baptize with water, but among you stands one whom you do not know;
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 27] he is coming after me, yet I am not worthy even to unfasten his sandal.’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 28] This happened at Bethany, across the Jordan, where John was baptizing.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 29] The next day John saw Jesus coming towards him, and exclaimed, ‘Here is the Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world!
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 30] I was talking about him when I said “After me there is coming a man who ranks ahead of me, because before I was born he already was.”
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 31] I did not know who he was, but I have come baptizing with water to make him known to Israel.’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 32] John also said: ‘I saw the Spirit come down from heaven like a dove and rest on him.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 33] I myself did not know him, but he who sent me to baptize with water, he said to me “He on whom you see the Spirit descending, and remaining on him – he it is who baptizes with the Holy Spirit.”
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 34] This I have seen myself, and I have declared my belief that he is the Son of God.’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 35] The next day, when John was standing with two of his disciples,
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 36] he looked at Jesus as he passed and exclaimed, ‘There is the Lamb of God!’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 37] The two disciples heard him say this, and followed Jesus.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 38] But Jesus turned round, and saw them following. ‘What are you looking for?’ he asked. ‘Rabbi,’ they answered (or, as we should say, “Teacher”), ‘where are you staying?’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 39] ‘Come, and you will see,’ he replied. So they went, and saw where he was staying, and spent that day with him. It was then about four in the afternoon.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 40] One of the two, who heard what John said and followed Jesus, was Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 41] He first found his own brother Simon, and said to him, ‘We have found the Messiah!’ (a word which means “Christ,” or “Consecrated”.)
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 42] Then he brought him to Jesus. Looking straight at him, Jesus said, ‘You are Simon, the son of John; you will be called Cephas’ (which means “Peter,” or “Rock”).
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 43] The following day Jesus decided to leave for Galilee. He found Philip, and said to him, ‘Follow me.’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 44] Philip was from Bethsaida, the same town as Andrew and Peter.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 45] He found Nathanael and said to him, ‘We have found him of whom Moses wrote in the Law, and of whom the prophets also wrote – Jesus of Nazareth, Joseph’s son!’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 46] ‘Can anything good come out of Nazareth?’ asked Nathanael. ‘Come and see,’ replied Philip.
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 47] When Jesus saw Nathanael coming towards him, he said, ‘Here is a true Israelite, in whom there is no deceit!’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 48] ‘How do you know me?’ asked Nathanael. ‘Even before Philip called you,’ replied Jesus, ‘when you were under the fig tree, I saw you.’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 49] ‘Rabbi,’ Nathanael exclaimed, ‘you are the Son of God, you are king of Israel!’
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 50] ‘Do you believe in me,’ asked Jesus, ‘because I told you that I saw you under the fig tree? You will see greater things than those!
[OEB | EN | NT | John | Chapter 1 | Verse 51] In truth I tell you,’ he added, ‘you will all see heaven open, and the angels of God ascending and descending on the Son of Man.’
