[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 1] Next, friends, I would like to remind you of the good news which I told you, and which you received – the good news on which you have taken your stand,
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 2] and by means of which you are being saved. I would like to remind you of the words that I used in telling it to you, since you are still holding fast to it, and since it was not in vain that you became believers in Christ.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 3] For at the very beginning of my teaching I gave you the account which I had myself received – that Christ died for our sins (as the scriptures had foretold),
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 4] that he was buried, that on the third day he was raised (as the scriptures had foretold),
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 5] and that he appeared to Cephas, and then to the Twelve.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 6] After that, he appeared to more than five hundred of his followers at one time, most of whom are still alive, though some have gone to their rest.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 7] After that, he appeared to James, and then to all the apostles.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 8] Last of all, he appeared even to me, who am, as it were, a miscarried baby – born untimely.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 9] For I am the least of the apostles, I who am unworthy of the name of “apostle,” because I persecuted the church of God.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 10] But it is through the love of God that I am what I am, and the love that he showed me has not been wasted. No, I have toiled harder than any of them, and yet it was not I, but the love of God working with me.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 11] Whether, then, it was I or whether it was they, this we proclaim, and this you believed.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 12] Now, if it is proclaimed of Christ that he has been raised from the dead, how is it that some of you say that there is no such thing as a resurrection of the dead?
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 13] But, if there is no such thing as a resurrection of the dead, then even Christ has not been raised;
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 14] and, if Christ has not been raised, then our proclamation is without meaning, and our faith without meaning also!
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 15] Yes, and we are being proved to have borne false testimony about God; for we testified of God that he raised the Christ, whom he did not raise, if, indeed, the dead do not rise!
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 16] For, if the dead do not rise, then even Christ himself has not been raised,
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 17] and, if Christ has not been raised, your faith is folly – your sins are on you still!
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 18] Yes, and they, who have passed to their rest in union with Christ, perished!
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 19] If all that we have done has been to place our hope in Christ for this life, then we of all people are the most to be pitied.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 20] But, in truth, Christ has been raised from the dead, the first-fruits of those who are at rest.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 21] For, since through a man there is death, so, too, through a man there is a resurrection of the dead.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 22] For, as through union with Adam all die, so through union with the Christ will all be made to live.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 23] But each in their proper order – Christ the first-fruits; afterwards, at his coming, those who belong to the Christ.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 24] Then will come the end – when he surrenders the kingdom to his God and Father, having overthrown all other rule and all other authority and power.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 25] For he must reign until God “has put all his enemies under his feet.”
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 26] The last enemy to be overthrown is death;
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 27] for God has placed all things under Christ’s feet. (But, when it is said that all things have been placed under Christ, it is plain that God is excepted who placed everything under him.)
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 28] And, when everything has been placed under him, the Son will place himself under God who placed everything under him, so that God may be all in all!
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 29] Again, what good will they be doing who are baptized on behalf of the dead? If it is true that the dead do not rise, why are people baptized on their behalf?
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 30] Why, too, do we risk our lives every hour?
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 31] Daily I face death – I swear it, friends, by the pride in you that I feel through my union with Christ Jesus, our Lord.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 32] If with only human hopes I had fought in the arena at Ephesus, what should I have gained by it? If the dead do not rise, then let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we will die!
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 33] Do not be deceived. “Good character is marred by evil company.”
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 34] Wake up to a righteous life, and cease to sin. There are some who have no true knowledge of God. I speak in this way to shame you.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 35] Someone, however, may ask “How do the dead rise? And in what body will they come?”
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 36] You foolish person! The seed you yourself sow does not come to life, unless it dies!
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 37] And when you sow, you sow not the body that will be, but a mere grain – perhaps of wheat, or something else.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 38] God gives it the body that he pleases – to each seed its special body.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 39] All forms of life are not the same; there is one for people, another for beasts, another for birds, and another for fish.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 40] There are heavenly bodies, and earthly bodies; but the beauty of the heavenly bodies is not the beauty of the earthly.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 41] There is a beauty of the sun, and a beauty of the moon, and a beauty of the stars; for even star differs from star in beauty.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 42] It is the same with the resurrection of the dead. Sown a mortal body, it rises immortal; sown disfigured, it rises beautiful;
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 43] sown weak, it rises strong; sown a human body, it rises a spiritual body.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 44] As surely as there is a human body, there is also a spiritual body.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 45] That is what is meant by the words – “Adam, the first man, became a human being”; the last Adam became a life-giving spirit.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 46] That which comes first is not the spiritual, but the human; afterwards comes the spiritual;
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 47] the first man was from the dust of the earth; the second man from heaven.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 48] Those who are of the dust are like him who came from the dust; and those who are of heaven are like him who came from heaven.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 49] And as we have borne the likeness of him who came from the dust, so let us bear the likeness of him who came from heaven.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 50] This I say, friends – Flesh and blood can have no share in the kingdom of God, nor can the perishable share the imperishable.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 51] Listen, I will tell you God’s hidden purpose! We will not all have passed to our rest, but we will all be transformed – in a moment, in the twinkling of an eye,
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 52] at the last trumpet-call; for the trumpet will sound, and the dead will rise immortal, and we, also, will be transformed.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 53] For this perishable body of ours must put on an imperishable form, and this dying body a deathless form.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 54] And, when this dying body has put on its deathless form, then indeed will the words of scripture come true –
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 55] “death has been swallowed up in victory! Where, Death, is your victory? Where, death, is your sting?”
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 56] It is sin that gives death its sting, and it is the Law that gives sin its power.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 57] But thanks be to God, who gives us the victory, through Jesus Christ, our Lord.
[OEB | EN | NT | 1 Corinthians | Chapter 15 | Verse 58] Therefore, my dear friends, stand firm, unshaken, always diligent in the Lord’s work, for you know that, in union with him, your toil is not in vain.
