[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 1] Once more Jesus spoke to them in parables.
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 2] ‘The kingdom of heaven,’ he said, ‘may be compared to a king who gave a banquet in honour of his son’s wedding.
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 3] He sent his servants to call those who had been invited to the banquet, but they were unwilling to come.
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 4] A second time he sent some servants, with orders to say to those who had been invited “I have prepared my meal, my cattle and fat beasts are killed and everything is ready; come to the banquet.”
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 5] They, however, took no notice, but went off, one to their farm, another to their business;
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 6] while the rest, seizing his servants, ill-treated them and killed them.
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 7] The king, in anger, sent his troops, put those murderers to death, and set their city on fire.
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 8] Then he said to his servants “The banquet is prepared, but those who were invited were not worthy.
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 9] So go to the cross-roads, and invite everyone you find to the banquet.”
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 10] The servants went out into the roads and collected all the people whom they found, whether bad or good; and the bridal-hall was filled with guests.
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 11] But, when the king went in to see his guests, he noticed there a man who had not put on a wedding-robe.
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 12] So he said to him “My friend, how is it that you came in here without a wedding-robe?” The man was speechless.
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 13] Then the king said to the attendants “Tie him hand and foot, and put him out into the darkness outside, where there will be weeping and grinding of teeth.”
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 14] For many are called, but few chosen.’
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 15] Then the Pharisees went away and conferred together as to how they might lay a trap for Jesus in the course of conversation.
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 16] They sent their disciples, with the Herodians, to say to him, ‘Teacher, we know that you are an honest person, and that you teach the way of God honestly, and are not afraid of anyone; for you pay no regard to a person’s position.
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 17] Tell us, then, what you think. Are we right in paying taxes to the Emperor, or not?’
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 18] Perceiving their malice, Jesus answered, ‘Why are you testing me, you hypocrites?
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 19] Show me the coin with which the tax is paid.’ And, when they had brought him a coin,
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 20] he asked, ‘Whose head and title are these?’
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 21] ‘The Emperor’s,’ they answered, at which he said to them, ‘Then pay to the Emperor what belongs to the Emperor, and to God what belongs to God.’
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 22] They wondered at his answer, and left him alone and went away.
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 23] That same day some Sadducees came up to Jesus, maintaining that there is no resurrection. Their question was this, –
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 24] ‘Teacher, Moses said – “should a man die without children, the man’s brother will become the husband of the widow, and raise a family for his brother.”
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 25] Now we had living among us seven brothers; of whom the eldest married and died, and, as he had no family, left his wife for his brother.
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 26] The same thing happened to the second and the third brothers, and indeed to all the seven.
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 27] The woman herself died last of all.
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 28] At the resurrection, then, whose wife will she be out of the seven, all of them having been married to her?’
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 29] ‘Your mistake,’ replied Jesus, ‘is due to your ignorance of the scriptures, and of the power of God.
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 30] For at the resurrection there is no marrying or being married, but all who rise are as angels in heaven.
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 31] As to the resurrection of the dead, have you not read these words of God –
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 32] “I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob”? He is not the God of dead people, but of living.’
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 33] The crowds, who had been listening to him, were greatly struck with his teaching.
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 34] When the Pharisees heard that Jesus had silenced the Sadducees, they collected together.
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 35] Then one of them, a Student of the Law, to test him, asked this question –

[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 38] This is the great first commandment.
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 39] The second, which is like it, is this – You must love your neighbour as you love yourself.
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 40] On these two commandments hang all the Law and the prophets.’
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 41] Before the Pharisees separated, Jesus put this question to them –
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 42] ‘What do you think about the Christ? Whose son is he?’ ‘David’s,’ they said.
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 43] ‘How is it, then,’ Jesus replied, ‘that David, speaking under inspiration, calls him “lord,” in the passage –
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 44] “The Lord said to my Lord: Sit at my right hand, until I put your enemies beneath your feet”?
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 45] Since, then, David calls him “lord,” how is he David’s son?’
[OEB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 22 | Verse 46] No one could say a word in answer; nor did anyone after that day venture to question him further.
