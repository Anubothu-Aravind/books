[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 1] About that time an edict was issued by the Emperor Augustus that a census should be taken of the whole Empire.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 2] (This was the first census taken while Quirinius was Governor of Syria).
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 3] And everyone went to his own town to be registered.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 4] Among others Joseph went up from the town of Nazareth in Galilee to Bethlehem, the town of David, in Judea – because he belonged to the family and house of David –
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 5] to be registered with Mary, his engaged wife, who was about to become a mother.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 6] While they were there her time came,
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 7] and she gave birth to her first child, a son. And because there was no room for them in the inn, she swathed him round and laid him in a manger.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 8] In that same countryside were shepherds out in the open fields, watching their flocks that night,
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 9] when an angel of the Lord suddenly stood by them, and the glory of the Lord shone around them; and they were seized with fear.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 10] ‘Have no fear,’ the angel said. ‘For I bring you good news of a great joy in store for all the nation.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 11] This day there has been born to you, in the town of David, a Saviour, who is Christ and Lord.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 12] And this will be the sign for you. You will find the infant swathed, and lying in a manger.’
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 13] Then suddenly there appeared with the angel a multitude of the heavenly Host, praising God, and singing –
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 14] ‘glory to God on high, and on earth peace among those in whom he finds pleasure.’
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 15] Now, when the angels had left them and gone back to heaven, the shepherds said to one another, ‘Let us go at once to Bethlehem, and see this thing that has happened, of which the Lord has told us.’
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 16] So they went quickly, and found Mary and Joseph, and the infant lying in a manger;
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 17] and, when they saw it, they told of all that had been said to them about this child.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 18] All who heard the shepherds were astonished at their story,
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 19] while Mary treasured in her heart all that they said, and thought about it often.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 20] And the shepherds went back, giving glory and praise to God for all that they had heard and seen. It had all happened as they had been told.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 21] Eight days after the birth of the child, when it was time to circumcise him, he received the name Jesus – the name given him by the angel before his conception.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 22] When the period of purification of mother and child, required by the Law of Moses, came to an end, his parents took the child up to Jerusalem to present him to the Lord,
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 23] in compliance with the Law of the Lord that every firstborn male will be dedicated to the Lord,
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 24] and also to offer the sacrifice required by the Law of the Lord – a pair of turtle-doves or two young pigeons.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 25] There was at that time in Jerusalem a man named Simeon, a righteous and devout man, who lived in constant expectation of Israel’s consulation, and under the guidance of the Holy Spirit.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 26] It had been revealed to him by the Holy Spirit that he should not die until he had seen the Lord’s Christ.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 27] Moved by the Spirit, Simeon came into the Temple Courts, and, when the parents brought in the child Jesus, to do for him what was customary under the Law,
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 28] Simeon himself took the child in his arms, and blessed God, and said:
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 29] ‘Now, Lord, you will let your servant go, according to your word, in peace,
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 30] for my eyes have seen the salvation
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 31] which you have prepared in the sight of all nations –
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 32] a light to bring light to the Gentiles, and to be the glory of your people Israel.’
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 33] While the child’s father and mother were wondering at what was said about him,
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 34] Simeon gave them his blessing, and said to Mary, the child’s mother, ‘This child is appointed to be the cause of the fall and rise of many in Israel, and to be a sign much spoken against –
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 35] Yes, the sword will pierce your own heart – and so the thoughts in many minds will be disclosed.’
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 36] There was also a prophet named Hannah, a daughter of Phanuel and of the tribe of Asher. She was far advanced in years, having lived with her husband for seven years after marriage,
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 37] and then a widow, until she had reached the age of eighty-four. She never left the Temple Courts, but, fasting and praying, worshiped God night and day.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 38] At that moment she came up, and began publicly to thank God and to speak about the child to all who were looking for the deliverance of Jerusalem.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 39] When the child’s parents had done everything required by the Law of the Lord, they returned to Galilee to their own town of Nazareth.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 40] The child grew and became strong and wise, and the blessing of God was on him.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 41] Every year the child’s parents used to go to Jerusalem at the Passover Festival.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 42] When Jesus was twelve years old, they went according to custom to Jerusalem,
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 43] and had finished their visit; but, when they started to return, the boy Jesus remained behind in Jerusalem, without their knowing it.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 44] Thinking that he was with their fellow travellers, they went one day’s journey before searching for him among their relatives and acquaintances;
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 45] and then, as they did not find him, they returned to Jerusalem, searching everywhere for him.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 46] It was not until the third day that they found him in the Temple Courts, sitting among the teachers, now listening to them, now asking them questions.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 47] All who listened to him marvelled at his intelligence and his answers.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 48] His parents were amazed when they saw him, and his mother said to him, ‘My child, why have you treated us like this? Your father and I have been searching for you in great distress.’
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 49] ‘What made you search for me?’ he answered. ‘Didn’t you know that I must be in my Father’s house?’
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 50] His parents did not understand what he meant.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 51] However he went down with them to Nazareth, and submitted himself to their control; and his mother treasured all that was said in her heart.
[OEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 2 | Verse 52] And Jesus grew in wisdom as he grew in years, and gained the blessing of God and people.
