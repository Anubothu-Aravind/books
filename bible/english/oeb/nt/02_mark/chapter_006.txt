[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 1] On leaving that place, Jesus, followed by his disciples, went to his own part of the country.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 2] When the Sabbath came, he began to teach in the synagogue; and the people, as they listened, were deeply impressed. ‘Where did he get this?’ they said, ‘and what is this wisdom that has been given him? And these miracles which he is doing?
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 3] Isn’t he the carpenter, the son of Mary, and the brother of James, and Joses, and Judas, and Simon? And aren’t his sisters, too, living here among us?’ This proved a hindrance to their believing in him;
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 4] at which Jesus said, ‘A prophet is not without honour, except in his home town, and among his own relatives, and in his own home.’
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 5] And he could not work any miracle there, beyond placing his hands on a few infirm persons, and curing them;
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 6] and he wondered at the want of faith shown by the people. Jesus went round the villages, one after another, teaching.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 7] He called the Twelve to him, and began to send them out as his messengers, two and two, and gave them authority over foul spirits.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 8] He instructed them to take nothing but a staff for the journey – not even bread, or a bag, or coins in their purse;
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 9] but they were to wear sandals, and not to put on a second coat.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 10] ‘Whenever you go to stay at a house,’ he said, ‘remain there until you leave that place;
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 11] and if a place does not welcome you, or listen to you, as you go out of it shake off the dust that is on the soles of your feet, as a protest against them.’
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 12] So they set out, and proclaimed the need of repentance.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 13] They drove out many demons, and anointed with oil many who were infirm, and cured them.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 14] Now King Herod heard of Jesus; for his name had become well known. People were saying – ‘John the Baptizer must have risen from the dead, and that is why these miraculous powers are active in him.’
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 15] Others again said – ‘He is Elijah,’ and others – ‘He is a prophet, like one of the great prophets.’
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 16] But when Herod heard of him, he said – ‘The man whom I beheaded – John – he must be risen!’
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 17] For Herod himself had sent and arrested John, and put him in prison, in chains, to please Herodias, the wife of his brother Philip, because Herod had married her.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 18] For John had said to Herod – ‘You have no right to be living with your brother’s wife.’
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 19] So Herodias was incensed against John, and wanted to put him to death, but was unable to do so,
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 20] because Herod stood in fear of John, knowing him to be an upright and holy man, and protected him. He had listened to John, but still remained much perplexed, and yet he found pleasure in listening to him.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 21] A suitable opportunity, however, occurred when Herod, on his birthday, gave a dinner to his high officials, and his generals, and the foremost men in Galilee.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 22] When his daughter – that is, the daughter of Herodias – came in and danced, she delighted Herod and those who were dining with him. ‘Ask me for whatever you like,’ the king said to the girl, ‘and I will give it to you’;
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 23] and he swore to her that he would give her whatever she asked him – up to half his kingdom.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 24] The girl went out, and said to her mother ‘What must I ask for?’ ‘The head of John the Baptizer,’ answered her mother.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 25] So she went in as quickly as possible to the king, and made her request. ‘I want you,’ she said, ‘to give me at once, on a dish, the head of John the Baptist.’
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 26] The king was much distressed; yet, because of his oath and of the guests at his table, he did not like to refuse her.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 27] He immediately dispatched one of his bodyguard, with orders to bring John’s head. The man went and beheaded John in the prison,
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 28] and, bringing his head on a dish, gave it to the girl, and the girl gave it to her mother.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 29] When John’s disciples heard of it, they came and took his body away, and laid it in a tomb.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 30] When the apostles came back to Jesus, they told him all that they had done and all that they had taught.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 31] ‘Come by yourselves privately to some lonely spot,’ he said, ‘and rest for a while’ – for there were so many people coming and going that they had not time even to eat.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 32] So they set off privately in their boat for a lonely spot.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 33] Many people saw them going, and recognised them, and from all the towns they flocked together to the place on foot, and got there before them.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 34] On getting out of the boat, Jesus saw a great crowd, and his heart was moved at the sight of them, because they were like sheep without a shepherd; and he began to teach them many things.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 35] When it grew late, his disciples came up to him, and said, ‘This is a lonely spot, and it is already late.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 36] Send the people away, so that they may go to the farms and villages around and buy themselves something to eat.’
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 37] But Jesus answered, ‘It is for you to give them something to eat.’ ‘Are we to go and spend almost a year’s wages on bread,’ they asked, ‘to give them to eat?’
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 38] ‘How many loaves have you?’ he asked, ‘Go, and see.’ When they had found out, they told him, ‘Five, and two fish.’
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 39] Jesus directed them to make all the people take their seats on the green grass, in parties;
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 40] and they sat down in groups – in hundreds, and in fifties.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 41] Taking the five loaves and the two fish, Jesus looked up to heaven, and said the blessing; he broke the loaves into pieces, and gave them to his disciples for them to serve out to the people, and he divided the two fish also among them all.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 42] Everyone had sufficient to eat;
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 43] and they picked up enough broken pieces to fill twelve baskets, as well as some of the fish.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 44] The people who ate the bread were five thousand in number.

[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 46] After he had taken leave of the people, he went away up the hill to pray.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 47] When evening fell, the boat was out in the middle of the sea, and Jesus on the shore alone.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 48] Seeing them labouring at the oars – for the wind was against them – about three hours after midnight Jesus came towards them, walking on the water, intending to join them.
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 49] But, when they saw him walking on the water, they thought it was a ghost, and cried out;
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 50] for all of them saw him, and were terrified. But Jesus at once spoke to them. ‘Courage!’ he said, ‘it is I; do not be afraid!’
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 51] Then he got into the boat with them, and the wind dropped. The disciples were utterly amazed,
[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 52] for they had not understood about the loaves, their minds being slow to learn.

[OEB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 56] So wherever he went – to villages, or towns, or farms – they would lay their sick in the market-places, begging him to let them touch only the tassel of his cloak; and all who touched were made well.
