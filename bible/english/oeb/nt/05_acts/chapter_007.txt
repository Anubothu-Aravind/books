[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 1] Then the high priest asked, ‘Is this true?’
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 2] Stephen replied, ‘Brothers and fathers, hear what I have to say. God, who manifests himself in the glory, appeared to our ancestor Abraham when he was in Mesopotamia, and before he settled in Haran, and said to him –
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 3] “Leave your country and your people, and come into the country that I will show you.”
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 4] And so Abraham left the country of the Chaldaeans and settled in Haran; and from there, after his father’s death, God caused him to migrate into this country, in which you are now living.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 5] God did not at that time give him any part of it, not even a foot of ground. But he promised to give him possession of it and his descendants after him, though at that time he had no child.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 6] God’s words were these – “Abraham’s descendants will live in a foreign country, where they will be enslaved and ill-treated for four hundred years.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 7] But I myself will judge the nation, to which they will be enslaved,” God said, “and after that they will leave the country and worship me in this place.”
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 8] Then God made with Abraham the covenant of circumcision; and under it Abraham became the father of Isaac, and circumcised him when he was eight days old; and Isaac became the father of Jacob; and Jacob of the Twelve Patriarchs.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 9] The Patriarchs, out of jealousy, sold Joseph into slavery in Egypt; but God was with him,
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 10] and delivered him out of all his troubles, and enabled him to win favour and show wisdom before Pharaoh, king of Egypt, who appointed him Governor of Egypt and of his whole household.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 11] Then a famine spread over the whole of Egypt and Canaan, causing great distress, and our ancestors could find no food.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 12] Hearing, however, that there was corn in Egypt, Jacob sent our ancestors there on their first visit.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 13] In the course of their second visit, Joseph revealed himself to his brothers, and his family became known to Pharaoh.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 14] Then Joseph sent an urgent invitation to his father Jacob and to his relatives, seventy-five persons in all;
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 15] and so Jacob went down into Egypt. There he died, and our ancestors also,
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 16] and their bodies were removed to Shechem, and laid in the tomb which Abraham had bought for a sum of money from the sons of Hamor in Shechem.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 17] As the time drew near for the fulfilment of the promise which God had made to Abraham, the people increased largely in numbers in Egypt,
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 18] until a new king, who knew nothing of Joseph, came to the throne.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 19] This king acted deceitfully towards our people and ill-treated our ancestors, making them abandon their own infants, so that they should not be reared.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 20] It was just at this time that Moses was born. He was an exceedingly beautiful child, and for three months was brought up in his own father’s house;
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 21] and, when he was abandoned, the daughter of Pharaoh found him and brought him up as her own son.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 22] So Moses was educated in all the learning of the Egyptians, and proved his ability both by his words and actions.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 23] When he was in his fortieth year, he resolved to visit his fellow Israelites;
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 24] and, seeing an Israelite ill-treated, he defended him, and avenged the man, who was being wronged, by striking down the Egyptian.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 25] He thought his own people would understand that God was using him to save them; but they failed to do so.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 26] The next day he again appeared on the scene, when some of them were fighting, and tried to make peace between them. “Men,” he said, “you are brothers; how is it that you are ill-treating one another?”
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 27] But the man who was ill-treating his fellow workman pushed Moses aside saying – “Who made you a ruler and judge over us?
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 28] Do you mean to make away with me as you did yesterday with that Egyptian?”
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 29] At these words Moses took to flight, and became an exile in Midian; and there he had two sons born to him.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 30] Forty years had passed when there appeared to him, in the desert of Mount Sinai, an angel in a flame of fire in a bush.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 31] When Moses saw it, he was astonished at the vision; but on his going nearer to look at it more closely, the voice of the Lord was heard to say –
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 32] “I am the God of your ancestors, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.” Moses trembled, and did not dare to look.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 33] Then the Lord said to him – “Take your sandals off your feet, for the spot where you are standing is holy ground.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 34] I have seen the oppression of my people who are in Egypt, and heard their groans, and I have come down to deliver them. Come now and I will send you into Egypt.”
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 35] This same Moses, whom they had disowned with the words – “Who made you a ruler and a judge?” was the man whom God sent to be both a ruler and a deliverer, under the guidance of the angel that had appeared to him in the bush.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 36] He it was who led them out, after he had shown wonders and signs in Egypt, in the Red Sea, and in the desert during forty years.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 37] This was the Moses who said to the people of Israel – “God will raise up for you, from among yourselves, a prophet, as he raised up me.”
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 38] He, too, it was who was present at the assembly in the desert, with the angel who talked to him on Mount Sinai, and with our ancestors, and who received living truths to impart to you.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 39] Yet our ancestors refused him obedience; more than that, they rejected him, and in their hearts turned back to Egypt,
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 40] while they said to Aaron – “Make us Gods who will lead the way for us, since, as for this Moses who has brought us out of Egypt, we do not know what has become of him.”
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 41] That was the time when they made the calf and offered sacrifice to their idol, and held festivities in honour of their own handiwork!
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 42] So God turned from them and left them to the worship of the Starry Host, as is written in the book of the prophets – “Did you offer victims and sacrifices to me, house of Israel, all those forty years in the desert?
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 43] You took with you the tent where Moloch is worshipped and the star of the god Rephan – the images which you had made to worship. Therefore I will exile you beyond Babylon.”
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 44] Our ancestors had the tent where they worshipped God in the desert, constructed, just as he who spoke to Moses had directed him to make it, after the model which he had seen.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 45] This tent, which was handed down to them, was brought into this country by our ancestors who accompanied Joshua (at the conquest of the nations that God drove out before their advance), and remained here until the time of David.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 46] David found favour with God, and prayed that he might provide the God of Jacob with a place to reside.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 47] But it was Solomon who built a house for God.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 48] Yet it is not in buildings made by hands that the Most High dwells. As the prophet says –
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 49] “The heavens are a throne for me, and the earth a stool for my feet. What manner of house will you build me, asks the Lord, or what place is there where I may rest?
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 50] Was it not my hand that made all these things?”
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 51] Stubborn people, heathen in heart and ears, you are for ever resisting the Holy Spirit; your ancestors did it, and you are doing it still.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 52] Which of the prophets escaped persecution at their hands? They killed those who foretold the coming of the righteous one; of whom you, in your turn, have now become the betrayers and murderers –
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 53] you who received the Law as transmitted by angels and yet failed to keep it.’
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 54] As they listened to this, the Council grew frantic with rage, and gnashed their teeth at Stephen.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 55] He, filled as he was with the Holy Spirit, fixed his eyes intently on the heavens, and saw the glory of God and Jesus standing at God’s right hand.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 56] ‘Look,’ he exclaimed, ‘I see heaven open and the Son of Man standing at God’s right hand!’
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 57] At this, with a loud shout, they stopped their ears and all rushed on him, forced him outside the city,
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 58] and began to stone him, the witnesses laying their clothes at the feet of a young man named Saul.
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 59] And they stoned Stephen, while he cried to the Lord, ‘Lord Jesus! Receive my spirit!’
[OEB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 7 | Verse 60] Falling on his knees, he called out loudly, ‘Lord! Do not charge them with this sin;’ and with these words he fell asleep.
