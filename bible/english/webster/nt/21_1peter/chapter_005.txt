[WEBSTER | EN | NT | 1 Peter | Chapter 5 | Verse 3] Neither as being lords over God's heritage, but being examples to the flock.

[WEBSTER | EN | NT | 1 Peter | Chapter 5 | Verse 7] Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | 1 Peter | Chapter 5 | Verse 8] Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour:
