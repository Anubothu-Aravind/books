[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 15 | Verse 2] And Pilate asked him, Art thou the King of the Jews? And he answering, said to him, Thou sayest it .

[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 15 | Verse 4] And Pilate asked him again, saying, Answerest thou nothing? behold how many things they testify against thee.

[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 15 | Verse 8] And the multitude crying aloud, began to desire him to do as he had ever done to them.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 15 | Verse 9] But Pilate answered them, saying, Will ye that I release to you the King of the Jews?
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 15 | Verse 10] (For he knew that the chief priests had delivered him for envy.)
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 15 | Verse 11] But the chief priests moved the people that he should rather release Barabbas to them.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 15 | Verse 12] And Pilate answered, and said again to them, What will ye then that I shall do to him whom ye call the King of the Jews?
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 15 | Verse 13] And they cried out again, Crucify him.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 15 | Verse 14] Then Pilate said to them, Why, what evil hath he done? And they cried out the more exceedingly, Crucify him.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 15 | Verse 15] And so Pilate, willing to content the people, released Barabbas to them, and having scourged Jesus, delivered him to be crucified.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 15 | Verse 16] And the soldiers led him away into the hall, called Pretorium; and they call together the whole band;
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 15 | Verse 17] And they clothed him with purple, and platted a crown of thorns, and put it about his head ,
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 15 | Verse 18] And began to salute him, Hail, King of the Jews!

[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 15 | Verse 20] And when they had mocked him, they took off the purple from him, and put his own clothes on him, and led him out to crucify him.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 15 | Verse 21] And they constrain one Simon, a Cyrenian, who was passing by, coming from the country, the father of Alexander and Rufus, to bear his cross.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 15 | Verse 22] And they bring him to the place Golgotha, which is, being interpreted, The place of a skull.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 15 | Verse 23] And they gave him to drink, wine mingled with myrrh: but he received it not.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 15 | Verse 24] And when they had crucified him, they parted his garments, casting lots upon them, what every man should take.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 15 | Verse 25] And it was the third hour, and they crucified him.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 15 | Verse 26] And the superscription of his accusation was written over, THE KING OF THE JEWS.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 15 | Verse 27] And with him they crucify two thieves, the one on his right hand, and the other on his left.

[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 15 | Verse 29] And they that passed by, railed on him, wagging their heads, and saying, Ah, thou that destroyest the temple, and buildest it in three days,

[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 15 | Verse 33] And when the sixth hour was come, there was darkness over the whole land, until the ninth hour.

[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 15 | Verse 47] And Mary Magdalene and Mary the mother of Joses, beheld where he was laid.
