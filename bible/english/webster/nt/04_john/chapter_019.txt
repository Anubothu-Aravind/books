[WEBSTER | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 12] And from thenceforth Pilate sought to release him: but the Jews cried out, saying, If thou let this man go, thou art not Cesar's friend. Whoever maketh himself a king, speaketh against Cesar.
