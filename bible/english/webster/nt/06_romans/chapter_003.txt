[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 1] What advantage then hath the Jew? or what profit is there of circumcision?
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 2] Much every way: chiefly, because that to them were committed the oracles of God.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 3] For what if some did not believe? will their unbelief make the faith of God without effect?
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 4] By no means: verily let God be true, but every man a liar; as it is written, That thou mayest be justified in thy sayings, and mayest overcome when thou art judged.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 5] But if our unrighteousness commendeth the righteousness of God, what shall we say? Is God unrighteous who taketh vengeance? (I speak as a man.)
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 6] By no means: for then how shall God judge the world?
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 7] For if the truth of God hath more abounded through my lie to his glory; why yet am I also judged as a sinner?
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 8] And not rather (as we are slanderously reported, and as some affirm that we say) Let us do evil, that good may come? whose damnation is just.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 9] What then? are we better than they ? No, in no wise: for we have before proved both Jews and Gentiles, that they are all under sin;
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 10] As it is written, There is none righteous, no, not one:
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 11] There is none that understandeth, there is none that seeketh God.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 12] They are all gone out of the way, they are together become unprofitable: there is none that doeth good, no, not one.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 13] Their throat is an open sepulcher; with their tongues they have used deceit; the poison of asps is under their lips:
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 14] Whose mouth is full of cursing and bitterness.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 15] Their feet are swift to shed blood.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 16] Destruction and misery are in their ways:
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 17] And the way of peace have they not known.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 18] There is no fear of God before their eyes.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 19] Now we know that whatever things the law saith, it saith to them who are under the law: that every mouth may be stopped, and all the world may become guilty before God.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 20] Therefore by the deeds of the law, there shall no flesh be justified in his sight: for by the law is the knowledge of sin.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 21] But now the righteousness of God without the law is manifested, being testified by the law and the prophets;
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 22] Even the righteousness of God, which is by faith of Jesus Christ to all, and upon all them that believe; for there is no difference:
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 23] For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God;
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 24] Being justified freely by his grace, through the redemption that is in Jesus Christ:
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 25] Whom God hath set forth to be a propitiation, through faith in his blood, to declare his righteousness for the remission of sins that are past, through the forbearance of God;
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 26] To declare, I say , at this time his righteousness: that he may be just, and the justifier of him who believeth in Jesus.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 27] Where is boasting then? It is excluded. By what law? of works? No; but by the law of faith.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 28] Therefore we conclude, that a man is justified by faith without the deeds of the law.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 29] Is he the God of the Jews only? is he not also of the Gentiles? Yes, of the Gentiles also:
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 30] Seeing it is one God who will justify the circumcision by faith, and uncircumcision through faith.
[WEBSTER | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 31] Do we then make void the law through faith? By no means: but we establish the law.
