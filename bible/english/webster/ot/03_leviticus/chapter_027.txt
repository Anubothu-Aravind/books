[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 27 | Verse 3] And thy estimation shall be, of the male from twenty years old even to sixty years old, even thy estimation shall be fifty shekels of silver, after the shekel of the sanctuary.

[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 27 | Verse 5] And if it shall be from five years old even to twenty years old, then thy estimation shall be of the male twenty shekels, and for the female ten shekels.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 27 | Verse 6] And if it shall be from a month old even to five years old, then thy estimation shall be of the male five shekels of silver, and for the female thy estimation shall be three shekels of silver.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 27 | Verse 7] And if it shall be from sixty years old and above; if a male, then thy estimation shall be fifteen shekels, and for the female ten shekels.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 27 | Verse 8] But if he shall be poorer than thy estimation, then he shall present himself before the priest, and the priest shall value him: according to his ability that vowed shall the priest value him.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 27 | Verse 9] And if it shall be a beast of which men bring an offering to the LORD, all that any man giveth of such to the LORD shall be holy.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 27 | Verse 10] He shall not alter it, nor change it, a good for a bad, or a bad for a good: and if he shall at all change beast for beast, then it and the exchange of it shall be holy.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 27 | Verse 11] And if it shall be any unclean beast, of which they do not offer a sacrifice to the LORD, then he shall present the beast before the priest:
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 27 | Verse 12] And the priest shall value it, whether it be good or bad: as thou valuest it, who art the priest, so shall it be.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 27 | Verse 13] But if he will at all redeem it, then he shall add a fifth part of it to thy estimation.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 27 | Verse 14] And when a man shall sanctify his house to be holy to the LORD, then the priest shall estimate it, whether it is good or bad: as the priest shall estimate it, so shall it stand.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 27 | Verse 15] And if he that sanctified it will redeem his house, then he shall add the fifth part of the money of thy estimation to it, and it shall be his.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 27 | Verse 16] And if a man shall sanctify to the LORD some part of a field of his possession, then thy estimation shall be according to the seed of it: a homer of barley seed shall be valued at fifty shekels of silver.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 27 | Verse 17] If he shall sanctify his field from the year of jubilee, according to thy estimation it shall stand.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 27 | Verse 18] But if he shall sanctify his field after the jubilee, then the priest shall reckon to him the money according to the years that remain, even to the year of the jubilee, and it shall be abated from thy estimation.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 27 | Verse 19] And if he that sanctified the field will in any wise redeem it, then he shall add the fifth part of the money of thy estimation to it, and it shall be assured to him.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 27 | Verse 20] And if he will not redeem the field, or if he shall have sold the field to another man, it shall not be redeemed any more.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 27 | Verse 21] But the field, when it goeth out in the jubilee, shall be holy to the LORD, as a field devoted: the possession of it shall be the priest's.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 27 | Verse 22] And if a man shall sanctify to the LORD a field which he hath bought, which is not of the fields of his possession;
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 27 | Verse 23] Then the priest shall reckon to him the worth of thy estimation, even to the year of the jubilee, and he shall give thy estimation in that day, as a holy thing to the LORD.

[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 27 | Verse 26] Only the firstling of the beasts, which should be the LORD'S firstling, no man shall sanctify it; whether ox, or sheep: it is the LORD'S.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 27 | Verse 27] And if it shall be of an unclean beast, then he shall redeem it , according to thy estimation, and shall add to it a fifth part of it: or if it shall be not redeemed, then it shall be sold according to thy estimation.
