[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 39 | Verse 1] At that time Merodach-baladan, the son of Baladan, king of Babylon, sent letters and a present to Hezekiah: for he had heard that he had been sick, and had recovered.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 39 | Verse 2] And Hezekiah was glad of them, and showed them the house of his precious things, the silver, and the gold, and the spices, and the precious ointment, and all the house of his armor, and all that was found in his treasures: there was nothing in his house, nor in all his dominion, that Hezekiah did not show them.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 39 | Verse 3] Then came Isaiah the prophet to king Hezekiah, and said to him, What said these men? and from whence came they to thee? and Hezekiah said, They have come to me from a far country, even from Babylon.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 39 | Verse 4] Then said he, What have they seen in thy house? And Hezekiah answered, All that is in my house have they seen: there is nothing among my treasures that I have not showed them.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 39 | Verse 5] Then said Isaiah to Hezekiah, Hear the word of the LORD of hosts:
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 39 | Verse 6] Behold, the days come, that all that is in thy house, and that which thy fathers have laid up in store until this day, shall be carried to Babylon: nothing shall be left, saith the LORD.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 39 | Verse 7] And of thy sons that shall issue from thee, which thou shalt beget, shall they take away; and they shall be eunuchs in the palace of the king of Babylon.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 39 | Verse 8] Then said Hezekiah to Isaiah, Good is the word of the LORD which thou hast spoken. He said moreover, For there shall be peace and truth in my days.
