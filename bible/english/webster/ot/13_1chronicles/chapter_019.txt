[WEBSTER | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 19 | Verse 3] But the princes of the children of Ammon said to Hanun, Thinkest thou that David doth honor thy father, that he hath sent comforters to thee? are not his servants come to thee to search, and to overthrow, and to explore the land?

[WEBSTER | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 19 | Verse 6] And when the children of Ammon saw that they had made themselves odious to David, Hanun and the children of Ammon sent a thousand talents of silver to hire them chariots and horsemen out of Mesopotamia, and out of Syria-maachah, and out of Zobah.

[WEBSTER | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 19 | Verse 9] And the children of Ammon came out, and put the battle in array before the gate of the city: and the kings that had come were by themselves in the field.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 19 | Verse 10] Now when Joab saw that the battle was set against him before and behind, he selected all the choice of Israel, and put them in array against the Syrians.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 19 | Verse 11] And the rest of the people he delivered to the hand of Abishai his brother, and they set themselves in array against the children of Ammon.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 19 | Verse 12] And he said, If the Syrians shall be too strong for me, then thou shalt help me: but if the children of Ammon shall be too strong for thee, then I will help thee.

[WEBSTER | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 19 | Verse 15] And when the children of Ammon saw that the Syrians had fled, they likewise fled before Abishai his brother, and entered into the city. Then Joab came to Jerusalem.

[WEBSTER | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 19 | Verse 17] And it was told to David; and he gathered all Israel, and passed over Jordan, and came upon them, and set the battle in array against them. So when David had put the battle in array against the Syrians, they fought with him.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 19 | Verse 18] But the Syrians fled before Israel; and David slew of the Syrians seven thousand men who fought in chariots, and forty thousand footmen, and killed Shophach the captain of the host.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 19 | Verse 19] And when the servants of Hadarezer saw that they were defeated before Israel, they made peace with David, and became his servants: neither would the Syrians help the children of Ammon any more.
