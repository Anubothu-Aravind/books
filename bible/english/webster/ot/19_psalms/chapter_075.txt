[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 75 | Verse 1] To the chief Musician, Al-taschith, A Psalm or Song of Asaph. To thee, O God, do we give thanks, to thee do we give thanks: for that thy name is near, thy wondrous works declare.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 75 | Verse 2] When I shall receive the congregation I will judge uprightly.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 75 | Verse 3] The earth and all its inhabitants are dissolved: I bear up the pillars of it. Selah.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 75 | Verse 4] I said to the fools, Deal not foolishly; and to the wicked, Lift not up the horn:
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 75 | Verse 5] Lift not up your horn on high: speak not with a stiff neck.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 75 | Verse 6] For promotion cometh neither from the east, nor from the west, nor from the south.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 75 | Verse 7] But God is the judge: he putteth down one, and setteth up another.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 75 | Verse 8] For in the hand of the LORD there is a cup, and the wine is red; it is full of mixture, and he poureth out of the same: but the dregs of it all the wicked of the earth shall wring them out, and drink them .
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 75 | Verse 9] But I will declare for ever; I will sing praises to the God of Jacob.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 75 | Verse 10] All the horns of the wicked also will I cut off; but the horns of the righteous shall be exalted.
