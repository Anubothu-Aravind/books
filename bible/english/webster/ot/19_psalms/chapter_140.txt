[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 140 | Verse 1] To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David. Deliver me, O LORD, from the evil man: preserve me from the violent man;
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 140 | Verse 2] Who imagine mischiefs in their heart; continually are they assembled for war.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 140 | Verse 3] They have sharpened their tongues like a serpent; adder's poison is under their lips. Selah.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 140 | Verse 4] Keep me, O LORD, from the hands of the wicked; preserve me from the violent man; who have purposed to overthrow my goings.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 140 | Verse 5] The proud have hid a snare for me, and cords; they have spread a net by the way side: they have set gins for me. Selah.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 140 | Verse 6] I said to the LORD, Thou art my God: hear the voice of my supplications, O LORD.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 140 | Verse 7] O GOD the Lord, the strength of my salvation, thou hast covered my head in the day of battle.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 140 | Verse 8] Grant not, O LORD, the desires of the wicked: further not his wicked device; lest they exalt themselves. Selah.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 140 | Verse 9] As for the head of those that encompass me, let the mischief of their own lips cover them.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 140 | Verse 10] Let burning coals fall upon them: let them be cast into the fire; into deep pits, that they rise not again.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 140 | Verse 11] Let not an evil speaker be established on the earth: evil shall hunt the violent man to overthrow him .
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 140 | Verse 12] I know that the LORD will maintain the cause of the afflicted, and the right of the poor.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 140 | Verse 13] Surely the righteous shall give thanks to thy name: the upright shall dwell in thy presence.
