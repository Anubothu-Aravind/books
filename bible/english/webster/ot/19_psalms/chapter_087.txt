[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 87 | Verse 1] A Psalm or Song for the sons of Korah. His foundation is in the holy mountains.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 87 | Verse 2] The LORD loveth the gates of Zion more than all the dwellings of Jacob.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 87 | Verse 3] Glorious things are spoken of thee, O city of God. Selah.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 87 | Verse 4] I will make mention of Rahab and Babylon to them that know me: behold Philistia, and Tyre, with Cush; this man was born there.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 87 | Verse 5] And of Zion it shall be said, This and that man was born in her: and the Highest himself shall establish her.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 87 | Verse 6] The LORD shall count, when he writeth up the people, that this man was born there. Selah.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 87 | Verse 7] As well the singers as the players on instruments shall be there : all my springs are in thee.
