[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 8 | Verse 1] To the chief Musician upon Gittith, A Psalm of David. O LORD our Lord, how excellent is thy name in all the earth! who hast set thy glory above the heavens.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 8 | Verse 2] Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings hast thou ordained strength because of thy enemies, that thou mightest still the enemy and the avenger.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 8 | Verse 3] When I consider thy heavens, the work of thy fingers; the moon and the stars, which thou hast ordained;
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 8 | Verse 4] What is man, that thou art mindful of him? and the son of man, that thou visitest him?
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 8 | Verse 5] For thou hast made him a little lower than the angels, and hast crowned him with glory and honor.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 8 | Verse 6] Thou hast made him to have dominion over the works of thy hands; thou hast put all things under his feet:
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 8 | Verse 7] All sheep and oxen, yes, and the beasts of the field;
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 8 | Verse 8] The fowl of the air, and the fish of the sea, and whatever passeth through the paths of the seas.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 8 | Verse 9] O LORD our Lord, how excellent is thy name in all the earth!
