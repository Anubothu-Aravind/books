[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 72 | Verse 1] A Psalm for Solomon. Give the king thy judgments, O God, and thy righteousness to the king's son.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 72 | Verse 2] He shall judge thy people with righteousness, and thy poor with judgment.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 72 | Verse 3] The mountains shall bring peace to the people, and the little hills, by righteousness.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 72 | Verse 4] He shall judge the poor of the people, he shall save the children of the needy, and shall break in pieces the oppressor.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 72 | Verse 5] They shall fear thee as long as the sun and moon endure, throughout all generations.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 72 | Verse 6] He shall come down like rain upon the mown grass: as showers that water the earth.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 72 | Verse 7] In his days shall the righteous flourish; and abundance of peace so long as the moon endureth.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 72 | Verse 8] He shall have dominion also from sea to sea, and from the river to the ends of the earth.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 72 | Verse 9] They that dwell in the wilderness shall bow before him; and his enemies shall lick the dust.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 72 | Verse 10] The kings of Tarshish and of the isles shall bring presents: the kings of Sheba and Seba shall offer gifts.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 72 | Verse 11] Yes, all kings shall fall down before him: all nations shall serve him.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 72 | Verse 12] For he shall deliver the needy when he crieth; the poor also, and him that hath no helper.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 72 | Verse 13] He shall spare the poor and needy, and shall save the souls of the needy.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 72 | Verse 14] He shall redeem their soul from deceit and violence: and precious shall be their blood in his sight.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 72 | Verse 15] And he shall live, and to him shall be given of the gold of Sheba: prayer also shall be made for him continually; and daily shall he be praised.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 72 | Verse 16] There shall be a handful of corn in the earth upon the top of the mountains; the fruit of it shall shake like Lebanon: and they of the city shall flourish like grass of the earth.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 72 | Verse 17] His name shall endure for ever: his name shall be continued as long as the sun: and men shall be blessed in him: all nations shall call him blessed.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 72 | Verse 18] Blessed be the LORD God, the God of Israel, who only doeth wondrous things.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 72 | Verse 19] And blessed be his glorious name for ever: and let the whole earth be filled with his glory; Amen, and amen.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 72 | Verse 20] The prayers of David the son of Jesse are ended.
