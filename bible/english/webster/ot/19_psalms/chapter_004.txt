[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 4 | Verse 6] There are many that say, Who will show us any good? LORD, lift thou upon us the light of thy countenance.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 4 | Verse 7] Thou hast put gladness in my heart, more than in the time when their corn and their wine increased.
