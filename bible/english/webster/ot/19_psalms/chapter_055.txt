[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 55 | Verse 1] To the chief Musician on Neginoth, Maschil, A Psalm of David. Give ear to my prayer, O God; and hide not thyself from my supplication.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 55 | Verse 2] Attend to me, and hear me: I mourn in my complaint, and make a noise;
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 55 | Verse 3] Because of the voice of the enemy, because of the oppression of the wicked: for they cast iniquity upon me, and in wrath they hate me.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 55 | Verse 4] My heart is severely pained within me: and the terrors of death have fallen upon me.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 55 | Verse 5] Fearfulness and trembling have come upon me, and horror hath overwhelmed me.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 55 | Verse 6] And I said, O that I had wings like a dove! for then I would fly away, and be at rest.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 55 | Verse 7] Lo, then I would wander far off, and remain in the wilderness. Selah.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 55 | Verse 8] I would hasten my escape from the windy storm and tempest.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 55 | Verse 9] Destroy, O LORD, and divide their tongues: for I have seen violence and strife in the city.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 55 | Verse 10] Day and night they go about it upon its walls: mischief also and sorrow are in the midst of it.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 55 | Verse 11] Wickedness is in the midst of it: deceit and guile depart not from her streets.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 55 | Verse 12] For it was not an enemy that reproached me; then I could have borne it : neither was it he that hated me that magnified himself against me; then I would have hid myself from him:
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 55 | Verse 13] But it was thou, a man my equal, my guide, and my acquaintance.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 55 | Verse 14] We took sweet counsel together, and walked to the house of God in company.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 55 | Verse 15] Let death seize upon them, and let them go down quick into hell: for wickedness is in their dwellings, and among them.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 55 | Verse 16] As for me, I will call upon God: and the LORD will save me.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 55 | Verse 17] Evening, and morning, and at noon, will I pray, and cry aloud: and he will hear my voice.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 55 | Verse 18] He hath delivered my soul in peace from the battle that was against me: for there were many with me.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 55 | Verse 19] God will hear and afflict them, even he that abideth of old. Selah. Because they have no changes, therefore they fear not God.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 55 | Verse 20] He hath put forth his hands against such as are at peace with him: he hath broken his covenant.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 55 | Verse 21] The words of his mouth were smoother than butter, but war was in his heart: his words were softer than oil, yet were they drawn swords.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 55 | Verse 22] Cast thy burden upon the LORD, and he will sustain thee: he will never suffer the righteous to be moved.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 55 | Verse 23] But thou, O God, wilt bring them down into the pit of destruction: bloody and deceitful men shall not live out half their days; but I will trust in thee.
