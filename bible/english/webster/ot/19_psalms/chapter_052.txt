[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 1] To the chief Musician, Maschil, A Psalm of David, when Doeg the Edomite came and told Saul, and said to him, David is come to the house of Ahimelech. Why boastest thou thyself in mischief, O mighty man? the goodness of God endureth continually.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 2] Thy tongue deviseth mischiefs; like a sharp razor, working deceitfully.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 3] Thou lovest evil more than good; and lying rather than to speak righteousness. Selah.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 4] Thou lovest all devouring words, O thou deceitful tongue.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 5] God shall likewise destroy thee for ever, he shall take thee away, and pluck thee out of thy dwelling-place, and root thee out of the land of the living. Selah.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 6] The righteous also shall see, and fear, and shall laugh at him:
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 7] Lo, this is the man that made not God his strength; but trusted in the abundance of his riches, and strengthened himself in his wickedness.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 8] But I am like a green olive tree in the house of God: I trust in the mercy of God for ever and ever.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 9] I will praise thee for ever, because thou hast done it : and I will wait on thy name; for it is good before thy saints.
