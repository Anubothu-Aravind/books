[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 1] To the chief Musician, a Psalm of David. Hear my voice, O God, in my prayer: preserve my life from fear of the enemy.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 2] Hide me from the secret counsel of the wicked; from the insurrection of the workers of iniquity:
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 3] Who whet their tongue like a sword, and bend their bows to shoot their arrows, even bitter words:
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 4] That they may shoot in secret at the perfect: suddenly do they shoot at him, and fear not.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 5] They encourage themselves in an evil matter: they commune of laying snares privily; they say, Who will see them?
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 6] They search out iniquities; they accomplish a diligent search: both the inward thought of every one of them , and the heart, is deep.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 7] But God shall shoot at them with an arrow; suddenly shall they be wounded.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 8] So they shall make their own tongue to fall upon themselves: all that see them shall flee away.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 9] And all men shall fear, and shall declare the work of God; for they shall wisely consider of his doing.
[WEBSTER | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 10] The righteous shall be glad in the LORD, and shall trust in him; and all the upright in heart shall glory.
