[BBE | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 18 | Verse 8] Then the king of Israel sent for one of his unsexed servants and said, Go quickly and come back with Micaiah, the son of Imla.
[BBE | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 18 | Verse 9] Now the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat, the king of Judah, were seated on their seats of authority, dressed in their robes, by the doorway into Samaria; and all the prophets were acting as prophets before them.
[BBE | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 18 | Verse 10] And Zedekiah, the son of Chenaanah, made himself iron horns and said, The Lord says, Pushing back the Aramaeans with these, you will put an end to them completely.
[BBE | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 18 | Verse 11] And all the prophets said the same thing, saying, Go up to Ramoth-gilead, and it will go well for you, for the Lord will give it into the hands of the king.
[BBE | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 18 | Verse 12] Now the servant who had gone to get Micaiah said to him, See now, all the prophets with one voice are saying good things to the king; so let your words be like theirs, and say good things.
[BBE | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 18 | Verse 13] And Micaiah said, By the living Lord, whatever the Lord says to me I will say.
[BBE | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 18 | Verse 14] When he came to the king, the king said to him, Micaiah, are we to go to Ramoth-gilead to make war or not? And he said, Go up, and it will go well for you; and they will be given up into your hands.
[BBE | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 18 | Verse 15] And the king said to him, Have I not, again and again, put you on your oath to say nothing to me but what is true in the name of the Lord?
[BBE | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 18 | Verse 16] Then he said, I saw all Israel wandering on the mountains like sheep without a keeper; and the Lord said, These have no master: let them go back, every man to his house in peace.
[BBE | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 18 | Verse 17] And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, Did I not say that he would not be a prophet of good to me, but of evil?
[BBE | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 18 | Verse 18] Then he said, Give ear now to the word of the Lord: I saw the Lord seated on his seat of power, and all the army of heaven in their places, at his right hand and at his left.
[BBE | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 18 | Verse 19] And the Lord said, How may Ahab, king of Israel, be tricked into going up to Ramoth-gilead to his death? And one said one thing and one another.

[BBE | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 18 | Verse 21] And he said, I will go out and be a spirit of deceit in the mouth of all his prophets. And the Lord said, Your trick will have its effect on him: go out and do so.
[BBE | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 18 | Verse 22] And now, see, the Lord has put a spirit of deceit in the mouth of these prophets of yours; and the Lord has said evil against you.
[BBE | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 18 | Verse 23] Then Zedekiah, the son of Chenaanah, came near and gave Micaiah a blow on the side of his face, saying, Where is the spirit of the Lord whose word is in you?
[BBE | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 18 | Verse 24] And Micaiah said, Truly, you will see on that day when you go into an inner room to keep yourself safe.
[BBE | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 18 | Verse 25] And the king of Israel said, Take Micaiah and send him back to Amon, the ruler of the town, and to Joash, the king's son;
[BBE | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 18 | Verse 26] And say, By the king's order this man is to be put in prison, and given prison food till I come back in peace.
[BBE | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 18 | Verse 27] And Micaiah said, If you come back at all in peace, the Lord has not sent his word by me.
[BBE | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 18 | Verse 28] So the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat, the king of Judah, went up to Ramoth-gilead.
[BBE | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 18 | Verse 29] And the king of Israel said to Jehoshaphat, I will make a change in my clothing, so that I do not seem to be the king, and will go into the fight; but do you put on your robes. [BBE | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 18 | Verse 30] Now the king of Aram had given orders to the captains of his war-carriages, saying, Make no attack on small or great, but only on the king of Israel.

[BBE | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 18 | Verse 32] Now when the captains of the war-carriages saw that he was not the king of Israel, they went back from going after him.

[BBE | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 18 | Verse 34] But the fight became more violent while the day went on; and the king of Israel was supported in his war-carriage facing the Aramaeans till the evening; and by sundown he was dead.
