[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 25 | Verse 1] Now when Israel was living in Shittim the people became false to the Lord, doing evil with the daughters of Moab:
[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 25 | Verse 2] For they sent for the people to be present at the offerings made to their gods; and the people took part in their feasts and gave honour to their gods.
[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 25 | Verse 3] So Israel had relations with the women of Moab in honour of the Baal of Peor: and the Lord was moved to wrath against Israel.
[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 25 | Verse 4] Then the Lord said to Moses, Take all the chiefs of the people, hanging them up in the sun before the Lord, so that the wrath of the Lord may be turned from Israel.
[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 25 | Verse 5] So Moses said to the judges of Israel, Let everyone put to death those of his men who have had relations with the women of Moab in honour of the Baal of Peor.
[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 25 | Verse 6] Then one of the children of Israel came to his brothers, taking with him a woman of Midian, before the eyes of Moses and all the meeting of the people, while they were weeping at the door of the Tent of meeting.
[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 25 | Verse 7] And Phinehas, the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, seeing it, got up from among the people and took a spear in his hand,
[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 25 | Verse 8] And went after the man of Israel into the tent, driving the spear through the two of them, through the man of Israel and through the stomach of the woman. So the disease was stopped among the children of Israel.
[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 25 | Verse 9] But twenty-four thousand of them had come to their death by the disease.
[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 25 | Verse 10] And the Lord said to Moses,
[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 25 | Verse 11] Through Phinehas, and because of his passion for my honour, my wrath has been turned away from the children of Israel, so that I have not sent destruction on them all in my wrath.
[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 25 | Verse 12] So say to them that I will make with him an agreement of peace:
[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 25 | Verse 13] And by this agreement, he and his sons after him have the right to be priests for ever; because, by his care for the honour of his God, he took away the sin of the children of Israel.
[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 25 | Verse 14] Now the man of Israel who was put to death with the woman of Midian was Zimri, the son of Salu, a chief of one of the families of the Simeonites.
[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 25 | Verse 15] And the woman of Midian who was put to death was Cozbi, the daughter of Zur; he was the head of a family in Midian.
[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 25 | Verse 16] Then the Lord said to Moses,
[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 25 | Verse 17] Take up arms against the Midianites and overcome them;
[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 25 | Verse 18] For they are a danger to you with their false ways, causing sin to come on you in the question of Peor, and because of Cozbi, their sister, the daughter of the chief of Midian, who was put to death at the time of the disease which came on you because of Peor.
