[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 33 | Verse 15] And they went on from Rephidim, and put up their tents in the waste land of Sinai.

[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 33 | Verse 17] And they went on from Kibroth-hattaavah, and put up their tents in Hazeroth.
[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 33 | Verse 18] And they went on from Hazeroth, and put up their tents in Rithmah.
[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 33 | Verse 19] And they went on from Rithmah, and put up their tents in Rimmon-perez.
[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 33 | Verse 20] And they went on from Rimmon-perez, and put up their tents in Libnah.
[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 33 | Verse 21] And they went on from Libnah, and put up their tents in Rissah.
[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 33 | Verse 22] And they went on from Rissah, and put up their tents in Kehelathah.
[BBE | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 33 | Verse 23] And they went on from Kehelathah, and put up their tents in Mount Shepher.
