[BBE | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 24 | Verse 8] Then Moses took the blood and let it come on the people, and said, This blood is the sign of the agreement which the Lord has made with you in these words.

[BBE | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 24 | Verse 18] And Moses went up the mountain, into the cloud, and was there for forty days and forty nights.
