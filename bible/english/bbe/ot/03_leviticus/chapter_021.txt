[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 21 | Verse 8] And he is to be holy in your eyes, for by him the bread of your God is offered; he is to be holy in your eyes, for I the Lord, who make you holy, am holy.

[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 21 | Verse 10] And he who is the chief priest among his brothers, on whose head the holy oil has been put, who is marked out to put on the holy robes, may not let his hair go loose or have his clothing out of order as a sign of sorrow.

[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 21 | Verse 12] He may not go out of the holy place or make the holy place of his God common; for the crown of the holy oil of his God is on him: I am the Lord.

[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 21 | Verse 23] But he may not go inside the veil or come near the altar, because he is damaged; and he may not make my holy places common; for I the Lord have made them holy.
