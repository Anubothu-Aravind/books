[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 1] And the Lord said to Moses,
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 2] This is the law of the leper on the day when he is made clean: he is to be taken to the priest;
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 3] And the priest is to go outside the tent-circle; and if, after looking, the priest sees that the mark of the disease has gone from him,
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 4] Then the priest is to give orders to take, for him who is to be made clean, two living clean birds and some cedar wood and red thread and hyssop.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 5] And the priest will give orders for one of the birds to be put to death in a vessel made of earth, over flowing water.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 6] And he will take the living bird and the wood and the red thread and the hyssop and put them in the blood of the bird which was put to death over flowing water.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 7] And shaking it seven times over the man who is to be made clean, he will say that he is clean and will let the living bird go free into the open country.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 8] And he who is to be made clean will have his clothing washed and his hair cut and have a bath, and he will be clean. And after that he will come back to the tent-circle; but he is to keep outside his tent for seven days.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 9] And on the seventh day he is to have all the hair cut off his head and his chin and over his eyes — all his hair is to be cut off — and he will have his clothing washed and his body bathed in water and he will be clean.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 10] And on the eighth day let him take two male lambs, without any marks on them, and one female lamb of the first year, without a mark, and three tenth parts of an ephah of the best meal, mixed with oil, and one log of oil.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 11] And the priest who is making him clean will put the man who is being made clean, together with these things, before the door of the Tent of meeting.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 12] And the priest is to take one of the male lambs and give it as an offering for wrongdoing, and the log of oil, waving them for a wave offering before the Lord;
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 13] And he is to put the male lamb to death in the place where they put to death the sin-offering and the burned offering, in the holy place; for as the sin-offering is the property of the priest, so is the offering for wrongdoing: it is most holy.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 14] And let the priest take some of the blood of the offering for wrongdoing and put it on the point of the right ear of him who is to be made clean, and on the thumb of his right hand and on the great toe of his right foot;
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 15] And take some of the oil and put it in the hollow of his left hand;
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 16] And let the priest put his right finger in the oil which is in his left hand, shaking it out with his finger seven times before the Lord;
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 17] And of the rest of the oil which is in his hand, the priest will put some on the point of the right ear of the man who is to be made clean, and on the thumb of his right hand and on the great toe of his right foot, over the blood of the offering for wrongdoing;
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 18] And the rest of the oil in the priest's hand he will put on the head of him who is to be made clean; and so the priest will make him free from sin before the Lord.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 19] And the priest will give the sin-offering, and take away the sin of him who is to be made clean from his unclean condition; and after that he will put the burned offering to death.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 20] And the priest is to have the burned offering and the meal offering burned on the altar; and the priest will take away his sin and he will be clean.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 21] And if he is poor and not able to get so much, then he may take one male lamb as an offering for wrongdoing, to be waved to take away his sin, and one tenth part of an ephah of the best meal mixed with oil for a meal offering, and a log of oil;
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 22] And two doves or two young pigeons, such as he is able to get; and one will be for a sin-offering and the other for a burned offering.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 23] And on the eighth day he will take them to the priest, to the door of the Tent of meeting before the Lord, so that he may be made clean.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 24] And the priest will take the lamb of the offering for wrongdoing and the oil, waving them for a wave offering before the Lord;
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 25] And he will put to death the lamb of the offering for wrongdoing and the priest will take some of the blood of the offering for wrongdoing and put it on the point of the right ear of him who is to be made clean, and on the thumb of his right hand and on the great toe of his right foot;
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 26] And the priest will put out some of the oil in the hollow of his left hand,
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 27] Shaking out drops of oil with his right finger before the Lord seven times:
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 28] And the priest will put some of the oil which is in his hand on the point of the ear of the man who is to be made clean and on the thumb of his right hand and on the great toe of his right foot, on the place where the blood of the offering for wrongdoing was put;
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 29] And the rest of the oil which is in the priest's hand he will put on the head of him who is to be made clean, to take away his sin before the Lord.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 30] And he will make an offering of one of the doves or the young pigeons, such as he is able to get;
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 31] And of these, he will give one for a sin-offering and one for a burned offering, with the meal offering; and the priest will take away the sin of him who is to be made clean before the Lord.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 32] This is the law for the man who has the disease of the leper on him, and who is not able to get that which is necessary for making himself clean.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 33] And the Lord said to Moses and Aaron,
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 34] When you have come into the land of Canaan which I will give you for your heritage, if I put the leper's disease on a house in the land of your heritage,
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 35] Then let the owner of the house come and say to the priest, It seems to me that there is a sort of leper's disease in the house.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 36] And the priest will give orders for everything to be taken out of the house, before he goes in to see the disease, so that the things in the house may not become unclean; and then the priest is to go in to see the house;
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 37] And if he sees that the walls of the house are marked with hollows of green and red, and if it seems to go deeper than the face of the wall;
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 38] Then the priest will go out of the door of the house, and keep the house shut up for seven days:
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 39] And the priest is to come again on the seventh day and have a look and see if the marks on the walls of the house are increased in size;
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 40] Then the priest will give orders to them to take out the stones in which the disease is seen, and put them out into an unclean place outside the town:
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 41] And he will have the house rubbed all over inside, and the paste which is rubbed off will be put out into an unclean place outside the town:
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 42] And they will take other stones and put them in place of those stones, and he will take other paste and put it on the walls of the house.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 43] And if the disease comes out again in the house after he has taken out the stones and after the walls have been rubbed and the new paste put on,
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 44] Then the priest will come and see it; and if the disease in the house is increased in size, it is the leper's disease working out in the house: it is unclean.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 45] And the house will have to be pulled down, the stones of it and the wood and the paste; and everything is to be taken out to an unclean place outside the town.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 46] And, in addition, anyone who goes into the house at any time, while it is shut up, will be unclean till evening.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 47] And anyone who has been sleeping in the house will have to have his clothing washed; and anyone who takes food in that house will have to have his clothing washed.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 48] And if the priest comes in, and sees that the disease is not increased after the new paste has been put on the house, then the priest will say that the house is clean, because the disease is gone.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 49] And in order to make the house clean, let him take two birds and cedar-wood and red thread and hyssop;
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 50] And put one of the birds to death in a vessel of earth over flowing water;
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 51] And take the cedar-wood and the hyssop and the red thread and the living bird and put them in the blood of the dead bird and in the flowing water, shaking it over the house seven times.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 52] And he will make the house clean with the blood of the bird and the flowing water and with the living bird and with the cedar-wood and the hyssop and the red thread.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 53] But he will let the living bird go out of the town into the open country; so he will take away sin from the house and it will be clean.
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 54] This is the law for all signs of the leper's disease and for skin diseases;
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 55] And for signs of disease in clothing, or in a house;
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 56] And for a growth or a bad place or a bright mark on the skin;
[BBE | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 14 | Verse 57] To make clear when it is unclean and when it is clean: this is the law about the disease of the leper.
