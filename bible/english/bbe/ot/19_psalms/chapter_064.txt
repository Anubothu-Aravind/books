[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 1] O God, let the voice of my grief come to your ear: keep my life from the fear of those who are against me.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 2] Keep me safe from the secret purpose of wrongdoers; from the band of the workers of evil;
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 3] Who make their tongues sharp like a sword, and whose arrows are pointed, even bitter words;
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 4] So that in secret they may let loose their arrows at the upright, suddenly and unseen.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 5] They make themselves strong in an evil purpose; they make holes for secret nets; they say, Who will see it,
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 6] Or make discovery of our secret purpose? The design is framed with care; and the inner thought of a man, and his heart, is deep.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 7] But God sends out an arrow against them; suddenly they are wounded.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 8] The evil of their tongues is the cause of their fall; all those who see them are shaking their heads at them.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 9] And in fear men make public the works of God; and giving thought to his acts they get wisdom.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 10] The upright will be glad in the Lord and have hope in him; and all the lovers of righteousness will give him glory.
