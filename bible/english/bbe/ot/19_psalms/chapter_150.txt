[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 150 | Verse 1] Let the Lord be praised. Give praise to God in his holy place: give him praise in the heaven of his power.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 150 | Verse 2] Give him praise for his acts of power: give him praise in the measure of his great strength.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 150 | Verse 3] Give him praise with the sound of the horn: give him praise with corded instruments of music.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 150 | Verse 4] Give him praise with instruments of brass and in the dance: give him praise with horns and corded instruments.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 150 | Verse 5] Give him praise with the loud brass: give him praise with the high-sounding brass.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 150 | Verse 6] Let everything which has breath give praise to the Lord. Let the Lord be praised.
