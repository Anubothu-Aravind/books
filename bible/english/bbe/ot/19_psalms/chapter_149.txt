[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 149 | Verse 1] Let the Lord be praised. Make a new song to the Lord, let his praise be in the meeting of his saints.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 149 | Verse 2] Let Israel have joy in his maker; let the children of Zion be glad in their King.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 149 | Verse 3] Let them give praise to his name in the dance: let them make melody to him with instruments of brass and corded instruments of music.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 149 | Verse 4] For the Lord has pleasure in his people: he gives the poor in spirit a crown of salvation.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 149 | Verse 5] Let the saints have joy and glory: let them give cries of joy on their beds.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 149 | Verse 6] Let the high praises of God be in their mouths, and a two-edged sword in their hands;
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 149 | Verse 7] To give the nations the reward of their sins, and the peoples their punishment;
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 149 | Verse 8] To put their kings in chains, and their rulers in bands of iron;
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 149 | Verse 9] To give them the punishment which is in the holy writings: this honour is given to all his saints. Praise be to the Lord.
