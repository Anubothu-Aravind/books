[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 35 | Verse 2] Be a breastplate to me, and give me your help.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 35 | Verse 3] Take up your spear and keep back my attackers; say to my soul, I am your salvation.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 35 | Verse 4] Let them be overcome and put to shame who make attempts to take my soul; let those who would do me damage be turned back and made foolish.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 35 | Verse 5] Let them be like dust from the grain before the wind; let the angel of the Lord send them in flight.

[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 35 | Verse 7] For without cause they have put a net ready for me secretly, in which to take my soul.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 35 | Verse 8] Let destruction come on them without their knowledge; let them be taken themselves in their secret nets, falling into the same destruction.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 35 | Verse 9] And my soul will have joy in the Lord; it will be glad in his salvation.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 35 | Verse 10] All my bones will say, Lord, who is like you? The saviour of the poor man from the hands of the strong, of him who is poor and in need from him who takes his goods.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 35 | Verse 11] False witnesses got up: they put questions to me about crimes of which I had no knowledge.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 35 | Verse 12] They gave me back evil for good, troubling my soul.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 35 | Verse 13] But as for me, when they were ill I put on the clothing of sorrow: I went without food and was sad, and my prayer came back again to my heart.

[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 35 | Verse 16] Like men of deceit they put me to shame; the voice of their wrath was loud against me.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 35 | Verse 17] Lord, how long will you be looking on? take my soul from their destruction, my life from the lions.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 35 | Verse 18] I will give you praise in the great meeting; I will give you honour among a strong people.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 35 | Verse 19] Do not let my haters be glad over me falsely; let not those who are against me without cause make sport of me.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 35 | Verse 20] For they do not say words of peace; in their deceit they are designing evil things against the quiet ones in the land.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 35 | Verse 21] Their mouths were open wide against me, and they said, Aha, aha, our eyes have seen it.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 35 | Verse 22] You have seen this, O Lord; be not unmoved: O Lord, be not far from me.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 35 | Verse 23] Be awake, O Lord, be moved to take up my cause, my God and my Lord.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 35 | Verse 24] Be my judge, O Lord my God, in your righteousness; do not let them be glad over me.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 35 | Verse 25] Let them not say in their hearts, So we will have it: let them not say, We have put an end to him.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 35 | Verse 26] Let all those who take pleasure in my troubles be shamed and come to nothing: let those who are lifted up against me be covered with shame and have no honour.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 35 | Verse 27] Let those who are on my side give cries of joy; let them ever say, The Lord be praised, for he has pleasure in the peace of his servant.
[BBE | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 35 | Verse 28] And my tongue will be talking of your righteousness and of your praise all the day.
