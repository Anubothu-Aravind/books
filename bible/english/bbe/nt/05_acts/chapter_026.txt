[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 1] And Agrippa said to Paul, You may put your cause before us. Then Paul, stretching out his hand, made his answer, saying:
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 2] In my opinion I am happy, King Agrippa, to be able to give my answer before you today to all these things which the Jews say against me:
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 3] The more so, because you are expert in all questions to do with the Jews and their ways: so I make my request to you to give me a hearing to the end.
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 4] All the Jews have knowledge of my way of life from my early years, as it was from the start among my nation, and at Jerusalem;
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 5] And they are able to say, if they would give witness, that I was living as a Pharisee, in that division of our religion which is most regular in the keeping of the law.
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 6] And now I am here to be judged because of the hope given by God's word to our fathers;
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 7] For the effecting of which our twelve tribes have been working and waiting night and day with all their hearts. And in connection with this hope I am attacked by the Jews, O king!
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 8] Why, in your opinion, is it outside belief for God to make the dead come to life again?
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 9] For I, truly, was of the opinion that it was right for me to do a number of things against the name of Jesus of Nazareth.
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 10] And this I did in Jerusalem: and numbers of the saints I put in prison, having had authority given to me from the chief priests, and when they were put to death, I gave my decision against them.
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 11] And I gave them punishment frequently, in all the Synagogues, forcing them to say things against God; and burning with passion against them, I went after them even into far-away towns.
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 12] Then, when I was journeying to Damascus with the authority and orders of the chief priests,
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 13] In the middle of the day, on the road I saw a light from heaven, brighter than the sun, shining round me and those who were journeying with me.
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 14] And when we had all gone down on the earth, a voice came to me, saying in the Hebrew language, Saul, Saul, why are you attacking me so cruelly? It is hard for you to go against the impulse which is driving you.
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 15] And I said, Who are you, Lord? And the Lord said, I am Jesus, whom you are attacking.
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 16] But get up on your feet: for I have come to you for this purpose, to make you a servant and a witness of the things in which you have seen me, and of those in which you will see me;
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 17] And I will keep you safe from the people, and from the Gentiles, to whom I send you,
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 18] To make their eyes open, turning them from the dark to the light, and from the power of Satan to God, so that they may have forgiveness of sins and a heritage among those who are made holy by faith in me.
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 19] So, then, King Agrippa, I did not go against the vision from heaven;
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 20] But I went about, first to those in Damascus and Jerusalem, and through all the country of Judaea, and then to the Gentiles, preaching a change of heart, so that they, being turned to God, might give, in their works, the fruits of a changed heart.
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 21] For this reason, the Jews took me in the Temple, and made an attempt to put me to death.
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 22] And so, by God's help, I am here today, witnessing to small and great, saying nothing but what the prophets and Moses said would come about;
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 23] That the Christ would go through pain, and being the first to come back from the dead, would give light to the people and to the Gentiles.
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 24] And when he made his answer in these words, Festus said in a loud voice, Paul, you are off your head; your great learning has made you unbalanced.
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 25] Then Paul said, I am not off my head, most noble Festus, but my words are true and wise.
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 26] For the king has knowledge of these things, to whom I am talking freely; being certain that all this is common knowledge to him; for it has not been done in secret.
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 27] King Agrippa, have you faith in the prophets? I am certain that you have.
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 28] And Agrippa said to Paul, A little more and you will be making me a Christian.
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 29] And Paul said, It is my prayer to God that, in little or great measure, not only you, but all those hearing me today might be even as I am, but for these chains.
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 30] And the king and the ruler and Bernice and those who were seated with them got up;
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 31] And when they had gone away they said to one another, This man has done nothing which might give cause for death or prison.
[BBE | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 26 | Verse 32] And Agrippa said to Festus, This man might have been made free, if he had not put his cause before Caesar.
