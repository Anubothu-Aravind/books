[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 1] Then Pilate took Jesus and had him whipped with cords.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 2] And the men of the army made a crown of thorns and put it on his head, and they put a purple robe on him.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 3] And they kept coming and saying, Long life to the King of the Jews! And they gave him blows with their hands.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 4] And Pilate went out again and said to them, See, I let him come out to you to make it clear to you that I see no wrong in him.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 5] Then Jesus came out with the crown of thorns and the purple robe. And Pilate said to them, Here is the man!
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 6] So when the chief priests and the police saw him they gave a loud cry, To the cross! to the cross! Pilate said to them, Take him yourselves and put him on the cross: I see no crime in him.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 7] And the Jews made answer, We have a law, and by that law it is right for him to be put to death because he said he was the Son of God.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 8] When this saying came to Pilate's ears his fear became greater;
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 9] And he went again into the Praetorium and said to Jesus, Where do you come from? But Jesus gave him no answer.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 10] Then Pilate said to him, You say nothing to me? is it not clear to you that I have power to let you go free and power to put you to death on the cross?
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 11] Jesus gave this answer: You would have no power at all over me if it was not given to you by God; so that he who gave me up to you has the greater sin.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 12] Hearing this, Pilate had a desire to let him go free, but the Jews said in a loud voice, If you let this man go, you are not Caesar's friend: everyone who makes himself a king goes against Caesar.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 13] So when these words came to Pilate's ear, he took Jesus out, seating himself in the judge's seat in a place named in Hebrew, Gabbatha, or the Stone Floor.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 14] (It was the day when they made ready for the Passover; and it was about the sixth hour.) And he said to the Jews, There is your King!
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 15] Then they gave a loud cry, Away with him! away with him! to the cross! Pilate said to them, Am I to put your King to death on the cross? The chief priests said in answer, We have no king but Caesar.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 16] So then he gave him up to them to be put to death on the cross. And they took Jesus away;
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 17] And he went out with his cross on him to the place which is named Dead Man's Head (in Hebrew, Golgotha):
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 18] Where they put him on the cross with two others, one on this side and one on that, and Jesus in the middle.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 19] And Pilate put on the cross a statement in writing. The writing was: JESUS THE NAZARENE, THE KING OF THE JEWS.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 20] The writing was seen by a number of the Jews, for the place where Jesus was put to death on the cross was near the town; and the writing was in Hebrew and Latin and Greek.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 21] Then the chief priests of the Jews said to Pilate, Do not put, The King of the Jews, but, He said, I am the King of the Jews.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 22] But Pilate made answer, What I have put in writing will not be changed.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 23] And when Jesus was nailed to the cross, the men of the army took his clothing, and made a division of it into four parts, to every man a part, and they took his coat: now the coat was without a join, made out of one bit of cloth.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 24] So they said among themselves, Let this not be cut up, but let us put it to the decision of chance and see who gets it. (They did this so that the Writings might come true, which say, They made a distribution of my clothing among them, and my coat they put to the decision of chance.) This was what the men of the army did.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 25] Now by the side of the cross of Jesus were his mother, and his mother's sister Mary, the wife of Cleopas, and Mary Magdalene.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 26] So when Jesus saw his mother and the disciple who was dear to him, he said to his mother, Mother, there is your son!
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 27] Then he said to the disciple, There is your mother! And from that hour the disciple took her to his house.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 28] After this, being conscious that all things had now been done so that the Writings might come true, Jesus said, Give me water.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 29] Now there was a vessel ready, full of bitter wine, and they put a sponge full of it on a stick and put it to his mouth.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 30] So when Jesus had taken the wine he said, All is done. And with his head bent he gave up his spirit.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 31] Now it was the day of getting ready for the Passover, and so that the bodies might not be on the cross on the Sabbath (because the day of that Sabbath was a great day), the Jews made a request to Pilate that their legs might be broken, and that they might be taken away.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 32] So the men of the army came, and the legs of the first were broken and then of the other who was put to death on the cross with Jesus:
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 33] But when they came to Jesus, they saw that he was dead by this time, and so his legs were not broken;
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 34] But one of the men made a wound in his side with a spear, and straight away there came out blood and water.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 35] And he who saw it has given witness (and his witness is true; he is certain that what he says is true) so that you may have belief.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 36] These things came about so that the Writings might be true, No bone of his body will be broken.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 37] And again another verse says, They will see him who was wounded by their spears.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 38] After these things, Joseph of Arimathaea, who was a disciple of Jesus, but secretly for fear of the Jews, made a request to Pilate to let him take away the body of Jesus: and Pilate said he might do so. So he went and took away his body.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 39] And Nicodemus came (he who had first come to Jesus by night) with a roll of myrrh and aloes mixed, about a hundred pounds.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 40] Then they took the body of Jesus, folding linen about it with the spices, as is the way of the Jews when they put the dead to rest.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 41] Now there was a garden near the cross, and in the garden a new place for the dead in which no man had ever been put.
[BBE | EN | NT | John | Chapter 19 | Verse 42] So they put Jesus there, because it was the Jews' day of getting ready for the Passover, and the place was near.
