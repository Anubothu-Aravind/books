[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 1] My brothers, if you have the faith of our Lord Jesus Christ of glory, do not take a man's position into account.
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 2] For if a man comes into your Synagogue in fair clothing and with a gold ring, and a poor man comes in with dirty clothing,
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 3] And you do honour to the man in fair clothing and say, Come here and take this good place; and you say to the poor man, Take up your position there, or be seated at my feet;
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 4] Is there not a division in your minds? have you not become judges with evil thoughts?
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 5] Give ear, my dear brothers; are not those who are poor in the things of this world marked out by God to have faith as their wealth, and for their heritage the kingdom which he has said he will give to those who have love for him?
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 6] But you have put the poor man to shame. Are not the men of wealth rulers over you? do they not take you by force before their judges?
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 7] Do they not say evil of the holy name which was given to you?
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 8] But if you keep the greatest law of all, as it is given in the holy Writings, Have love for your neighbour as for yourself, you do well:
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 9] But if you take a man's position into account, you do evil, and are judged as evil-doers by the law.
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 10] For anyone who keeps all the law, but makes a slip in one point, is judged to have gone against it all.
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 11] For he who said, Do not be untrue in married life, is the same who said, Put no man to death. Now if you are not untrue in married life, but you put a man to death, the law is broken.
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 12] Let your words and your acts be those of men who are to be judged by the law which makes free.
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 13] For the man who has had no mercy will be judged without mercy, but mercy takes pride in overcoming judging.
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 14] What use is it, my brothers, for a man to say that he has faith, if he does nothing? will such a faith give him salvation?
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 15] If a brother or a sister is without clothing and in need of the day's food,
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 16] And one of you says to them, Go in peace, be warm and full of food; but you do not give them the things of which their bodies have need, what profit is there in this?
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 17] Even so faith without works is dead.
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 18] But a man may say, You have faith and I have works; let me see your faith without your works, and I will make my faith clear to you by my works.
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 19] You have the belief that God is one, and you do well: the evil spirits have the same belief, shaking with fear.
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 20] Do you not see, O foolish man, that faith without works is of no use?
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 21] Was not the righteousness of Abraham our father judged by his works, when he made an offering of Isaac his son on the altar?
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 22] You see that his faith was helping his works and was made complete by them;
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 23] And the holy Writings were put into effect which said, And Abraham had faith in God and it was put to his account as righteousness; and he was named the friend of God.
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 24] You see that a man's righteousness is judged by his works and not by his faith only.
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 25] And in the same way, was not the righteousness of Rahab, the loose woman, judged by her works, when she took into her house those who were sent and let them go out by another way?
[BBE | EN | NT | James | Chapter 2 | Verse 26] For as the body without the spirit is dead even so faith without works is dead.
