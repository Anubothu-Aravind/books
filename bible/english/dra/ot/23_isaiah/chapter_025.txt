[DRA | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 2] For thou hast reduced the city to a heap, the strong city to ruin, the house of strangers, to be no city, and to be no more built up for ever.
[DRA | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 3] Therefore shall a strong people praise thee, the city of mighty nations shall fear thee.
[DRA | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 4] Because thou hast been a strength to the poor, a strength to the needy in his distress: a refuge from the whirlwind, a shadow from the heat. For the blast of the mighty is like a whirlwind beating against a wall.
[DRA | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 5] Thou shalt bring down the tumult of strangers, as heat in thirst: and as with heat under a burning cloud, thou shalt make the branch of the mighty to wither away.
[DRA | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 6] And the Lord of hosts shall make unto all people in this mountain, a feast of fat things, a feast of wine, of fat things full of marrow, of wine purified from the lees.
[DRA | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 7] And he shall destroy in this mountain the face of the bond with which all people were tied, and the web that he began over all nations.
[DRA | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 8] He shall cast death down headlong for ever: and the Lord God shall wipe away tears from every face, and the reproach of his people he shall take away from off the whole earth: for the Lord hath spoken it.
[DRA | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 9] And they shall say in that day: Lo, this is our God, we have waited for him, and he will save us: this is the Lord, we have patiently waited for him, we shall rejoice and be joyfull in his salvation.
[DRA | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 10] For the hand of the Lord shall rest in this mountain: and Moab shall be trodden down under him, as straw is broken in pieces with the wain.
[DRA | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 11] And he shall stretch forth his hands under him, as he that swimmeth stretcheth forth his hands to swim: and he shall bring down his glory with the dashing of his hands.
[DRA | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 12] And the bulwarks of thy high walls shall fall, and be brought low, and shall be pulled down to the ground, even to the dust.
