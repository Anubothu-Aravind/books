[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 70 | Verse 1] A psalm for David. Of the sons of Jonadab, and the former captives. In thee, O Lord, I have hoped, let me never be put to confusion:
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 70 | Verse 2] Deliver me in thy justice, and rescue me. Incline thy ear unto me, and save me.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 70 | Verse 3] Be thou unto me a God, a protector, and a place of strength: that thou mayst make me safe. For thou art my firmament and my refuge.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 70 | Verse 4] Deliver me, O my God, out of the hand of the sinner, and out of the hand of the transgressor of the law and of the unjust.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 70 | Verse 5] For thou art my patience, O Lord: my hope, O Lord, from my youth;
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 70 | Verse 6] By thee have I been confirmed from the womb: from my mother’s womb thou art my protector. Of thee shall I continually sing:
