[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 28 | Verse 1] A psalm for David, at the finishing of the tabernacle. Bring to the Lord, O ye children of God: bring to the Lord the offspring of rams.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 28 | Verse 2] Bring to the Lord glory and honour: bring to the Lord glory to his name: adore ye the Lord in his holy court.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 28 | Verse 3] The voice of the Lord is upon the waters; the God of majesty hath thundered, The Lord is upon many waters.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 28 | Verse 4] The voice of the Lord is in power; the voice of the Lord in magnificence.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 28 | Verse 5] The voice of the Lord breaketh the cedars: yea, the Lord shall break the cedars of Libanus.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 28 | Verse 6] And shall reduce them to pieces, as a calf of Libanus, and as the beloved son of unicorns.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 28 | Verse 7] The voice of the Lord divideth the flame of fire:
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 28 | Verse 8] The voice of the Lord shaketh the desert: and the Lord shall shake the desert of Cades.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 28 | Verse 9] The voice of the Lord prepareth the stags: and he will discover the thick woods: and in his temple all shall speak his glory.
