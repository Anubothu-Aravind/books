[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 14 | Verse 1] A psalm of David. Lord, who shall dwell in thy tabernacle? or who shall rest in thy holy hill?
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 14 | Verse 2] He that walketh without blemish, and worketh justice:
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 14 | Verse 3] He that speaketh truth in his heart, who hath not used deceit in his tongue: Nor hath done evil to his neighbour: nor taken up a reproach against his neighbours.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 14 | Verse 4] In his sight the malignant is brought to nothing: but he glorifieth them that fear the Lord. He that sweareth to his neighbour, and deceiveth not;
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 14 | Verse 5] He that hath not put out his money to usury, nor taken bribes against the innocent: He that doth these things shall not be moved for ever.
