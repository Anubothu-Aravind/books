[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 69 | Verse 1] Unto the end, a psalm for David, to bring to remembrance that the Lord saved him.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 69 | Verse 2] O God, come to my assistance; O Lord, make haste to help me.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 69 | Verse 3] Let them be confounded and ashamed that seek my soul:
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 69 | Verse 4] Let them be turned backward, and blush for shame that desire evils to me: Let them be presently turned away blushing for shame that say to me: Tis well, tis well.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 69 | Verse 5] Let all that seek thee rejoice and be glad in thee; and let such as love thy salvation say always: The Lord be magnified.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 69 | Verse 6] But I am needy and poor; O God, help me. Thou art my helper and my deliverer: O Lord, make no delay.
