[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 20 | Verse 1] Unto the end. A psalm for David.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 20 | Verse 2] In thy strength, O Lord, the king shall joy; and in thy salvation he shall rejoice exceedingly.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 20 | Verse 3] Thou hast given him his heart’s desire: and hast not withholden from him the will of his lips.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 20 | Verse 4] For thou hast prevented him with blessings of sweetness: thou hast set on his head a crown of precious stones.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 20 | Verse 5] He asked life of thee: and thou hast given him length of days for ever and ever.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 20 | Verse 6] His glory is great in thy salvation: glory and great beauty shalt thou lay upon him.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 20 | Verse 7] For thou shalt give him to be a blessing for ever and ever: thou shalt make him joyful in gladness with thy countenance.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 20 | Verse 8] For the king hopeth in the Lord: and through the mercy of the most High he shall not be moved.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 20 | Verse 9] Let thy hand be found by all thy enemies: let thy right hand find out all them that hate thee.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 20 | Verse 10] Thou shalt make them as an oven of fire, in the time of thy anger: the Lord shall trouble them in his wrath, and fire shall devour them.
