[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 13 | Verse 1] Unto the end, a psalm for David. The fool hath said in his heart: There is no God, They are corrupt, and are become abominable in their ways: there is none that doth good, no not one.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 13 | Verse 2] The Lord hath looked down from heaven upon the children of men, to see if there be any that understand and seek God.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 13 | Verse 3] They are all gone aside, they are become unprofitable together: there is none that doth good, no not one. Their throat is an open sepulchre: with their tongues they acted deceitfully; the poison of asps is under their lips. Their mouth is full of cursing and bitterness; their feet are swift to shed blood. Destruction and unhappiness in their ways: and the way of peace they have not known: there is no fear of God before their eyes.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 13 | Verse 4] Shall not all they know that work iniquity, who devour my people as they eat bread?
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 13 | Verse 5] They have not called upon the Lord: there have they trembled for fear, where there was no fear.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 13 | Verse 6] For the Lord is in the just generation: you have confounded the counsel of the poor man, but the Lord is his hope.
