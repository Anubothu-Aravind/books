[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 81 | Verse 1] A psalm for Asaph. God hath stood in the congregation of gods: and being in the midst of them he judgeth gods.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 81 | Verse 2] How long will you judge unjustly: and accept the persons of the wicked?
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 81 | Verse 3] Judge for the needy and fatherless: do justice to the humble and the poor.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 81 | Verse 4] Rescue the poor; and deliver the needy out of the hand of the sinner.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 81 | Verse 5] They have not known nor understood: they walk on in darkness: all the foundations of the earth shall be moved.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 81 | Verse 6] I have said: You are gods and all of you the sons of the most High.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 81 | Verse 7] But you like men shall die: and shall fall like one of the princes.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 81 | Verse 8] Arise, O God, judge thou the earth: for thou shalt inherit among all the nations.
