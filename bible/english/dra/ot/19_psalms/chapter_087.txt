[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 87 | Verse 4] For my soul is filled with evils: and my life hath drawn nigh to hell.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 87 | Verse 5] I am counted among them that go down to the pit: I am become as a man without help,
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 87 | Verse 6] Free among the dead. Like the slain sleeping in the sepulchres, whom thou rememberest no more: and they are cast off from thy hand.
