[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 147 | Verse 1] Praise the Lord, O Jerusalem: praise thy God, O Sion.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 147 | Verse 2] Because he hath strengthened the bolts of thy gates, he hath blessed thy children within thee.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 147 | Verse 3] Who hath placed peace in thy borders: and filleth thee with the fat of corn.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 147 | Verse 4] Who sendeth forth his speech to the earth: his word runneth swiftly.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 147 | Verse 5] Who giveth snow like wool: scattereth mists like ashes.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 147 | Verse 6] He sendeth his crystal like morsels: who shall stand before the face of his cold?
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 147 | Verse 7] He shall send out his word, and shall melt them: his wind shall blow, and the waters shall run.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 147 | Verse 8] Who declareth his word to Jacob: his justices and his judgments to Israel.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 147 | Verse 9] He hath not done in like manner to every nation: and his judgments he hath not made manifest to them. Alleluia.
