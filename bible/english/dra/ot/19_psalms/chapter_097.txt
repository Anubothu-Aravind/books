[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 97 | Verse 1] A psalm for David himself. Sing ye to the Lord anew canticle: because he hath done wonderful things. His right hand hath wrought for him salvation, and his arm is holy.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 97 | Verse 2] The Lord hath made known his salvation: he hath revealed his justice in the sight of the Gentiles.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 97 | Verse 3] He hath remembered his mercy his truth toward the house of Israel. All the ends of the earth have seen the salvation of our God.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 97 | Verse 4] Sing joyfully to God, all the earth; make melody, rejoice and sing.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 97 | Verse 5] Sing praise to the Lord on the harp, on the harp, and with the voice of a psalm:
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 97 | Verse 6] With long trumpets, and sound of comet. Make a joyful noise before the Lord our king:
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 97 | Verse 7] Let the sea be moved and the fulness thereof: the world and they that dwell therein.
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 97 | Verse 8] The rivers shall clap their hands, the mountains shall rejoice together
[DRA | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 97 | Verse 9] At the presence of the Lord: because he cometh to judge the earth. He shall judge the world with justice, and the people with equity.
