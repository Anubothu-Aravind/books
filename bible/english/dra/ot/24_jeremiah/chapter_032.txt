[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 2] At that time the army of the king o Babylon besieged Jerusalem: and Jeremias the prophet was shut up in the court of the prison, which was in the house of the king of Juda.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 3] For Sedecias king of Juda had shut him up, saying: Why dost thou prophesy, saying: Thus saith the Lord: Behold I will give this city into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he shall take it?
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 4] And Sedecias king of Juda shall not escape out of the hand of the Chaldeans: but he shall be delivered into the hands of the king of Babylon: and he shall speak to him mouth to mouth, and his eyes shall see his eyes.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 5] And he shall lead Sedecias to Babylon: and he shall be there till I visit him, saith the Lord. But if you will fight against the Chaldeans, you shall have no success.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 6] And Jeremias said: The word of the Lord came to me, saying:
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 7] Behold, Hanameel the son of Sellum thy cousin shall come to thee, saying: Buy thee my field, which is in Anathoth, for it is thy right to buy it, being akin.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 8] And Hanameel my uncle’s son cam to me, according to the word of the to the entry of the prison, and said me: Buy my held, which is in in the land of Benjamin: for the right of inheritance is thins, and thou art next of kin to possess it. And I understood this was the word of the Lord.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 9] And I bought the held of my uncle’s son, that is in Anathoth: and I weighed him the money, seven staters, and ten pieces of silver.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 10] And I wrote it in a book and sealed it, and took witnesses: and I weighed him the money in the balances.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 11] And I took the deed of the purchase that was sealed, and the stipulations, and the ratifications with the seals that were on the outside.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 12] And I gave the deed of the purchase to Baruch the son of Neri the son of Maasias in the sight of Hanameel my uncle’s son, in the presence of the witnesses that subscribed the book of the purchase, and before all the Jews that sat in the court of the prison.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 13] And I charged Baruch before them, saying:
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 14] Thus saith the Lord of hosts the God of Israel: Take these writings, this deed of the purchase that is sealed up, and this deed that is open: and put them in an earthen vessel, that they may continue many days.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 15] For thus saith the Lord of hosts the God of Israel: Houses, and fields, and vineyards shall be possessed again in this land.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 16] And after I had delivered the deed of purchase to Baruch the son of Neri, I prayed to the Lord, saying:
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 17] Alas, alas, alas, O Lord God, behold thou hast made heaven and earth by thy great power, and thy stretched out arm: no word shall be hard to thee:
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 18] Thou shewest mercy unto thousands, and returnest the iniquity of the fathers into the bosom of their children after them: O most mighty, great, and powerful, the Lord of hosts is thy name.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 19] Great in counsel and incomprehensible in thought: whose eyes are open upon all the ways of the children of Adam, to render unto every one according to his ways, and according to the fruit of his devices.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 20] Who hast set signs and wonders in the land of Egypt even until this day, and in Israel, and amongst men, and hast made thee a name as at this day.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 21] And hast brought forth thy people Israel, out of the land of Egypt with signs, and with wonders, and with a strong hand, and a stretched out arm, and with great terror.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 22] And best given them this land which thou didst swear to their fathers, to give them a land flowing with milk and honey.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 23] And they came in, and possessed it: but they obeyed not thy voice, and they walked not in thy law: and they did not any of those things that thou didst command them to do, and all these evils are come upon them.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 24] Behold works are built up against the city to take it: and the city is given into the hands of the Chaldeans, who fight against it, by the sword, and the famine, and the pestilence: and what thou hast spoken, is all come to pass, as thou thyself seest.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 25] And sayest thou to me, O Lord God: Buy a field for money, and take witnesses, whereas the city is given into the hands of the Chaldeans?
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 26] And the word of the Lord came to Jeremias, saying:
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 27] Behold I am the Lord the God of all flesh: shall any thing be hard for me?
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 28] Therefore thus saith the Lord: Behold I will deliver this city into the hands of the Chaldeans, and into the hands of the king of Babylon, and they shall take it.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 29] And the Chaldeans that fight against this city, shall come and set it on fire, and burn it, with the houses upon whose roofs they offered sacrifice to Baal, and poured out drink offerings to strange gods, to provoke me to wrath.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 30] For the children of Israel, and the children of Juda, have continually done evil in my eyes from their youth: the children of Israel who even till now provoke me with the work of their hands, saith the Lord.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 31] For this city hath been to me a provocation and indignation from the day that they built it, until this day, in which it shall be taken out of my sight.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 32] Because of all the evil of the children of Israel, and of the children of Juda, which they have done, provoking me to wrath, they and their kings, their princes, and their priests, and their prophets, the men of Juda, and the inhabitants of Jerusalem.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 33] And they have turned their backs to me, and not their faces: when I taught them early in the morning, and instructed them, and they would not hearken to receive instruction.

[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 35] And they have built the high places of Baal, which are in the valley of the son of Ennom, to consecrate their sons and their daughters to Moloch: which I commanded them not, neither entered it into my heart, that they should do this abomination, and cause Juda to sin.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 36] And now, therefore, thus saith the Lord the God of Israel to this city, whereof you say that it shall be delivered into the hands of the king of Babylon by the sword, and by famine, and by pestilence:
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 37] Behold I will gather them together out of all the lands to which I have cast them out in my anger, and in my wrath, and in my great indignation: and I will bring them again into this place, and will cause them to dwell securely.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 38] And they shall be my people, and I will be their God.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 39] And I will give them one heart, and one way, that they may fear me all days: and that it may be well with them, and with their children after them.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 40] And I will make an everlasting covenant with them, and will not cease to do them good: and I will give my fear in their heart, that they may not revolt from me.
[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 41] And I will rejoice over them, when I shall do them good: and I will plant them in this land in truth, with my whole heart, and with all my soul.

[DRA | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 32 | Verse 43] And fields shall be purchased in this land: whereof you say that it is desolate, because there remaineth neither man nor beast, and it is given into the hands of the Chaldeans.
