[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 2] Let each one of us please his neighbor for that which is good, to be building him up.
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 3] For even Christ didn’t please himself. But, as it is written, “The reproaches of those who reproached you fell on me.”
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 4] For whatever things were written before were written for our learning, that through perseverance and through encouragement of the Scriptures we might have hope.
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 5] Now the God of perseverance and of encouragement grant you to be of the same mind with one another according to Christ Jesus,
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 6] that with one accord you may with one mouth glorify the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 7] Therefore accept one another, even as Christ also accepted you, to the glory of God.
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 8] Now I say that Christ has been made a servant of the circumcision for the truth of God, that he might confirm the promises given to the fathers,
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 9] and that the Gentiles might glorify God for his mercy. As it is written, “Therefore I will give praise to you among the Gentiles and sing to your name.”
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 10] Again he says, “Rejoice, you Gentiles, with his people.”
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 11] Again, “Praise the Lord, all you Gentiles! Let all the peoples praise him.”
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 12] Again, Isaiah says, “There will be the root of Jesse, he who arises to rule over the Gentiles; in him the Gentiles will hope.”
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 13] Now may the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, that you may abound in hope in the power of the Holy Spirit.
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 14] I myself am also persuaded about you, my brothers, that you yourselves are full of goodness, filled with all knowledge, able also to admonish others.
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 15] But I write the more boldly to you in part as reminding you, because of the grace that was given to me by God,
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 16] that I should be a servant of Christ Jesus to the Gentiles, serving as a priest of the Good News of God, that the offering up of the Gentiles might be made acceptable, sanctified by the Holy Spirit.
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 17] I have therefore my boasting in Christ Jesus in things pertaining to God.
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 18] For I will not dare to speak of any things except those which Christ worked through me for the obedience of the Gentiles, by word and deed,
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 19] in the power of signs and wonders, in the power of God’s Spirit; so that from Jerusalem and around as far as to Illyricum, I have fully preached the Good News of Christ;
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 20] yes, making it my aim to preach the Good News, not where Christ was already named, that I might not build on another’s foundation.
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 21] But, as it is written, “They will see, to whom no news of him came. They who haven’t heard will understand.”
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 22] Therefore also I was hindered these many times from coming to you,
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 23] but now, no longer having any place in these regions, and having these many years a longing to come to you,
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 24] whenever I travel to Spain, I will come to you. For I hope to see you on my journey, and to be helped on my way there by you, if first I may enjoy your company for a while.
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 25] But now, I say, I am going to Jerusalem, serving the saints.
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 26] For it has been the good pleasure of Macedonia and Achaia to make a certain contribution for the poor among the saints who are at Jerusalem.
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 27] Yes, it has been their good pleasure, and they are their debtors. For if the Gentiles have been made partakers of their spiritual things, they owe it to them also to serve them in material things.
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 28] When therefore I have accomplished this, and have sealed to them this fruit, I will go on by way of you to Spain.
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 29] I know that when I come to you, I will come in the fullness of the blessing of the Good News of Christ.
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 30] Now I beg you, brothers, by our Lord Jesus Christ and by the love of the Spirit, that you strive together with me in your prayers to God for me,
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 31] that I may be delivered from those who are disobedient in Judea, and that my service which I have for Jerusalem may be acceptable to the saints,
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 32] that I may come to you in joy through the will of God, and together with you, find rest.
[WEB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 15 | Verse 33] Now the God of peace be with you all. Amen.
