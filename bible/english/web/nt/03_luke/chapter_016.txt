[WEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 16 | Verse 2] He called him, and said to him, ‘What is this that I hear about you? Give an accounting of your management, for you can no longer be manager.’
[WEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 16 | Verse 3] “The manager said within himself, ‘What will I do, seeing that my lord is taking away the management position from me? I don’t have strength to dig. I am ashamed to beg.
[WEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 16 | Verse 4] I know what I will do, so that when I am removed from management, they may receive me into their houses.’
[WEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 16 | Verse 5] Calling each one of his lord’s debtors to him, he said to the first, ‘How much do you owe to my lord?’
[WEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 16 | Verse 6] He said, ‘A hundred batos of oil.’ He said to him, ‘Take your bill, and sit down quickly and write fifty.’

[WEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 16 | Verse 8] “His lord commended the dishonest manager because he had done wisely, for the children of this world are, in their own generation, wiser than the children of the light.
[WEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 16 | Verse 9] I tell you, make for yourselves friends by means of unrighteous mammon, so that when you fail, they may receive you into the eternal tents.
[WEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 16 | Verse 10] He who is faithful in a very little is faithful also in much. He who is dishonest in a very little is also dishonest in much.
[WEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 16 | Verse 11] If therefore you have not been faithful in the unrighteous mammon, who will commit to your trust the true riches?
[WEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 16 | Verse 12] If you have not been faithful in that which is another’s, who will give you that which is your own?
[WEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 16 | Verse 13] No servant can serve two masters, for either he will hate the one and love the other; or else he will hold to one and despise the other. You aren’t able to serve God and Mammon.”
[WEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 16 | Verse 14] The Pharisees, who were lovers of money, also heard all these things, and they scoffed at him.
[WEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 16 | Verse 15] He said to them, “You are those who justify yourselves in the sight of men, but God knows your hearts. For that which is exalted among men is an abomination in the sight of God.

[WEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 16 | Verse 17] But it is easier for heaven and earth to pass away than for one tiny stroke of a pen in the law to fall.
[WEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 16 | Verse 18] “Everyone who divorces his wife and marries another commits adultery. He who marries one who is divorced from a husband commits adultery.

[WEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 16 | Verse 20] A certain beggar, named Lazarus, was taken to his gate, full of sores,

[WEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 16 | Verse 27] “He said, ‘I ask you therefore, father, that you would send him to my father’s house—

[WEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 16 | Verse 30] “He said, ‘No, father Abraham, but if one goes to them from the dead, they will repent.’
[WEB | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 16 | Verse 31] “He said to him, ‘If they don’t listen to Moses and the prophets, neither will they be persuaded if one rises from the dead.’”
