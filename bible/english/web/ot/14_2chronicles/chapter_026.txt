[WEB | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 26 | Verse 22] Now the rest of the acts of Uzziah, first and last, Isaiah the prophet, the son of Amoz, wrote.
[WEB | EN | OT | 2 Chronicles | Chapter 26 | Verse 23] So Uzziah slept with his fathers; and they buried him with his fathers in the field of burial which belonged to the kings, for they said, “He is a leper.” Jotham his son reigned in his place.
