[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 1] Antiochus son of Demetrius the king sent a letter from the islands of the sea to Simon the priest and governor of the Jews, and to all the nation.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 2] Its contents follow: “King Antiochus to Simon the chief priest and governor, and to the nation of the Jews, greetings.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 3] Whereas certain troublemakers have made themselves masters of the kingdom of our fathers, but my purpose is to claim the kingdom, that I may restore it as it was before; and moreover I have raised a multitude of foreign soldiers, and have prepared warships;
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 4] moreover I plan to land in the country, that I may punish those who have destroyed our country, and those who have made many cities in the kingdom desolate;
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 5] now therefore I confirm to you all the tax remissions which the kings who were before me remitted to you, and whatever gifts besides they remitted to you,
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 6] and I permit you to coin money for your country with your own stamp,
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 7] but that Jerusalem and the sanctuary should be free. All the weapons that you have prepared, and the strongholds that you have built, which you have in your possession, let them remain yours.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 8] Every debt owed to the king, and the things that will be owed to the king from henceforth and for evermore, let them be remitted to you.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 9] Moreover, when we have established our kingdom, we will glorify you and your nation and the temple with great glory, so that your glory will be made manifest in all the earth.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 10] In the one hundred seventy-fourth year, Antiochus went into the land of his fathers; and all the forces came together to him, so that there were few men with Tryphon.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 11] King Antiochus pursued him, and he came, as he fled, to Dor, which is by the sea;
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 12] for he knew that troubles had come upon him all at once, and that his forces had deserted him.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 13] Antiochus encamped against Dor, and with him one hundred twenty thousand men of war and eight thousand cavalry.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 14] He surrounded the city, and the ships joined in the attack from the sea. He harassed the city by land and sea, and permitted no one to go out or in.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 15] Numenius and his company came from Rome, having letters to the kings and to the countries, in which were written these things:
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 16] “Lucius, consul of the Romans, to King Ptolemy, greetings.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 17] The Jews’ ambassadors came to us as our friends and allies, to renew the old friendship and alliance, being sent from Simon the high priest, and from the people of the Jews.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 18] Moreover they brought a shield of gold weighing one thousand minas.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 19] It pleased us therefore to write to the kings and to the countries, that they should not seek their harm or fight against them, their cities, and their country, or be allies with those who fight against them.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 20] Moreover, it seemed good to us to receive the shield from them.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 21] If therefore any troublemakers have fled from their country to you, deliver them to Simon the high priest, that he may take vengeance on them according to their law.”
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 22] He wrote the same things to King Demetrius, to Attalus, to Arathes, to Arsaces,
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 23] to all the countries, to Sampsames, to the Spartans, to Delos, to Myndos, to Sicyon, to Caria, to Samos, to Pamphylia, to Lycia, to Halicarnassus, to Rhodes, to Phaselis, to Cos, to Side, to Aradus, Gortyna, Cnidus, Cyprus, and Cyrene.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 24] They also wrote this copy to Simon the high priest.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 25] But King Antiochus encamped against Dor the second day, bringing his forces up to it continually, and making engines of war; and he shut up Tryphon from going in or out.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 26] Simon sent him two thousand chosen men to fight on his side, with silver, gold, and instruments of war in abundance.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 27] He would not receive them, but nullified all the covenants which he had made with him before, and was estranged from him.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 28] He sent to him Athenobius, one of his friends, to confer with him, saying, “You hold possession of Joppa, Gazara, and the citadel that is in Jerusalem, cities of my kingdom.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 29] You have devastated their territory, and done great harm in the land, and control of many places in my kingdom.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 30] Now therefore hand over the cities which you have taken, and the tributes of the places which you have taken control of outside of the borders of Judea;
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 31] or else give me for them five hundred talents of silver; and for the harm that you have done, and the tributes of the cities, another five hundred talents. Otherwise we will come and subdue you.”
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 32] Athenobius, the king’s friend, came to Jerusalem. When he saw the glory of Simon, the cupboard of gold and silver vessels, and his great attendance, he was amazed. He reported to him the king’s words.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 33] Simon answered, and said to him, “We have not taken other men’s land nor do we have possession of that which belongs to others, but of the inheritance of our fathers. However, it had been in possession of our enemies wrongfully for a while.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 34] But we, having opportunity, firmly hold the inheritance of our fathers.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 35] As for Joppa and Gazara, which you demand, they did great harm among the people throughout our country. We will give one hundred talents for them.” Athenobius didn’t answer even one word,
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 36] but returned in a rage to the king, and reported to him these words, and the glory of Simon, and all that he had seen; and the king was exceedingly angry.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 37] Meanwhile, Tryphon embarked on board a ship, and fled to Orthosia.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 38] The king appointed Cendebaeus chief captain of the sea coast, and gave him troops of infantry and cavalry.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 39] He commanded him to encamp against Judea, and he commanded him to build up Kidron, and to fortify the gates, and that he should fight against the people; but the king pursued Tryphon.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 40] So Cendebaeus came to Jamnia and began to provoke the people, and to invade Judea, and to take the people captive and kill them.
[WEB | EN | OT | 1 Maccabees | Chapter 15 | Verse 41] He built Kidron and stationed cavalry and infantry there, to the end that going out they might make raids on the highways of Judea, as the king had commanded him.
