[WEB | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 25 | Verse 8] “‘You shall count off seven Sabbaths of years, seven times seven years; and there shall be to you the days of seven Sabbaths of years, even forty-nine years.

[WEB | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 25 | Verse 13] “‘In this Year of Jubilee each of you shall return to his property.

[WEB | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 25 | Verse 17] You shall not wrong one another, but you shall fear your God; for I am the LORD your God.
[WEB | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 25 | Verse 18] “‘Therefore you shall do my statutes, and keep my ordinances and do them; and you shall dwell in the land in safety.

[WEB | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 25 | Verse 22] You shall sow the eighth year, and eat of the fruits from the old store until the ninth year. Until its fruits come in, you shall eat the old store.
[WEB | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 25 | Verse 23] “‘The land shall not be sold in perpetuity, for the land is mine; for you are strangers and live as foreigners with me.
[WEB | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 25 | Verse 24] In all the land of your possession you shall grant a redemption for the land.
