[WEB | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 18 | Verse 1] Ah, the land of the rustling of wings, which is beyond the rivers of Ethiopia;
[WEB | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 18 | Verse 2] that sends ambassadors by the sea, even in vessels of papyrus on the waters, saying, “Go, you swift messengers, to a nation tall and smooth, to a people awesome from their beginning onward, a nation that measures out and treads down, whose land the rivers divide!”
[WEB | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 18 | Verse 3] All you inhabitants of the world, and you dwellers on the earth, when a banner is lifted up on the mountains, look! When the trumpet is blown, listen!
[WEB | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 18 | Verse 4] For the LORD said to me, “I will be still, and I will see in my dwelling place, like clear heat in sunshine, like a cloud of dew in the heat of harvest.”
[WEB | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 18 | Verse 5] For before the harvest, when the blossom is over, and the flower becomes a ripening grape, he will cut off the sprigs with pruning hooks, and he will cut down and take away the spreading branches.
[WEB | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 18 | Verse 6] They will be left together for the ravenous birds of the mountains, and for the animals of the earth. The ravenous birds will eat them in the summer, and all the animals of the earth will eat them in the winter.
[WEB | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 18 | Verse 7] In that time, a present will be brought to the LORD of Armies from a people tall and smooth, even from a people awesome from their beginning onward, a nation that measures out and treads down, whose land the rivers divide, to the place of the name of the LORD of Armies, Mount Zion.
