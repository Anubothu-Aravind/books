[KJV | EN | NT | 1 Peter | Chapter 5 | Verse 1] The elders which are among you I exhort, who am also an elder, and a witness of the sufferings of Christ, and also a partaker of the glory that shall be revealed:
[KJV | EN | NT | 1 Peter | Chapter 5 | Verse 2] feed the flock of God which is among you, taking the oversight thereof, not by constraint, but willingly; not for filthy lucre, but of a ready mind;
[KJV | EN | NT | 1 Peter | Chapter 5 | Verse 3] neither as being lords over God’s heritage, but being ensamples to the flock.
[KJV | EN | NT | 1 Peter | Chapter 5 | Verse 4] And when the chief Shepherd shall appear, ye shall receive a crown of glory that fadeth not away.
[KJV | EN | NT | 1 Peter | Chapter 5 | Verse 5] Likewise, ye younger, submit yourselves unto the elder. Yea, all of you be subject one to another, and be clothed with humility: for God resisteth the proud, and giveth grace to the humble.
[KJV | EN | NT | 1 Peter | Chapter 5 | Verse 6] Humble yourselves therefore under the mighty hand of God, that he may exalt you in due time:
[KJV | EN | NT | 1 Peter | Chapter 5 | Verse 7] casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you.
[KJV | EN | NT | 1 Peter | Chapter 5 | Verse 8] Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour:
[KJV | EN | NT | 1 Peter | Chapter 5 | Verse 9] whom resist stedfast in the faith, knowing that the same afflictions are accomplished in your brethren that are in the world.
[KJV | EN | NT | 1 Peter | Chapter 5 | Verse 10] But the God of all grace, who hath called us into his eternal glory by Christ Jesus, after that ye have suffered a while, make you perfect, stablish, strengthen, settle you.
[KJV | EN | NT | 1 Peter | Chapter 5 | Verse 11] To him be glory and dominion for ever and ever. Amen.
[KJV | EN | NT | 1 Peter | Chapter 5 | Verse 12] By Silvanus, a faithful brother unto you, as I suppose, I have written briefly, exhorting, and testifying that this is the true grace of God wherein ye stand.
[KJV | EN | NT | 1 Peter | Chapter 5 | Verse 13] The church that is at Babylon, elected together with you, saluteth you; and so doth Marcus my son.
[KJV | EN | NT | 1 Peter | Chapter 5 | Verse 14] Greet ye one another with a kiss of charity. Peace be with you all that are in Christ Jesus. Amen.
