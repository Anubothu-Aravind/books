[KJV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 24 | Verse 2] And when he was called forth, Tertullus began to accuse him, saying, Seeing that by thee we enjoy great quietness, and that very worthy deeds are done unto this nation by thy providence,
[KJV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 24 | Verse 3] we accept it always, and in all places, most noble Felix, with all thankfulness.
[KJV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 24 | Verse 4] Notwithstanding, that I be not further tedious unto thee, I pray thee that thou wouldest hear us of thy clemency a few words.

[KJV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 24 | Verse 10] Then Paul, after that the governor had beckoned unto him to speak, answered, Forasmuch as I know that thou hast been of many years a judge unto this nation, I do the more cheerfully answer for myself:

[KJV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 24 | Verse 12] And they neither found me in the temple disputing with any man, neither raising up the people, neither in the synagogues, nor in the city:
[KJV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 24 | Verse 13] neither can they prove the things whereof they now accuse me.
[KJV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 24 | Verse 14] But this I confess unto thee, that after the way which they call heresy, so worship I the God of my fathers, believing all things which are written in the law and the prophets:

[KJV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 24 | Verse 16] And herein do I exercise myself, to have always a conscience void of offence toward God, and toward men.

[KJV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 24 | Verse 18] Whereupon certain Jews from Asia found me purified in the temple, neither with multitude, nor with tumult.
[KJV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 24 | Verse 19] Who ought to have been here before thee, and object, if they had ought against me.
[KJV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 24 | Verse 20] Or else let these same here say, if they have found any evil doing in me, while I stood before the council,

[KJV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 24 | Verse 22] And when Felix heard these things, having more perfect knowledge of that way, he deferred them, and said, When Lysias the chief captain shall come down, I will know the uttermost of your matter.
[KJV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 24 | Verse 23] And he commanded a centurion to keep Paul, and to let him have liberty, and that he should forbid none of his acquaintance to minister or come unto him.
[KJV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 24 | Verse 24] And after certain days, when Felix came with his wife Drusilla, which was a Jewess, he sent for Paul, and heard him concerning the faith in Christ.
[KJV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 24 | Verse 25] And as he reasoned of righteousness, temperance, and judgment to come, Felix trembled, and answered, Go thy way for this time; when I have a convenient season, I will call for thee.
[KJV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 24 | Verse 26] He hoped also that money should have been given him of Paul, that he might loose him: wherefore he sent for him the oftener, and communed with him.
[KJV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 24 | Verse 27] But after two years Porcius Festus came into Felix’ room: and Felix, willing to shew the Jews a pleasure, left Paul bound.
