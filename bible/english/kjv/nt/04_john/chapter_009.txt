[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 1] And as Jesus passed by, he saw a man which was blind from his birth.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 2] And his disciples asked him, saying, Master, who did sin, this man, or his parents, that he was born blind?
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 3] Jesus answered, Neither hath this man sinned, nor his parents: but that the works of God should be made manifest in him.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 4] I must work the works of him that sent me, while it is day: the night cometh, when no man can work.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 5] As long as I am in the world, I am the light of the world.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 6] When he had thus spoken, he spat on the ground, and made clay of the spittle, and he anointed the eyes of the blind man with the clay,
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 7] and said unto him, Go, wash in the pool of Siloam, (which is by interpretation, Sent.) He went his way therefore, and washed, and came seeing.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 8] The neighbours therefore, and they which before had seen him that he was blind, said, Is not this he that sat and begged?
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 9] Some said, This is he: others said, He is like him: but he said, I am he.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 10] Therefore said they unto him, How were thine eyes opened?
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 11] He answered and said, A man that is called Jesus made clay, and anointed mine eyes, and said unto me, Go to the pool of Siloam, and wash: and I went and washed, and I received sight.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 12] Then said they unto him, Where is he? He said, I know not.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 13] They brought to the Pharisees him that aforetime was blind.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 14] And it was the sabbath day when Jesus made the clay, and opened his eyes.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 15] Then again the Pharisees also asked him how he had received his sight. He said unto them, He put clay upon mine eyes, and I washed, and do see.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 16] Therefore said some of the Pharisees, This man is not of God, because he keepeth not the sabbath day. Others said, How can a man that is a sinner do such miracles? And there was a division among them.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 17] They say unto the blind man again, What sayest thou of him, that he hath opened thine eyes? He said, He is a prophet.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 18] But the Jews did not believe concerning him, that he had been blind, and received his sight, until they called the parents of him that had received his sight.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 19] And they asked them, saying, Is this your son, who ye say was born blind? how then doth he now see?
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 20] His parents answered them and said, We know that this is our son, and that he was born blind:
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 21] but by what means he now seeth, we know not; or who hath opened his eyes, we know not: he is of age; ask him: he shall speak for himself.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 22] These words spake his parents, because they feared the Jews: for the Jews had agreed already, that if any man did confess that he was Christ, he should be put out of the synagogue.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 23] Therefore said his parents, He is of age; ask him.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 24] Then again called they the man that was blind, and said unto him, Give God the praise: we know that this man is a sinner.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 25] He answered and said, Whether he be a sinner or no, I know not: one thing I know, that, whereas I was blind, now I see.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 26] Then said they to him again, What did he to thee? how opened he thine eyes?
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 27] He answered them, I have told you already, and ye did not hear: wherefore would you hear it again? will ye also be his disciples?
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 28] Then they reviled him, and said, Thou art his disciple; but we are Moses’ disciples.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 29] We know that God spake unto Moses: as for this fellow, we know not from whence he is.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 30] The man answered and said unto them, Why herein is a marvellous thing, that ye know not from whence he is, and yet he hath opened mine eyes.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 31] Now we know that God heareth not sinners: but if any man be a worshipper of God, and doeth his will, him he heareth.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 32] Since the world began was it not heard that any man opened the eyes of one that was born blind.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 33] If this man were not of God, he could do nothing.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 34] They answered and said unto him, Thou wast altogether born in sins, and dost thou teach us? And they cast him out.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 35] Jesus heard that they had cast him out; and when he had found him, he said unto him, Dost thou believe on the Son of God?
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 36] He answered and said, Who is he, Lord, that I might believe on him?
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 37] And Jesus said unto him, Thou hast both seen him, and it is he that talketh with thee.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 38] And he said, Lord, I believe. And he worshipped him.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 39] And Jesus said, For judgment I am come into this world, that they which see not might see; and that they which see might be made blind.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 40] And some of the Pharisees which were with him heard these words, and said unto him, Are we blind also?
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 9 | Verse 41] Jesus said unto them, If ye were blind, ye should have no sin: but now ye say, We see; therefore your sin remaineth.
