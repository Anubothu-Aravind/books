[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 13 | Verse 14] If I then, your Lord and Master, have washed your feet; ye also ought to wash one another’s feet.

[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 13 | Verse 16] Verily, verily, I say unto you, The servant is not greater than his lord; neither he that is sent greater than he that sent him.

[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 13 | Verse 19] Now I tell you before it come, that, when it is come to pass, ye may believe that I am he.
[KJV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 13 | Verse 20] Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that receiveth whomsoever I send receiveth me; and he that receiveth me receiveth him that sent me.
