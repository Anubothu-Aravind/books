[KJV | EN | NT | James | Chapter 3 | Verse 2] For in many things we offend all. If any man offend not in word, the same is a perfect man, and able also to bridle the whole body.

[KJV | EN | NT | James | Chapter 3 | Verse 17] But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be intreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy.
