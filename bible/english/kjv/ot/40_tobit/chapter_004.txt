[KJV | EN | OT | Tobit | Chapter 4 | Verse 1] In that day Tobit remembered the money which he had committed to Gabael in Rages of Media,
[KJV | EN | OT | Tobit | Chapter 4 | Verse 2] and said with himself, I have wished for death; wherefore do I not call for my son Tobias, that I may signify to him of the money before I die?
[KJV | EN | OT | Tobit | Chapter 4 | Verse 3] And when he had called him, he said, My son, when I am dead, bury me; and despise not thy mother, but honour her all the days of thy life, and do that which shall please her, and grieve her not.
[KJV | EN | OT | Tobit | Chapter 4 | Verse 4] Remember, my son, that she saw many dangers for thee, when thou wast in her womb; and when she is dead, bury her by me in one grave.
[KJV | EN | OT | Tobit | Chapter 4 | Verse 5] My son, be mindful of the Lord our God all thy days, and let not thy will be set to sin, or to transgress his commandments: do uprightly all thy life long, and follow not the ways of unrighteousness.
[KJV | EN | OT | Tobit | Chapter 4 | Verse 6] For if thou deal truly, thy doings shall prosperously succeed to thee, and to all them that live justly.
[KJV | EN | OT | Tobit | Chapter 4 | Verse 7] Give alms of thy substance; and when thou givest alms, let not thine eye be envious, neither turn thy face from any poor, and the face of God shall not be turned away from thee.
[KJV | EN | OT | Tobit | Chapter 4 | Verse 8] If thou hast abundance, give alms accordingly: if thou have but a little, be not afraid to give according to that little:
[KJV | EN | OT | Tobit | Chapter 4 | Verse 9] for thou layest up a good treasure for thyself against the day of necessity.
[KJV | EN | OT | Tobit | Chapter 4 | Verse 10] Because that alms doth deliver from death, and suffereth not to come into darkness.
[KJV | EN | OT | Tobit | Chapter 4 | Verse 11] For alms is a good gift unto all that give it, in the sight of the most High.
[KJV | EN | OT | Tobit | Chapter 4 | Verse 12] Beware of all whoredom, my son, and chiefly take a wife of the seed of thy fathers, and take not a strange woman to wife, which is not of thy father’s tribe: for we are the children of the prophets, Noe, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob: remember, my son, that our fathers from the beginning, even that they all married wives of their own kindred, and were blessed in their children, and their seed shall inherit the land.
[KJV | EN | OT | Tobit | Chapter 4 | Verse 13] Now therefore, my son, love thy brethren, and despise not in thy heart thy brethren, the sons and daughters of thy people, in not taking a wife of them: for in pride is destruction and much trouble, and in lewdness is decay and great want: for lewdness is the mother of famine.
[KJV | EN | OT | Tobit | Chapter 4 | Verse 14] Let not the wages of any man, which hath wrought for thee, tarry with thee, but give him it out of hand: for if thou serve God, he will also repay thee: be circumspect, my son, in all things thou doest, and be wise in all thy conversation.
[KJV | EN | OT | Tobit | Chapter 4 | Verse 15] Do that to no man which thou hatest: drink not wine to make thee drunken: neither let drunkenness go with thee in thy journey.
[KJV | EN | OT | Tobit | Chapter 4 | Verse 16] Give of thy bread to the hungry, and of thy garments to them that are naked; and according to thine abundance give alms; and let not thine eye be envious, when thou givest alms.
[KJV | EN | OT | Tobit | Chapter 4 | Verse 17] Pour out thy bread on the burial of the just, but give nothing to the wicked.
[KJV | EN | OT | Tobit | Chapter 4 | Verse 18] Ask counsel of all that are wise, and despise not any counsel that is profitable.
[KJV | EN | OT | Tobit | Chapter 4 | Verse 19] Bless the Lord thy God alway, and desire of him that thy ways may be directed, and that all thy paths and counsels may prosper: for every nation hath not counsel; but the Lord himself giveth all good things, and he humbleth whom he will, as he will; now therefore, my son, remember my commandments, neither let them be put out of thy mind.
[KJV | EN | OT | Tobit | Chapter 4 | Verse 20] And now I signify this to thee, that I committed ten talents to Gabael the son of Gabrias at Rages in Media.
[KJV | EN | OT | Tobit | Chapter 4 | Verse 21] And fear not, my son, that we are made poor: for thou hast much wealth, if thou fear God, and depart from all sin, and do that which is pleasing in his sight.
