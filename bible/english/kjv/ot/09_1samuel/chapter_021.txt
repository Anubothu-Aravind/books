[KJV | EN | OT | 1 Samuel | Chapter 21 | Verse 1] Then came David to Nob to Ahimelech the priest: and Ahimelech was afraid at the meeting of David, and said unto him, Why art thou alone, and no man with thee?
[KJV | EN | OT | 1 Samuel | Chapter 21 | Verse 2] And David said unto Ahimelech the priest, The king hath commanded me a business, and hath said unto me, Let no man know any thing of the business whereabout I send thee, and what I have commanded thee: and I have appointed my servants to such and such a place.
[KJV | EN | OT | 1 Samuel | Chapter 21 | Verse 3] Now therefore what is under thine hand? give me five loaves of bread in mine hand, or what there is present.
[KJV | EN | OT | 1 Samuel | Chapter 21 | Verse 4] And the priest answered David, and said, There is no common bread under mine hand, but there is hallowed bread; if the young men have kept themselves at least from women.
[KJV | EN | OT | 1 Samuel | Chapter 21 | Verse 5] And David answered the priest, and said unto him, Of a truth women have been kept from us about these three days, since I came out, and the vessels of the young men are holy, and the bread is in a manner common, yea, though it were sanctified this day in the vessel.
[KJV | EN | OT | 1 Samuel | Chapter 21 | Verse 6] So the priest gave him hallowed bread: for there was no bread there but the shewbread, that was taken from before the Lord, to put hot bread in the day when it was taken away.
[KJV | EN | OT | 1 Samuel | Chapter 21 | Verse 7] Now a certain man of the servants of Saul was there that day, detained before the Lord; and his name was Doeg, an Edomite, the chiefest of the herdmen that belonged to Saul.
[KJV | EN | OT | 1 Samuel | Chapter 21 | Verse 8] And David said unto Ahimelech, And is there not here under thine hand spear or sword? for I have neither brought my sword nor my weapons with me, because the king’s business required haste.
[KJV | EN | OT | 1 Samuel | Chapter 21 | Verse 9] And the priest said, The sword of Goliath the Philistine, whom thou slewest in the valley of Elah, behold, it is here wrapt in a cloth behind the ephod: if thou wilt take that, take it: for there is no other save that here. And David said, There is none like that; give it me.
[KJV | EN | OT | 1 Samuel | Chapter 21 | Verse 10] And David arose, and fled that day for fear of Saul, and went to Achish the king of Gath.
[KJV | EN | OT | 1 Samuel | Chapter 21 | Verse 11] And the servants of Achish said unto him, Is not this David the king of the land? did they not sing one to another of him in dances, saying, Saul hath slain his thousands, And David his ten thousands?
[KJV | EN | OT | 1 Samuel | Chapter 21 | Verse 12] And David laid up these words in his heart, and was sore afraid of Achish the king of Gath.
[KJV | EN | OT | 1 Samuel | Chapter 21 | Verse 13] And he changed his behaviour before them, and feigned himself mad in their hands, and scrabled on the doors of the gate, and let his spittle fall down upon his beard.
[KJV | EN | OT | 1 Samuel | Chapter 21 | Verse 14] Then said Achish unto his servants, Lo, you see the man is mad: wherefore then have ye brought him to me?
[KJV | EN | OT | 1 Samuel | Chapter 21 | Verse 15] Have I need of mad men, that ye have brought this fellow to play the mad man in my presence? shall this fellow come into my house?
