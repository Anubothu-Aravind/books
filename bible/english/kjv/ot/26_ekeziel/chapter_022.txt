[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 4] Thou art become guilty in thy blood that thou hast shed; and hast defiled thyself in thine idols which thou hast made; and thou hast caused thy days to draw near, and art come even unto thy years: therefore have I made thee a reproach unto the heathen, and a mocking to all countries.
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 5] Those that be near, and those that be far from thee, shall mock thee, which art infamous and much vexed.
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 6] Behold, the princes of Israel, every one were in thee to their power to shed blood.
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 7] In thee have they set light by father and mother: in the midst of thee have they dealt by oppression with the stranger: in thee have they vexed the fatherless and the widow.
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 8] Thou hast despised mine holy things, and hast profaned my sabbaths.
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 9] In thee are men that carry tales to shed blood: and in thee they eat upon the mountains: in the midst of thee they commit lewdness.
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 10] In thee have they discovered their father’s nakedness: in thee have they humbled her that was set apart for pollution.
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 11] And one hath committed abomination with his neighbour’s wife; and another hath lewdly defiled his daughter in law; and another in thee hath humbled his sister, his father’s daughter.
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 12] In thee have they taken gifts to shed blood; thou hast taken usury and increase, and thou hast greedily gained of thy neighbours by extortion, and hast forgotten me, saith the Lord God.
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 13] Behold therefore, I have smitten mine hand at thy dishonest gain which thou hast made, and at thy blood which hath been in the midst of thee.
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 14] Can thine heart endure, or can thine hands be strong, in the days that I shall deal with thee? I the Lord have spoken it, and will do it.
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 15] And I will scatter thee among the heathen, and disperse thee in the countries, and will consume thy filthiness out of thee.
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 16] And thou shalt take thine inheritance in thyself in the sight of the heathen, and thou shalt know that I am the Lord.
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 17] And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 18] Son of man, the house of Israel is to me become dross: all they are brass, and tin, and iron, and lead, in the midst of the furnace; they are even the dross of silver.
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 19] Therefore thus saith the Lord God; Because ye are all become dross, behold therefore, I will gather you into the midst of Jerusalem.
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 20] As they gather silver, and brass, and iron, and lead, and tin, into the midst of the furnace, to blow the fire upon it, to melt it; so will I gather you in mine anger and in my fury, and I will leave you there, and melt you.
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 21] Yea, I will gather you, and blow upon you in the fire of my wrath, and ye shall be melted in the midst thereof.
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 22] As silver is melted in the midst of the furnace, so shall ye be melted in the midst thereof; and ye shall know that I the Lord have poured out my fury upon you.
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 23] And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 24] Son of man, say unto her, Thou art the land that is not cleansed, nor rained upon in the day of indignation.
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 25] There is a conspiracy of her prophets in the midst thereof, like a roaring lion ravening the prey; they have devoured souls; they have taken the treasure and precious things; they have made her many widows in the midst thereof.

[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 27] Her princes in the midst thereof are like wolves ravening the prey, to shed blood, and to destroy souls, to get dishonest gain.
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 28] And her prophets have daubed them with untempered morter, seeing vanity, and divining lies unto them, saying, Thus saith the Lord God, when the Lord hath not spoken.

[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 30] And I sought for a man among them, that should make up the hedge, and stand in the gap before me for the land, that I should not destroy it: but I found none.
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 22 | Verse 31] Therefore have I poured out mine indignation upon them; I have consumed them with the fire of my wrath: their own way have I recompensed upon their heads, saith the Lord God.
