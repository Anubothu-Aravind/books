[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 14] And thy renown went forth among the heathen for thy beauty: for it was perfect through my comeliness, which I had put upon thee, saith the Lord God.

[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 17] Thou hast also taken thy fair jewels of my gold and of my silver, which I had given thee, and madest to thyself images of men, and didst commit whoredom with them,

[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 19] My meat also which I gave thee, fine flour, and oil, and honey, wherewith I fed thee, thou hast even set it before them for a sweet savour: and thus it was, saith the Lord God.
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 20] Moreover thou hast taken thy sons and thy daughters, whom thou hast borne unto me, and these hast thou sacrificed unto them to be devoured. Is this of thy whoredoms a small matter,

[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 30] How weak is thine heart, saith the Lord God, seeing thou doest all these things, the work of an imperious whorish woman;
