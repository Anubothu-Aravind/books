[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 15 | Verse 2] Son of man, What is the vine tree more than any tree, or than a branch which is among the trees of the forest?
[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 15 | Verse 3] Shall wood be taken thereof to do any work? or will men take a pin of it to hang any vessel thereon?

[KJV | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 15 | Verse 7] And I will set my face against them; they shall go out from one fire, and another fire shall devour them; and ye shall know that I am the Lord, when I set my face against them.
