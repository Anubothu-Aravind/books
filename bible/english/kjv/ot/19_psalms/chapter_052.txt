[KJV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 8] But I am like a green olive tree in the house of God: I trust in the mercy of God for ever and ever.
[KJV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 9] I will praise thee for ever, because thou hast done it: And I will wait on thy name; for it is good before thy saints.
