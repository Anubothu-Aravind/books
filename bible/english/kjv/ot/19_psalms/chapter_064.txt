[KJV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 1] Hear my voice, O God, in my prayer: Preserve my life from fear of the enemy.
[KJV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 2] Hide me from the secret counsel of the wicked; From the insurrection of the workers of iniquity:
[KJV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 3] Who whet their tongue like a sword, And bend their bows to shoot their arrows, even bitter words:
[KJV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 4] That they may shoot in secret at the perfect: Suddenly do they shoot at him, and fear not.
[KJV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 5] They encourage themselves in an evil matter: They commune of laying snares privily; They say, Who shall see them?
[KJV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 6] They search out iniquities; They accomplish a diligent search: Both the inward thought of every one of them, and the heart, is deep.
[KJV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 7] But God shall shoot at them with an arrow; Suddenly shall they be wounded.
[KJV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 8] So they shall make their own tongue to fall upon themselves: All that see them shall flee away.
[KJV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 9] And all men shall fear, And shall declare the work of God; For they shall wisely consider of his doing.
[KJV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 64 | Verse 10] The righteous shall be glad in the Lord, and shall trust in him; And all the upright in heart shall glory.
