[KJV | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 13 | Verse 17] and the priest shall see him: and behold, if the plague be turned into white; then the priest shall pronounce him clean that hath the plague: he is clean.
[KJV | EN | OT | Leviticus | Chapter 13 | Verse 18] The flesh also, in which, even in the skin thereof, was a boil, and is healed,
