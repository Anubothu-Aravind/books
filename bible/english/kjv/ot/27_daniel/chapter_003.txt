[KJV | EN | OT | Daniel | Chapter 3 | Verse 17] If it be so, our God whom we serve is able to deliver us from the burning fiery furnace, and he will deliver us out of thine hand, O king.
[KJV | EN | OT | Daniel | Chapter 3 | Verse 18] But if not, be it known unto thee, O king, that we will not serve thy gods, nor worship the golden image which thou hast set up.
