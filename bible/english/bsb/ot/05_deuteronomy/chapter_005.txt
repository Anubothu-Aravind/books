[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 5 | Verse 9] You shall not bow down to them or worship them; for I, the LORD your God, am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers on their children to the third and fourth generations of those who hate Me,

[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 5 | Verse 11] You shall not take the name of the LORD your God in vain, for the LORD will not leave anyone unpunished who takes His name in vain.

[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 5 | Verse 22] The LORD spoke these commandments in a loud voice to your whole assembly out of the fire, the cloud, and the deep darkness on the mountain; He added nothing more. And He wrote them on two tablets of stone and gave them to me.
