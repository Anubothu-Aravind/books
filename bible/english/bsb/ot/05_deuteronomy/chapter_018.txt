[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 18 | Verse 1] The Levitical priests—indeed the whole tribe of Levi—shall have no portion or inheritance with Israel. They are to eat the offerings made by fire to the LORD; that is their inheritance.
[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 18 | Verse 2] Although they have no inheritance among their brothers, the LORD is their inheritance, as He promised them.
[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 18 | Verse 3] This shall be the priests’ share from the people who offer a sacrifice, whether a bull or a sheep: the priests are to be given the shoulder, the jowls, and the stomach.
[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 18 | Verse 4] You are to give them the firstfruits of your grain, new wine, and oil, and the first wool sheared from your flock.
[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 18 | Verse 5] For the LORD your God has chosen Levi and his sons out of all your tribes to stand and minister in His name for all time.
[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 18 | Verse 6] Now if a Levite moves from any town of residence throughout Israel and comes in all earnestness to the place the LORD will choose,
[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 18 | Verse 7] then he shall serve in the name of the LORD his God like all his fellow Levites who stand there before the LORD.
[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 18 | Verse 8] They shall eat equal portions, even though he has received money from the sale of his father’s estate.
[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 18 | Verse 9] When you enter the land that the LORD your God is giving you, do not imitate the detestable ways of the nations there.
[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 18 | Verse 10] Let no one be found among you who sacrifices his son or daughter in the fire, practices divination or conjury, interprets omens, practices sorcery,
[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 18 | Verse 11] casts spells, consults a medium or spiritist, or inquires of the dead.
[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 18 | Verse 12] For whoever does these things is detestable to the LORD. And because of these detestable things, the LORD your God is driving out the nations before you.
[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 18 | Verse 13] You must be blameless before the LORD your God.
[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 18 | Verse 14] Though these nations, which you will dispossess, listen to conjurers and diviners, the LORD your God has not permitted you to do so.
[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 18 | Verse 15] The LORD your God will raise up for you a prophet like me from among your brothers. You must listen to him.
[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 18 | Verse 16] This is what you asked of the LORD your God at Horeb on the day of the assembly, when you said, “Let us not hear the voice of the LORD our God or see this great fire anymore, so that we will not die!”
[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 18 | Verse 17] Then the LORD said to me, “They have spoken well.
[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 18 | Verse 18] I will raise up for them a prophet like you from among their brothers. I will put My words in his mouth, and he will tell them everything I command him.
[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 18 | Verse 19] And I will hold accountable anyone who does not listen to My words that the prophet speaks in My name.
[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 18 | Verse 20] But if any prophet dares to speak a message in My name that I have not commanded him to speak, or to speak in the name of other gods, that prophet must be put to death.”
[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 18 | Verse 21] You may ask in your heart, “How can we recognize a message that the LORD has not spoken?”
[BSB | EN | OT | Deuteronomy | Chapter 18 | Verse 22] When a prophet speaks in the name of the LORD and the message does not come to pass or come true, that is a message the LORD has not spoken. The prophet has spoken presumptuously. Do not be afraid of him.
