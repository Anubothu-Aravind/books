[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 1] Again the word of the LORD came to me, saying,
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 2] “Son of man, confront Jerusalem with her abominations
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 3] and tell her that this is what the Lord GOD says to Jerusalem: Your origin and your birth were in the land of the Canaanites. Your father was an Amorite and your mother a Hittite.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 4] On the day of your birth your cord was not cut, nor were you washed with water for cleansing. You were not rubbed with salt or wrapped in cloths.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 5] No one cared enough for you to do even one of these things out of compassion for you. Instead, you were thrown out into the open field, because you were despised on the day of your birth.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 6] Then I passed by and saw you wallowing in your blood, and as you lay there in your blood I said to you, ‘Live!’ There I said to you, ‘Live!’
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 7] I made you thrive like a plant of the field. You grew up and matured and became very beautiful. Your breasts were formed and your hair grew, but you were naked and bare.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 8] Then I passed by and saw you, and you were indeed old enough for love. So I spread My cloak over you and covered your nakedness. I pledged Myself to you, entered into a covenant with you, and you became Mine, declares the Lord GOD.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 9] Then I bathed you with water, rinsed off your blood, and anointed you with oil.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 10] I clothed you in embroidered cloth and gave you sandals of fine leather. I wrapped you in fine linen and covered you with silk.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 11] I adorned you with jewelry, and I put bracelets on your wrists and a chain around your neck.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 12] I put a ring in your nose, earrings on your ears, and a beautiful crown upon your head.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 13] So you were adorned with gold and silver, and your clothing was made of fine linen, silk, and embroidered cloth. You ate fine flour, honey, and oil. You became very beautiful and rose to be queen.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 14] Your fame spread among the nations on account of your beauty, for it was perfect in the splendor I bestowed on you, declares the Lord GOD.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 15] But because of your fame, you trusted in your beauty and played the harlot. You lavished your favors on everyone who passed by, and your beauty was theirs for the asking.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 16] You took some of your garments and made colorful high places for yourself, and on them you prostituted yourself. Such things should not have happened; never should they have occurred!
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 17] You also took the fine jewelry of gold and silver I had given you, and you made male idols with which to prostitute yourself.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 18] You took your embroidered garments to cover them, and you set My oil and incense before them.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 19] And you set before them as a pleasing aroma the food I had given you—the fine flour, oil, and honey that I had fed you. That is what happened, declares the Lord GOD.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 20] You even took the sons and daughters you bore to Me and sacrificed them as food to idols. Was your prostitution not enough?
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 21] You slaughtered My children and delivered them up through the fire to idols.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 22] And in all your abominations and acts of prostitution, you did not remember the days of your youth when you were naked and bare, wallowing in your own blood.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 23] Woe! Woe to you, declares the Lord GOD. And in addition to all your other wickedness,
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 24] you built yourself a mound and made yourself a lofty shrine in every public square.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 25] At the head of every street you built your lofty shrines and degraded your beauty. With increasing promiscuity, you spread your legs to all who passed by.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 26] You prostituted yourself with your lustful neighbors, the Egyptians, and increased your promiscuity to provoke Me to anger.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 27] Therefore I stretched out My hand against you and reduced your portion. I gave you over to the desire of those who hate you, the daughters of the Philistines, who were ashamed of your lewd conduct.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 28] Then you prostituted yourself with the Assyrians, because you were not yet satisfied. Even after that, you were still not satisfied.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 29] So you extended your promiscuity to Chaldea, the land of merchants—but even with this you were not satisfied!
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 30] How weak-willed is your heart, declares the Lord GOD, while you do all these things, the acts of a shameless prostitute!
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 31] But when you built your mounds at the head of every street and made your lofty shrines in every public square, you were not even like a prostitute, because you scorned payment.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 32] You adulterous wife! You receive strangers instead of your own husband!
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 33] Men give gifts to all their prostitutes, but you gave gifts to all your lovers. You bribed them to come to you from everywhere for your illicit favors.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 34] So your prostitution is the opposite of that of other women: No one solicited your favors, and you paid a fee instead of receiving one; so you are the very opposite!
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 35] Therefore, O prostitute, hear the word of the LORD!
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 36] This is what the Lord GOD says: Because you poured out your wealth and exposed your nakedness in your promiscuity with your lovers and with all your detestable idols, and because of the blood of your children which you gave to them,
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 37] therefore I will surely gather all the lovers with whom you found pleasure, all those you loved and all those you hated. I will gather them against you from all around and expose you before them, and they will see you completely naked.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 38] And I will sentence you to the punishment of women who commit adultery and those who shed blood; so I will bring upon you the wrath of your bloodshed and jealousy.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 39] Then I will deliver you into the hands of your lovers, and they will level your mounds and tear down your lofty shrines. They will strip off your clothes, take your fine jewelry, and leave you naked and bare.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 40] They will bring a mob against you, who will stone you and cut you to pieces with their swords.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 41] Then they will burn down your houses and execute judgment against you in the sight of many women. I will put an end to your prostitution, and you will never again pay your lovers.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 42] So I will lay to rest My wrath against you, and My jealousy will turn away from you. Then I will be calm and no longer angry.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 43] Because you did not remember the days of your youth, but enraged Me with all these things, I will surely bring your deeds down upon your own head, declares the Lord GOD. Have you not committed this lewdness on top of all your other abominations?
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 44] Behold, all who speak in proverbs will quote this proverb about you: ‘Like mother, like daughter.’
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 45] You are the daughter of your mother, who despised her husband and children. You are the sister of your sisters, who despised their husbands and children. Your mother was a Hittite and your father an Amorite.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 46] Your older sister was Samaria, who lived with her daughters to your north; and your younger sister was Sodom, who lived with her daughters to your south.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 47] And you not only walked in their ways and practiced their abominations, but soon you were more depraved than they were.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 48] As surely as I live, declares the Lord GOD, your sister Sodom and her daughters never did as you and your daughters have done.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 49] Now this was the iniquity of your sister Sodom: She and her daughters were arrogant, overfed, and complacent; they did not help the poor and needy.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 50] Thus they were haughty and committed abominations before Me. Therefore I removed them, as you have seen.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 51] Furthermore, Samaria did not commit half the sins you did. You have multiplied your abominations beyond theirs, and all the abominations you have committed have made your sisters appear righteous.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 52] So now you must bear your disgrace, since you have brought justification for your sisters. For they appear more righteous than you, because your sins were more vile than theirs. So you too must bear your shame and disgrace, since you have made your sisters appear righteous.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 53] But I will restore Sodom and her daughters from captivity, as well as Samaria and her daughters. And I will restore you along with them.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 54] So you will bear your disgrace and be ashamed of all you did to comfort them.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 55] And your sisters, Sodom with her daughters and Samaria with her daughters, will return to their former state. You and your daughters will also return to your former state.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 56] Did you not treat your sister Sodom as an object of scorn in the day of your pride,
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 57] before your wickedness was uncovered? Even so, you are now scorned by the daughters of Edom and all those around her, and by the daughters of the Philistines—all those around you who despise you.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 58] You will bear the consequences of your lewdness and your abominations, declares the LORD.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 59] For this is what the Lord GOD says: I will deal with you according to your deeds, since you have despised the oath by breaking the covenant.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 60] But I will remember the covenant I made with you in the days of your youth, and I will establish an everlasting covenant with you.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 61] Then you will remember your ways and be ashamed when you receive your older and younger sisters. I will give them to you as daughters, but not because of My covenant with you.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 62] So I will establish My covenant with you, and you will know that I am the LORD,
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 16 | Verse 63] so that when I make atonement for all you have done, you will remember and be ashamed and never again open your mouth because of your disgrace, declares the Lord GOD.”
