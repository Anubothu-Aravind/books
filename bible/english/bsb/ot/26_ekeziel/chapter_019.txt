[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 19 | Verse 1] “As for you, take up a lament for the princes of Israel
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 19 | Verse 2] and say: ‘What was your mother? A lioness among the lions! She lay down among the young lions; she reared her cubs.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 19 | Verse 3] She brought up one of her cubs, and he became a young lion. After learning to tear his prey, he devoured men.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 19 | Verse 4] When the nations heard of him, he was trapped in their pit. With hooks they led him away to the land of Egypt.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 19 | Verse 5] When she saw that she had waited in vain, that her hope was lost, she took another of her cubs and made him a young lion.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 19 | Verse 6] He prowled among the lions, and became a young lion. After learning to tear his prey, he devoured men.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 19 | Verse 7] He broke down their strongholds and devastated their cities. The land and everything in it shuddered at the sound of his roaring.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 19 | Verse 8] Then the nations set out against him from the provinces on every side. They spread their net over him; he was trapped in their pit.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 19 | Verse 9] With hooks they caged him and brought him to the king of Babylon. They brought him into captivity so that his roar was heard no longer on the mountains of Israel.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 19 | Verse 10] Your mother was like a vine in your vineyard, planted by the water; it was fruitful and full of branches because of the abundant waters.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 19 | Verse 11] It had strong branches, fit for a ruler’s scepter. It towered high above the thick branches, conspicuous for its height and for its dense foliage.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 19 | Verse 12] But it was uprooted in fury, cast down to the ground, and the east wind dried up its fruit. Its strong branches were stripped off and they withered; the fire consumed them.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 19 | Verse 13] Now it is planted in the wilderness, in a dry and thirsty land.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 19 | Verse 14] Fire has gone out from its main branch and devoured its fruit; on it no strong branch remains fit for a ruler’s scepter.’ This is a lament and shall be used as a lament.”
