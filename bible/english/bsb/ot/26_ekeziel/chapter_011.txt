[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 11 | Verse 2] And the LORD said to me, “Son of man, these are the men who plot evil and give wicked counsel in this city.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 11 | Verse 3] They are saying, ‘Is not the time near to build houses? The city is the cooking pot, and we are the meat.’
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 11 | Verse 4] Therefore prophesy against them; prophesy, O son of man!”
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 11 | Verse 5] And the Spirit of the LORD fell upon me and told me to declare that this is what the LORD says: “That is what you are thinking, O house of Israel; and I know the thoughts that arise in your minds.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 11 | Verse 6] You have multiplied those you killed in this city and filled its streets with the dead.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 11 | Verse 7] Therefore this is what the Lord GOD says: The slain you have laid within this city are the meat, and the city is the pot; but I will remove you from it.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 11 | Verse 8] You fear the sword, so I will bring the sword against you, declares the Lord GOD.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 11 | Verse 9] I will bring you out of the city and deliver you into the hands of foreigners, and I will execute judgments against you.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 11 | Verse 10] You will fall by the sword, and I will judge you even to the borders of Israel. Then you will know that I am the LORD.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 11 | Verse 11] The city will not be a pot for you, nor will you be the meat within it. I will judge you even to the borders of Israel.
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 11 | Verse 12] Then you will know that I am the LORD. For you have neither followed My statutes nor practiced My ordinances, but you have conformed to the ordinances of the nations around you.”
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 11 | Verse 13] Now as I was prophesying, Pelatiah son of Benaiah died. Then I fell facedown and cried out in a loud voice, “Oh, Lord GOD, will You bring the remnant of Israel to a complete end?”
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 11 | Verse 14] Then the word of the LORD came to me, saying,

[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 11 | Verse 17] Therefore declare that this is what the Lord GOD says: ‘I will gather you from the peoples and assemble you from the countries to which you have been scattered, and I will give back to you the land of Israel.’

[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 11 | Verse 19] And I will give them singleness of heart and put a new spirit within them; I will remove their heart of stone and give them a heart of flesh,
[BSB | EN | OT | Ezekiel | Chapter 11 | Verse 20] so that they may follow My statutes, keep My ordinances, and practice them. Then they will be My people, and I will be their God.
