[BSB | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 1] Hear now what the LORD says: “Arise, plead your case before the mountains, and let the hills hear your voice.
[BSB | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 2] Hear, O mountains, the LORD’s indictment, you enduring foundations of the earth. For the LORD has a case against His people, and He will argue it against Israel:
[BSB | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 3] ‘My people, what have I done to you? Testify against Me how I have wearied you!
[BSB | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 4] For I brought you up from the land of Egypt and redeemed you from the house of slavery. I sent Moses before you, as well as Aaron and Miriam.
[BSB | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 5] My people, remember what Balak king of Moab counseled and what Balaam son of Beor answered. Remember your journey from Shittim to Gilgal, so that you may acknowledge the righteousness of the LORD.’”
[BSB | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 6] With what shall I come before the LORD when I bow before the God on high? Should I come to Him with burnt offerings, with year-old calves?
[BSB | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 7] Would the LORD be pleased with thousands of rams, with ten thousand rivers of oil? Shall I present my firstborn for my transgression, the fruit of my body for the sin of my soul?
[BSB | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 8] He has shown you, O man, what is good. And what does the LORD require of you but to act justly, to love mercy, and to walk humbly with your God?
[BSB | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 9] The voice of the LORD calls out to the city (and it is sound wisdom to fear Your name): “Heed the rod and the One who ordained it.
[BSB | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 10] Can I forget any longer, O house of the wicked, the treasures of wickedness and the short ephah, which is accursed?
[BSB | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 11] Can I excuse dishonest scales or bags of false weights?
[BSB | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 12] For the wealthy of the city are full of violence, and its residents speak lies; their tongues are deceitful in their mouths.
[BSB | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 13] Therefore I am striking you severely, to ruin you because of your sins.
[BSB | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 14] You will eat but not be satisfied, and your hunger will remain with you. What you acquire, you will not preserve; and what you save, I will give to the sword.
[BSB | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 15] You will sow but not reap; you will press olives but not anoint yourselves with oil; you will tread grapes but not drink the wine.
[BSB | EN | OT | Micah | Chapter 6 | Verse 16] You have kept the statutes of Omri and all the practices of Ahab’s house; you have followed their counsel. Therefore I will make you a desolation, and your inhabitants an object of contempt; you will bear the scorn of the nations.”
