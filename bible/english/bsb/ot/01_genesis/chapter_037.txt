[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 1] Now Jacob lived in the land where his father had resided, the land of Canaan.
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 2] This is the account of Jacob. When Joseph was seventeen years old, he was tending the flock with his brothers, the sons of his father’s wives Bilhah and Zilpah, and he brought their father a bad report about them.
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 3] Now Israel loved Joseph more than his other sons, because Joseph had been born to him in his old age; so he made him a robe of many colors.
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 4] When Joseph’s brothers saw that their father loved him more than any of them, they hated him and could not speak a kind word to him.
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 5] Then Joseph had a dream, and when he told it to his brothers, they hated him even more.
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 6] He said to them, “Listen to this dream I had:
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 7] We were binding sheaves of grain in the field, and suddenly my sheaf rose and stood upright, while your sheaves gathered around and bowed down to mine.”
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 8] “Do you intend to reign over us?” his brothers asked. “Will you actually rule us?” So they hated him even more because of his dream and his statements.
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 9] Then Joseph had another dream and told it to his brothers. “Look,” he said, “I had another dream, and this time the sun and moon and eleven stars were bowing down to me.”
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 10] He told his father and brothers, but his father rebuked him and said, “What is this dream that you have had? Will your mother and brothers and I actually come and bow down to the ground before you?”
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 11] And his brothers were jealous of him, but his father kept in mind what he had said.
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 12] Some time later, Joseph’s brothers had gone to pasture their father’s flocks near Shechem.
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 13] Israel said to him, “Are not your brothers pasturing the flocks at Shechem? Get ready; I am sending you to them.” “I am ready,” Joseph replied.
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 14] Then Israel told him, “Go now and see how your brothers and the flocks are faring, and bring word back to me.” So he sent him off from the Valley of Hebron. And when Joseph arrived in Shechem,
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 15] a man found him wandering in the field and asked, “What are you looking for?”
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 16] “I am looking for my brothers,” Joseph replied. “Can you please tell me where they are pasturing their flocks?”
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 17] “They have moved on from here,” the man answered. “I heard them say, ‘Let us go to Dothan.’” So Joseph set out after his brothers and found them at Dothan.
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 18] Now Joseph’s brothers saw him in the distance, and before he arrived, they plotted to kill him.
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 19] “Here comes that dreamer!” they said to one another.
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 20] “Come now, let us kill him and throw him into one of the pits. We can say that a vicious animal has devoured him. Then we shall see what becomes of his dreams!”
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 21] When Reuben heard this, he tried to rescue Joseph from their hands. “Let us not take his life,” he said.
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 22] “Do not shed his blood. Throw him into this pit in the wilderness, but do not lay a hand on him.” Reuben said this so that he could rescue Joseph from their hands and return him to his father.
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 23] So when Joseph came to his brothers, they stripped him of his robe—the robe of many colors he was wearing—
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 24] and they took him and threw him into the pit. Now the pit was empty, with no water in it.
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 25] And as they sat down to eat a meal, they looked up and saw a caravan of Ishmaelites coming from Gilead. Their camels were carrying spices, balm, and myrrh on their way down to Egypt.
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 26] Then Judah said to his brothers, “What profit will we gain if we kill our brother and cover up his blood?
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 27] Come, let us sell him to the Ishmaelites and not lay a hand on him; for he is our brother, our own flesh.” And they agreed.
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 28] So when the Midianite traders passed by, his brothers pulled Joseph out of the pit and sold him for twenty shekels of silver to the Ishmaelites, who took him to Egypt.
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 29] When Reuben returned to the pit and saw that Joseph was not there, he tore his clothes,
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 30] returned to his brothers, and said, “The boy is gone! What am I going to do?”
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 31] Then they took Joseph’s robe, slaughtered a young goat, and dipped the robe in its blood.
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 32] They sent the robe of many colors to their father and said, “We found this. Examine it to see whether it is your son’s robe or not.”
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 33] His father recognized it and said, “It is my son’s robe! A vicious animal has devoured him. Joseph has surely been torn to pieces!”
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 34] Then Jacob tore his clothes, put sackcloth around his waist, and mourned for his son many days.
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 35] All his sons and daughters tried to comfort him, but he refused to be comforted. “No,” he said. “I will go down to Sheol mourning for my son.” So his father wept for him.
[BSB | EN | OT | Genesis | Chapter 37 | Verse 36] Meanwhile, the Midianites sold Joseph in Egypt to Potiphar, an officer of Pharaoh and captain of the guard.
