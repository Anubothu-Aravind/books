[BSB | EN | OT | 1 Samuel | Chapter 28 | Verse 24] The woman had a fattened calf at her house, and she quickly slaughtered it. She also took flour, kneaded it, and baked unleavened bread.
[BSB | EN | OT | 1 Samuel | Chapter 28 | Verse 25] She served it to Saul and his servants, and they ate. And that night they got up and left.
