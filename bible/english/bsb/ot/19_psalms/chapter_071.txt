[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 71 | Verse 1] In You, O LORD, I have taken refuge; let me never be put to shame.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 71 | Verse 2] In Your justice, rescue and deliver me; incline Your ear and save me.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 71 | Verse 3] Be my rock of refuge, where I can always go. Give the command to save me, for You are my rock and my fortress.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 71 | Verse 4] Deliver me, O my God, from the hand of the wicked, from the grasp of the unjust and ruthless.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 71 | Verse 5] For You are my hope, O Lord GOD, my confidence from my youth.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 71 | Verse 6] I have leaned on You since birth; You pulled me from my mother’s womb. My praise is always for You.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 71 | Verse 7] I have become a portent to many, but You are my strong refuge.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 71 | Verse 8] My mouth is filled with Your praise and with Your splendor all day long.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 71 | Verse 9] Do not discard me in my old age; do not forsake me when my strength fails.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 71 | Verse 10] For my enemies speak against me, and those who lie in wait for my life conspire,
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 71 | Verse 11] saying, “God has forsaken him; pursue him and seize him, for there is no one to rescue him.”
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 71 | Verse 12] Be not far from me, O God. Hurry, O my God, to help me.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 71 | Verse 13] May the accusers of my soul be ashamed and consumed; may those who seek my harm be covered with scorn and disgrace.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 71 | Verse 14] But I will always hope and will praise You more and more.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 71 | Verse 15] My mouth will declare Your righteousness and Your salvation all day long, though I cannot know their full measure.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 71 | Verse 16] I will enter in the strength of the Lord GOD; I will proclaim Your righteousness—Yours alone.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 71 | Verse 17] O God, You have taught me from my youth, and to this day I proclaim Your marvelous deeds.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 71 | Verse 18] Even when I am old and gray, do not forsake me, O God, until I proclaim Your power to the next generation, Your might to all who are to come.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 71 | Verse 19] Your righteousness reaches to the heavens, O God, You who have done great things. Who, O God, is like You?
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 71 | Verse 20] Though You have shown me many troubles and misfortunes, You will revive me once again. Even from the depths of the earth You will bring me back up.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 71 | Verse 21] You will increase my honor and comfort me once again.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 71 | Verse 22] So I will praise You with the harp for Your faithfulness, O my God; I will sing praise to You with the lyre, O Holy One of Israel.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 71 | Verse 23] When I sing praise to You my lips will shout for joy, along with my soul, which You have redeemed.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 71 | Verse 24] My tongue will indeed proclaim Your righteousness all day long, for those who seek my harm are disgraced and confounded.
