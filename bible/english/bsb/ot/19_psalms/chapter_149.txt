[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 149 | Verse 1] Hallelujah! Sing to the LORD a new song— His praise in the assembly of the godly.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 149 | Verse 2] Let Israel rejoice in their Maker; let the children of Zion rejoice in their King.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 149 | Verse 3] Let them praise His name with dancing, and make music to Him with tambourine and harp.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 149 | Verse 4] For the LORD takes pleasure in His people; He adorns the afflicted with salvation.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 149 | Verse 5] Let the saints exult in glory; let them shout for joy upon their beds.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 149 | Verse 6] May the high praises of God be in their mouths, and a double-edged sword in their hands,
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 149 | Verse 7] to inflict vengeance on the nations and punishment on the peoples,
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 149 | Verse 8] to bind their kings with chains and their nobles with shackles of iron,
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 149 | Verse 9] to execute the judgment written against them. This honor is for all His saints. Hallelujah!
