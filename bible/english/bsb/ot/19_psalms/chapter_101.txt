[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 101 | Verse 1] I will sing of Your loving devotion and justice; to You, O LORD, I will sing praises.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 101 | Verse 2] I will ponder the way that is blameless— when will You come to me? I will walk in my house with integrity of heart.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 101 | Verse 3] I will set no worthless thing before my eyes. I hate the work of those who fall away; it shall not cling to me.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 101 | Verse 4] A perverse heart shall depart from me; I will know nothing of evil.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 101 | Verse 5] Whoever slanders his neighbor in secret, I will put to silence; the one with haughty eyes and a proud heart, I will not endure.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 101 | Verse 6] My eyes favor the faithful of the land, that they may dwell with me; he who walks in the way of integrity shall minister to me.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 101 | Verse 7] No one who practices deceit shall dwell in my house; no one who tells lies shall stand in my presence.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 101 | Verse 8] Every morning I will remove all the wicked of the land, that I may cut off every evildoer from the city of the LORD.
