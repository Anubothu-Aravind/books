[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 28 | Verse 7] The LORD is my strength and my shield; my heart trusts in Him, and I am helped. Therefore my heart rejoices, and I give thanks to Him with my song.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 28 | Verse 8] The LORD is the strength of His people, a stronghold of salvation for His anointed.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 28 | Verse 9] Save Your people and bless Your inheritance; shepherd them and carry them forever.
