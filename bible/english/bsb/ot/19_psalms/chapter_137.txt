[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 137 | Verse 1] By the rivers of Babylon we sat and wept when we remembered Zion.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 137 | Verse 2] There on the willows we hung our harps,
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 137 | Verse 3] for there our captors requested a song; our tormentors demanded songs of joy: “Sing us a song of Zion.”
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 137 | Verse 4] How can we sing a song of the LORD in a foreign land?
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 137 | Verse 5] If I forget you, O Jerusalem, may my right hand cease to function.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 137 | Verse 6] May my tongue cling to the roof of my mouth if I do not remember you, if I do not exalt Jerusalem as my greatest joy!
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 137 | Verse 7] Remember, O LORD, the sons of Edom on the day Jerusalem fell: “Destroy it,” they said, “tear it down to its foundations!”
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 137 | Verse 8] O Daughter of Babylon, doomed to destruction, blessed is he who repays you as you have done to us.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 137 | Verse 9] Blessed is he who seizes your infants and dashes them against the rocks.
