[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 1] Why do you boast of evil, O mighty man? The loving devotion of God endures all day long.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 2] Your tongue devises destruction like a sharpened razor, O worker of deceit.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 3] You love evil more than good, falsehood more than speaking truth. Selah
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 4] You love every word that devours, O deceitful tongue.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 5] Surely God will bring you down to everlasting ruin; He will snatch you up and tear you away from your tent; He will uproot you from the land of the living. Selah
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 6] The righteous will see and fear; they will mock the evildoer, saying,
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 7] “Look at the man who did not make God his refuge, but trusted in the abundance of his wealth and strengthened himself by destruction.”
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 8] But I am like an olive tree flourishing in the house of God; I trust in the loving devotion of God forever and ever.
[BSB | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 52 | Verse 9] I will praise You forever, because You have done it. I will wait on Your name— for it is good— in the presence of Your saints.
