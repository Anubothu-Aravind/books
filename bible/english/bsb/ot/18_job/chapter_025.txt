[BSB | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 25 | Verse 3] Can His troops be numbered? On whom does His light not rise?
[BSB | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 25 | Verse 4] How then can a man be just before God? How can one born of woman be pure?
[BSB | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 25 | Verse 5] If even the moon does not shine, and the stars are not pure in His sight,
[BSB | EN | OT | Job | Chapter 25 | Verse 6] how much less man, who is but a maggot, and the son of man, who is but a worm!”
