[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 1] Concerning Moab, this is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: “Woe to Nebo, for it will be devastated. Kiriathaim will be captured and disgraced; the fortress will be shattered and dismantled.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 2] There is no longer praise for Moab; in Heshbon they devise evil against her: ‘Come, let us cut her off from nationhood.’ You too, O people of Madmen, will be silenced; the sword will pursue you.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 3] A voice cries out from Horonaim: ‘Devastation and great destruction!’
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 4] Moab will be shattered; her little ones will cry out.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 5] For on the ascent to Luhith they weep bitterly as they go, and on the descent to Horonaim cries of distress resound over the destruction:
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 6] ‘Flee! Run for your lives! Become like a juniper in the desert.’
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 7] Because you trust in your works and treasures, you too will be captured, and Chemosh will go into exile with his priests and officials.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 8] The destroyer will move against every city, and not one town will escape. The valley will also be ruined, and the high plain will be destroyed, as the LORD has said.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 9] Put salt on Moab, for she will be laid waste; her cities will become desolate, with no one to dwell in them.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 10] Cursed is the one who is remiss in doing the work of the LORD, and cursed is he who withholds his sword from bloodshed.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 11] Moab has been at ease from youth, settled like wine on its dregs; he has not been poured from vessel to vessel or gone into exile. So his flavor has remained the same, and his aroma is unchanged.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 12] Therefore behold, the days are coming, declares the LORD, when I will send to him wanderers, who will pour him out. They will empty his vessels and shatter his jars.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 13] Then Moab will be ashamed of Chemosh, just as the house of Israel was ashamed when they trusted in Bethel.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 14] How can you say, ‘We are warriors, mighty men ready for battle’?
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 15] Moab has been destroyed and its towns have been invaded; the best of its young men have gone down in the slaughter, declares the King, whose name is the LORD of Hosts.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 16] Moab’s calamity is at hand, and his affliction is rushing swiftly.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 17] Mourn for him, all you who surround him, everyone who knows his name; tell how the mighty scepter is shattered— the glorious staff!
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 18] Come down from your glory; sit on parched ground, O daughter dwelling in Dibon, for the destroyer of Moab has come against you; he has destroyed your fortresses.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 19] Stand by the road and watch, O dweller of Aroer! Ask the man fleeing or the woman escaping, ‘What has happened?’
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 20] Moab is put to shame, for it has been shattered. Wail and cry out! Declare by the Arnon that Moab is destroyed.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 21] Judgment has come upon the high plain— upon Holon, Jahzah, and Mephaath,
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 22] upon Dibon, Nebo, and Beth-diblathaim,
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 23] upon Kiriathaim, Beth-gamul, and Beth-meon,
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 24] upon Kerioth, Bozrah, and all the towns of Moab, those far and near.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 25] The horn of Moab has been cut off, and his arm is broken,” declares the LORD.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 26] “Make him drunk, because he has magnified himself against the LORD; so Moab will wallow in his own vomit, and he will also become a laughingstock.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 27] Was not Israel your object of ridicule? Was he ever found among thieves? For whenever you speak of him you shake your head.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 28] Abandon the towns and settle among the rocks, O dwellers of Moab! Be like a dove that nests at the mouth of a cave.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 29] We have heard of Moab’s pomposity, his exceeding pride and conceit, his proud arrogance and haughtiness of heart.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 30] I know his insolence,” declares the LORD, “but it is futile. His boasting is as empty as his deeds.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 31] Therefore I will wail for Moab; I will cry out for all of Moab; I will moan for the men of Kir-heres.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 32] I will weep for you, O vine of Sibmah, more than I weep for Jazer. Your tendrils have extended to the sea; they reach even to Jazer. The destroyer has descended on your summer fruit and grape harvest.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 33] Joy and gladness are removed from the orchard and from the fields of Moab. I have stopped the flow of wine from the presses; no one treads them with shouts of joy; their shouts are not for joy.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 34] There is a cry from Heshbon to Elealeh; they raise their voices to Jahaz, from Zoar to Horonaim and Eglath-shelishiyah; for even the waters of Nimrim have dried up.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 35] In Moab, declares the LORD, I will bring an end to those who make offerings on the high places and burn incense to their gods.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 36] Therefore My heart laments like a flute for Moab; it laments like a flute for the men of Kir-heres, because the wealth they acquired has perished.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 37] For every head is shaved and every beard is clipped; on every hand is a gash, and around every waist is sackcloth.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 38] On all the rooftops of Moab and in the public squares, everyone is mourning; for I have shattered Moab like an unwanted jar,” declares the LORD.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 39] “How shattered it is! How they wail! How Moab has turned his back in shame! Moab has become an object of ridicule and horror to all those around him.”
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 40] For this is what the LORD says: “Behold, an eagle swoops down and spreads his wings against Moab.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 41] Kirioth has been taken, and the strongholds seized. In that day the heart of Moab’s warriors will be like the heart of a woman in labor.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 42] Moab will be destroyed as a nation because he vaunted himself against the LORD.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 43] Terror and pit and snare await you, O dweller of Moab,” declares the LORD.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 44] “Whoever flees the panic will fall into the pit, and whoever climbs from the pit will be caught in the snare. For I will bring upon Moab the year of their punishment,” declares the LORD.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 45] “Those who flee will stand helpless in Heshbon’s shadow, because fire has gone forth from Heshbon and a flame from within Sihon. It devours the foreheads of Moab and the skulls of the sons of tumult.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 46] Woe to you, O Moab! The people of Chemosh have perished; for your sons have been taken into exile and your daughters have gone into captivity.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 48 | Verse 47] Yet in the latter days I will restore Moab from captivity,” declares the LORD. Here ends the judgment on Moab.
