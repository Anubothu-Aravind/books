[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 1] This is the word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the people of Judah in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah king of Judah, which was the first year of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 2] So the prophet Jeremiah spoke to all the people of Judah and all the residents of Jerusalem as follows:
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 3] “From the thirteenth year of Josiah son of Amon king of Judah until this very day—twenty-three years—the word of the LORD has come to me, and I have spoken to you again and again, but you have not listened.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 4] And the LORD has sent all His servants the prophets to you again and again, but you have not listened or inclined your ear to hear.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 5] The prophets told you, ‘Turn now, each of you, from your evil ways and deeds, and you can dwell in the land that the LORD has given to you and your fathers forever and ever.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 6] Do not follow other gods to serve and worship them, and do not provoke Me to anger with the works of your hands. Then I will do you no harm.’
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 7] ‘But to your own harm, you have not listened to Me,’ declares the LORD, ‘so you have provoked Me to anger with the works of your hands.’
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 8] Therefore this is what the LORD of Hosts says: ‘Because you have not obeyed My words,
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 9] behold, I will summon all the families of the north, declares the LORD, and I will send for My servant Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, whom I will bring against this land, against its residents, and against all the surrounding nations. So I will devote them to destruction and make them an object of horror and contempt, an everlasting desolation.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 10] Moreover, I will banish from them the sounds of joy and gladness, the voices of the bride and bridegroom, the sound of the millstones, and the light of the lamp.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 11] And this whole land will become a desolate wasteland, and these nations will serve the king of Babylon for seventy years.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 12] But when seventy years are complete, I will punish the king of Babylon and that nation, the land of the Chaldeans, for their guilt, declares the LORD, and I will make it an everlasting desolation.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 13] I will bring upon that land all the words I have pronounced against it, all that is written in this book, which Jeremiah has prophesied against all the nations.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 14] For many nations and great kings will enslave them, and I will repay them according to their deeds and according to the work of their hands.’”
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 15] This is what the LORD, the God of Israel, said to me: “Take from My hand this cup of the wine of wrath, and make all the nations to whom I send you drink from it.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 16] And they will drink and stagger and go out of their minds, because of the sword that I will send among them.”
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 17] So I took the cup from the LORD’s hand and made all the nations drink from it, each one to whom the LORD had sent me,
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 18] to make them a ruin, an object of horror and contempt and cursing, as they are to this day—Jerusalem and the cities of Judah, its kings and officials;
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 19] Pharaoh king of Egypt, his officials, his leaders, and all his people;
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 20] all the mixed tribes; all the kings of Uz; all the kings of the Philistines: Ashkelon, Gaza, Ekron, and the remnant of Ashdod;
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 21] Edom, Moab, and the Ammonites;
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 22] all the kings of Tyre and Sidon; the kings of the coastlands across the sea;
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 23] Dedan, Tema, Buz, and all who cut the corners of their hair;
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 24] all the kings of Arabia, and all the kings of the mixed tribes who dwell in the desert;
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 25] all the kings of Zimri, Elam, and Media;
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 26] all the kings of the north, both near and far, one after another—all the kingdoms on the face of the earth. And after all of them, the king of Sheshach will drink it too.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 27] “Then you are to tell them that this is what the LORD of Hosts, the God of Israel, says: ‘Drink, get drunk, and vomit. Fall down and never get up again, because of the sword I will send among you.’
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 28] If they refuse to take the cup from your hand and drink it, you are to tell them that this is what the LORD of Hosts says: ‘You most certainly must drink it!
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 29] For behold, I am beginning to bring disaster on the city that bears My Name, so how could you possibly go unpunished? You will not go unpunished, for I am calling down a sword upon all the inhabitants of the earth, declares the LORD of Hosts.’
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 30] So you are to prophesy all these words against them and say to them: ‘The LORD will roar from on high; He will raise His voice from His holy habitation. He will roar loudly over His pasture; like those who tread the grapes, He will call out with a shout against all the inhabitants of the earth.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 31] The tumult will resound to the ends of the earth because the LORD brings a charge against the nations. He brings judgment on all mankind and puts the wicked to the sword,’” declares the LORD.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 32] This is what the LORD of Hosts says: “Behold! Disaster is spreading from nation to nation; a mighty storm is rising from the ends of the earth.”
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 33] Those slain by the LORD on that day will be spread from one end of the earth to the other. They will not be mourned, gathered, or buried. They will be like dung lying on the ground.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 34] Wail, you shepherds, and cry out; roll in the dust, you leaders of the flock. For the days of your slaughter have come; you will fall and be shattered like fine pottery.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 35] Flight will evade the shepherds, and escape will elude the leaders of the flock.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 36] Hear the cry of the shepherds, the wailing of the leaders of the flock, for the LORD is destroying their pasture.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 37] The peaceful meadows have been silenced because of the LORD’s burning anger.
[BSB | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 25 | Verse 38] He has left His den like a lion, for their land has been made a desolation by the sword of the oppressor, and because of the fierce anger of the LORD.
