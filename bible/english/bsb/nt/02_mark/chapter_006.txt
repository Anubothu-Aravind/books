[BSB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 22] When the daughter of Herodias came and danced, she pleased Herod and his guests, and the king said to the girl, “Ask me for whatever you wish, and I will give it to you.”

[BSB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 26] The king was consumed with sorrow, but because of his oaths and his guests, he did not want to refuse her.
[BSB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 27] So without delay, the king commanded that John’s head be brought in. He sent an executioner, who went and beheaded him in the prison.

[BSB | EN | NT | Mark | Chapter 6 | Verse 29] When John’s disciples heard about this, they came and took his body and placed it in a tomb.
