[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 1] As a prisoner in the Lord, then, I urge you to walk in a manner worthy of the calling you have received:
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 2] with all humility and gentleness, with patience, bearing with one another in love,
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 3] and with diligence to preserve the unity of the Spirit through the bond of peace.
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 4] There is one body and one Spirit, just as you were called to one hope when you were called;
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 5] one Lord, one faith, one baptism;
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 6] one God and Father of all, who is over all and through all and in all.
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 7] Now to each one of us grace has been given according to the measure of the gift of Christ.
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 8] This is why it says: “When He ascended on high, He led captives away, and gave gifts to men.”
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 9] What does “He ascended” mean, except that He also descended to the lower parts of the earth?
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 10] He who descended is the very One who ascended above all the heavens, in order to fill all things.
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 11] And it was He who gave some to be apostles, some to be prophets, some to be evangelists, and some to be pastors and teachers,
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 12] to equip the saints for works of ministry and to build up the body of Christ,
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 13] until we all reach unity in the faith and in the knowledge of the Son of God, as we mature to the full measure of the stature of Christ.
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 14] Then we will no longer be infants, tossed about by the waves and carried around by every wind of teaching and by the clever cunning of men in their deceitful scheming.
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 15] Instead, speaking the truth in love, we will in all things grow up into Christ Himself, who is the head.
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 16] From Him the whole body, fitted and held together by every supporting ligament, grows and builds itself up in love through the work of each individual part.
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 17] So I tell you this, and insist on it in the Lord, that you must no longer walk as the Gentiles do, in the futility of their thinking.
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 18] They are darkened in their understanding and alienated from the life of God because of the ignorance that is in them due to the hardness of their hearts.
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 19] Having lost all sense of shame, they have given themselves over to sensuality for the practice of every kind of impurity, with a craving for more.
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 20] But this is not the way you came to know Christ.
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 21] Surely you heard of Him and were taught in Him—in keeping with the truth that is in Jesus—
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 22] to put off your former way of life, your old self, which is being corrupted by its deceitful desires;
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 23] to be renewed in the spirit of your minds;
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 24] and to put on the new self, created to be like God in true righteousness and holiness.
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 25] Therefore each of you must put off falsehood and speak truthfully to his neighbor, for we are all members of one another.
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 26] “Be angry, yet do not sin.” Do not let the sun set upon your anger,
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 27] and do not give the devil a foothold.
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 28] He who has been stealing must steal no longer, but must work, doing good with his own hands, that he may have something to share with the one in need.
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 29] Let no unwholesome talk come out of your mouths, but only what is helpful for building up the one in need and bringing grace to those who listen.
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 30] And do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God, in whom you were sealed for the day of redemption.
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 31] Get rid of all bitterness, rage and anger, outcry and slander, along with every form of malice.
[BSB | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 4 | Verse 32] Be kind and tenderhearted to one another, forgiving each other just as in Christ God forgave you.
