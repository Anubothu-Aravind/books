[BSB | EN | NT | Revelation | Chapter 15 | Verse 6] And out of the temple came the seven angels with the seven plagues, dressed in clean and bright linen and girded with golden sashes around their chests.
[BSB | EN | NT | Revelation | Chapter 15 | Verse 7] Then one of the four living creatures gave the seven angels seven golden bowls full of the wrath of God, who lives forever and ever.
