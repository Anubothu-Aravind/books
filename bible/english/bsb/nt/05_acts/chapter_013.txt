[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 1] Now in the church at Antioch there were prophets and teachers: Barnabas, Simeon called Niger, Lucius of Cyrene, Manaen (who had been brought up with Herod the tetrarch), and Saul.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 2] While they were worshiping the Lord and fasting, the Holy Spirit said, “Set apart for Me Barnabas and Saul for the work to which I have called them.”
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 3] And after they had fasted and prayed, they laid their hands on them and sent them off.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 4] So Barnabas and Saul, sent forth by the Holy Spirit, went down to Seleucia and sailed from there to Cyprus.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 5] When they arrived at Salamis, they proclaimed the word of God in the Jewish synagogues. And John was with them as their helper.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 6] They traveled through the whole island as far as Paphos, where they found a Jewish sorcerer and false prophet named Bar-Jesus,
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 7] an attendant of the proconsul, Sergius Paulus. The proconsul, a man of intelligence, summoned Barnabas and Saul because he wanted to hear the word of God.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 8] But Elymas the sorcerer (for that is what his name means) opposed them and tried to turn the proconsul from the faith.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 9] Then Saul, who was also called Paul, filled with the Holy Spirit, looked directly at Elymas
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 10] and said, “O child of the devil and enemy of all righteousness, you are full of all kinds of deceit and trickery! Will you never stop perverting the straight ways of the Lord?
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 11] Now look, the hand of the Lord is against you, and for a time you will be blind and unable to see the light of the sun.” Immediately mist and darkness came over him, and he groped about, seeking someone to lead him by the hand.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 12] When the proconsul saw what had happened, he believed, for he was astonished at the teaching about the Lord.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 13] After setting sail from Paphos, Paul and his companions came to Perga in Pamphylia, where John left them to return to Jerusalem.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 14] And from Perga, they traveled inland to Pisidian Antioch, where they entered the synagogue on the Sabbath and sat down.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 15] After the reading from the Law and the Prophets, the synagogue leaders sent word to them: “Brothers, if you have a word of encouragement for the people, please speak.”
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 16] Paul stood up, motioned with his hand, and began to speak: “Men of Israel and you Gentiles who fear God, listen to me!
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 17] The God of the people of Israel chose our fathers. He made them into a great people during their stay in Egypt, and with an uplifted arm He led them out of that land.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 18] He endured their conduct for about forty years in the wilderness.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 19] And having vanquished seven nations in Canaan, He gave their land to His people as an inheritance.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 20] All this took about 450 years. After this, God gave them judges until the time of Samuel the prophet.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 21] Then the people asked for a king, and God gave them forty years under Saul son of Kish, from the tribe of Benjamin.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 22] After removing Saul, He raised up David as their king and testified about him: ‘I have found David son of Jesse a man after My own heart; he will carry out My will in its entirety.’
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 23] From the descendants of this man, God has brought to Israel the Savior Jesus, as He promised.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 24] Before the arrival of Jesus, John preached a baptism of repentance to all the people of Israel.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 25] As John was completing his course, he said, ‘Who do you suppose I am? I am not that One. But He is coming after me whose sandals I am not worthy to untie.’
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 26] Brothers, children of Abraham, and you Gentiles who fear God, it is to us that this message of salvation has been sent.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 27] The people of Jerusalem and their rulers did not recognize Jesus, yet in condemning Him they fulfilled the words of the prophets that are read every Sabbath.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 28] And though they found no ground for a death sentence, they asked Pilate to have Him executed.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 29] When they had carried out all that was written about Him, they took Him down from the tree and laid Him in a tomb.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 30] But God raised Him from the dead,
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 31] and for many days He was seen by those who had accompanied Him from Galilee to Jerusalem. They are now His witnesses to our people.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 32] And now we proclaim to you the good news: What God promised our fathers
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 33] He has fulfilled for us, their children, by raising up Jesus. As it is written in the second Psalm: ‘You are My Son; today I have become Your Father.’
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 34] In fact, God raised Him from the dead, never to see decay. As He has said: ‘I will give you the holy and sure blessings promised to David.’
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 35] So also, He says in another Psalm: ‘You will not let Your Holy One see decay.’
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 36] For when David had served God’s purpose in his own generation, he fell asleep. His body was buried with his fathers and saw decay.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 37] But the One whom God raised from the dead did not see decay.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 38] Therefore let it be known to you, brothers, that through Jesus the forgiveness of sins is proclaimed to you.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 39] Through Him everyone who believes is justified from everything you could not be justified from by the law of Moses.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 40] Watch out, then, that what was spoken by the prophets does not happen to you:
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 41] ‘Look, you scoffers, wonder and perish! For I am doing a work in your days that you would never believe, even if someone told you.’”
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 42] As Paul and Barnabas were leaving the synagogue, the people urged them to continue this message on the next Sabbath.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 43] After the synagogue was dismissed, many of the Jews and devout converts to Judaism followed Paul and Barnabas, who spoke to them and urged them to continue in the grace of God.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 44] On the following Sabbath, nearly the whole city gathered to hear the word of the Lord.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 45] But when the Jews saw the crowds, they were filled with jealousy, and they blasphemously contradicted what Paul was saying.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 46] Then Paul and Barnabas answered them boldly: “It was necessary to speak the word of God to you first. But since you reject it and do not consider yourselves worthy of eternal life, we now turn to the Gentiles.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 47] For this is what the Lord has commanded us: ‘I have made you a light for the Gentiles, to bring salvation to the ends of the earth.’”
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 48] When the Gentiles heard this, they rejoiced and glorified the word of the Lord, and all who were appointed for eternal life believed.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 49] And the word of the Lord spread throughout that region.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 50] The Jews, however, incited the religious women of prominence and the leading men of the city. They stirred up persecution against Paul and Barnabas and drove them out of their district.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 51] So they shook the dust off their feet in protest against them and went to Iconium.
[BSB | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 13 | Verse 52] And the disciples were filled with joy and with the Holy Spirit.
