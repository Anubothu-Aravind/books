[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 27 | Verse 1] When morning came, all the chief priests and elders of the people conspired against Jesus to put Him to death.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 27 | Verse 2] They bound Him, led Him away, and handed Him over to Pilate the governor.

[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 27 | Verse 4] “I have sinned by betraying innocent blood,” he said. “What is that to us?” they replied. “You bear the responsibility.”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 27 | Verse 5] So Judas threw the silver into the temple and left. Then he went away and hanged himself.

[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 27 | Verse 9] Then what was spoken through Jeremiah the prophet was fulfilled: “They took the thirty pieces of silver, the price set on Him by the people of Israel,
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 27 | Verse 10] and they gave them for the potter’s field, as the Lord had commanded me.”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 27 | Verse 11] Meanwhile Jesus stood before the governor, who questioned Him: “Are You the King of the Jews?” “You have said so,” Jesus replied.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 27 | Verse 12] And when He was accused by the chief priests and elders, He gave no answer.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 27 | Verse 13] Then Pilate asked Him, “Do You not hear how many charges they are bringing against You?”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 27 | Verse 14] But Jesus gave no answer, not even to a single charge, much to the governor’s amazement.

[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 27 | Verse 17] So when the crowd had assembled, Pilate asked them, “Which one do you want me to release to you: Barabbas, or Jesus who is called Christ?”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 27 | Verse 18] For he knew it was out of envy that they had handed Jesus over to him.

[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 27 | Verse 20] But the chief priests and elders persuaded the crowds to ask for Barabbas and to have Jesus put to death.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 27 | Verse 21] “Which of the two do you want me to release to you?” asked the governor. “Barabbas,” they replied.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 27 | Verse 22] “What then should I do with Jesus who is called Christ?” Pilate asked. They all answered, “Crucify Him!”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 27 | Verse 23] “Why?” asked Pilate. “What evil has He done?” But they shouted all the louder, “Crucify Him!”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 27 | Verse 24] When Pilate saw that he was accomplishing nothing, but that instead a riot was breaking out, he took water and washed his hands before the crowd. “I am innocent of this man’s blood,” he said. “You bear the responsibility.”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 27 | Verse 25] All the people answered, “His blood be on us and on our children!”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 27 | Verse 26] So Pilate released Barabbas to them. But he had Jesus flogged, and handed Him over to be crucified.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 27 | Verse 27] Then the governor’s soldiers took Jesus into the Praetorium and gathered the whole company around Him.
