[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 28 | Verse 1] After the Sabbath, at dawn on the first day of the week, Mary Magdalene and the other Mary went to see the tomb.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 28 | Verse 2] Suddenly there was a great earthquake, for an angel of the Lord descended from heaven, rolled away the stone, and sat on it.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 28 | Verse 3] His appearance was like lightning, and his clothes were white as snow.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 28 | Verse 4] The guards trembled in fear of him and became like dead men.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 28 | Verse 5] But the angel said to the women, “Do not be afraid, for I know that you are looking for Jesus, who was crucified.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 28 | Verse 6] He is not here; He has risen, just as He said! Come, see the place where He lay.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 28 | Verse 7] Then go quickly and tell His disciples, ‘He has risen from the dead and is going ahead of you into Galilee. There you will see Him.’ See, I have told you.”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 28 | Verse 8] So they hurried away from the tomb in fear and great joy, and ran to tell His disciples.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 28 | Verse 9] Suddenly Jesus met them and said, “Greetings!” They came to Him, grasped His feet, and worshiped Him.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 28 | Verse 10] “Do not be afraid,” said Jesus. “Go and tell My brothers to go to Galilee. There they will see Me.”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 28 | Verse 11] While the women were on their way, some of the guards went into the city and reported to the chief priests all that had happened.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 28 | Verse 12] And after the chief priests had met with the elders and formed a plan, they gave the soldiers a large sum of money
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 28 | Verse 13] and instructed them: “You are to say, ‘His disciples came by night and stole Him away while we were asleep.’
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 28 | Verse 14] If this report reaches the governor, we will satisfy him and keep you out of trouble.”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 28 | Verse 15] So the guards took the money and did as they were instructed. And this account has been circulated among the Jews to this very day.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 28 | Verse 16] Meanwhile, the eleven disciples went to Galilee, to the mountain Jesus had designated.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 28 | Verse 17] When they saw Him, they worshiped Him, but some doubted.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 28 | Verse 18] Then Jesus came to them and said, “All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to Me.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 28 | Verse 19] Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit,
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 28 | Verse 20] and teaching them to obey all that I have commanded you. And surely I am with you always, even to the end of the age.”
