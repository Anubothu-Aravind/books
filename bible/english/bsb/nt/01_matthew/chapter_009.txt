[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 2] Just then some men brought to Him a paralytic lying on a mat. When Jesus saw their faith, He said to the paralytic, “Take courage, son; your sins are forgiven.”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 3] On seeing this, some of the scribes said to themselves, “This man is blaspheming!”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 4] But Jesus knew what they were thinking and said, “Why do you harbor evil in your hearts?
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 5] Which is easier: to say, ‘Your sins are forgiven,’ or to say, ‘Get up and walk?’
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 6] But so that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins...” Then He said to the paralytic, “Get up, pick up your mat, and go home.”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 7] And the man got up and went home.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 8] When the crowds saw this, they were filled with awe and glorified God, who had given such authority to men.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 9] As Jesus went on from there, He saw a man named Matthew sitting at the tax booth. “Follow Me,” He told him, and Matthew got up and followed Him.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 10] Later, as Jesus was dining at Matthew’s house, many tax collectors and sinners came and ate with Him and His disciples.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 11] When the Pharisees saw this, they asked His disciples, “Why does your Teacher eat with tax collectors and sinners?”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 12] On hearing this, Jesus said, “It is not the healthy who need a doctor, but the sick.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 13] But go and learn what this means: ‘I desire mercy, not sacrifice.’ For I have not come to call the righteous, but sinners.”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 14] At that time John’s disciples came to Jesus and asked, “Why is it that we and the Pharisees fast so often, but Your disciples do not fast?”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 15] Jesus replied, “How can the guests of the bridegroom mourn while He is with them? But the time will come when the bridegroom will be taken from them; then they will fast.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 16] No one sews a patch of unshrunk cloth on an old garment. For the patch will pull away from the garment, and a worse tear will result.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 17] Neither do men pour new wine into old wineskins. If they do, the skins will burst, the wine will spill, and the wineskins will be ruined. Instead, they pour new wine into new wineskins, and both are preserved.”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 18] While Jesus was saying these things, a synagogue leader came and knelt before Him. “My daughter has just died,” he said. “But come and place Your hand on her, and she will live.”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 19] So Jesus got up and went with him, along with His disciples.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 20] Suddenly a woman who had suffered from bleeding for twelve years came up behind Him and touched the fringe of His cloak.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 21] She said to herself, “If only I touch His cloak, I will be healed.”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 22] Jesus turned and saw her. “Take courage, daughter,” He said, “your faith has healed you.” And the woman was cured from that very hour.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 23] When Jesus entered the house of the synagogue leader, He saw the flute players and the noisy crowd.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 24] “Go away,” He told them. “The girl is not dead, but asleep.” And they laughed at Him.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 25] After the crowd had been put outside, Jesus went in and took the girl by the hand, and she got up.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 26] And the news about this spread throughout that region.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 27] As Jesus went on from there, two blind men followed Him, crying out, “Have mercy on us, Son of David!”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 28] After Jesus had entered the house, the blind men came to Him. “Do you believe that I am able to do this?” He asked. “Yes, Lord,” they answered.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 29] Then He touched their eyes and said, “According to your faith will it be done to you.”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 30] And their eyes were opened. Jesus warned them sternly, “See that no one finds out about this!”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 31] But they went out and spread the news about Him throughout the land.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 32] As they were leaving, a demon-possessed man who was mute was brought to Jesus.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 33] And when the demon had been driven out, the man began to speak. The crowds were amazed and said, “Nothing like this has ever been seen in Israel!”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 34] But the Pharisees said, “It is by the prince of demons that He drives out demons.”
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 35] Jesus went through all the towns and villages, teaching in their synagogues, preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing every disease and sickness.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 36] When He saw the crowds, He was moved with compassion for them, because they were harassed and helpless, like sheep without a shepherd.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 37] Then He said to His disciples, “The harvest is plentiful, but the workers are few.
[BSB | EN | NT | Matthew | Chapter 9 | Verse 38] Ask the Lord of the harvest, therefore, to send out workers into His harvest.”
