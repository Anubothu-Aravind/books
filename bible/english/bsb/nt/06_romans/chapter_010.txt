[BSB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 10 | Verse 1] Brothers, my heart’s desire and prayer to God for the Israelites is for their salvation.
[BSB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 10 | Verse 2] For I testify about them that they are zealous for God, but not on the basis of knowledge.
[BSB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 10 | Verse 3] Because they were ignorant of God’s righteousness and sought to establish their own, they did not submit to God’s righteousness.
[BSB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 10 | Verse 4] For Christ is the end of the law, to bring righteousness to everyone who believes.
[BSB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 10 | Verse 5] For concerning the righteousness that is by the law, Moses writes: “The man who does these things will live by them.”
[BSB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 10 | Verse 6] But the righteousness that is by faith says: “Do not say in your heart, ‘Who will ascend into heaven?’ (that is, to bring Christ down)
[BSB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 10 | Verse 7] or, ‘Who will descend into the Abyss?’ (that is, to bring Christ up from the dead).”
[BSB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 10 | Verse 8] But what does it say? “The word is near you; it is in your mouth and in your heart,” that is, the word of faith we are proclaiming:
[BSB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 10 | Verse 9] that if you confess with your mouth, “Jesus is Lord,” and believe in your heart that God raised Him from the dead, you will be saved.
[BSB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 10 | Verse 10] For with your heart you believe and are justified, and with your mouth you confess and are saved.
[BSB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 10 | Verse 11] It is just as the Scripture says: “Anyone who believes in Him will never be put to shame.”
[BSB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 10 | Verse 12] For there is no difference between Jew and Greek: The same Lord is Lord of all, and gives richly to all who call on Him,
[BSB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 10 | Verse 13] for, “Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.”
[BSB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 10 | Verse 14] How then can they call on the One in whom they have not believed? And how can they believe in the One of whom they have not heard? And how can they hear without someone to preach?
[BSB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 10 | Verse 15] And how can they preach unless they are sent? As it is written: “How beautiful are the feet of those who bring good news!”
[BSB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 10 | Verse 16] But not all of them welcomed the good news. For Isaiah says, “Lord, who has believed our message?”
[BSB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 10 | Verse 17] Consequently, faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the word of Christ.
[BSB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 10 | Verse 18] But I ask, did they not hear? Indeed they did: “Their voice has gone out into all the earth, their words to the ends of the world.”
[BSB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 10 | Verse 19] I ask instead, did Israel not understand? First, Moses says: “I will make you jealous by those who are not a nation; I will make you angry by a nation without understanding.”
[BSB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 10 | Verse 20] And Isaiah boldly says: “I was found by those who did not seek Me; I revealed Myself to those who did not ask for Me.”
[BSB | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 10 | Verse 21] But as for Israel he says: “All day long I have held out My hands to a disobedient and obstinate people.”
