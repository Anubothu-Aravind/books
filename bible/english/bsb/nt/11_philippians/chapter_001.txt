[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 1] Paul and Timothy, servants of Christ Jesus, To all the saints in Christ Jesus at Philippi, together with the overseers and deacons:
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 2] Grace and peace to you from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 3] I thank my God every time I remember you.
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 4] In every prayer for all of you, I always pray with joy,
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 5] because of your partnership in the gospel from the first day until now,
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 6] being confident of this, that He who began a good work in you will carry it on to completion until the day of Christ Jesus.
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 7] It is right for me to feel this way about all of you, since I have you in my heart. For in my chains and in my defense and confirmation of the gospel, you are all partners in grace with me.
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 8] God is my witness how I long for all of you with the affection of Christ Jesus.
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 9] And this is my prayer: that your love may abound more and more in knowledge and depth of insight,
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 10] so that you may be able to test and prove what is best and may be pure and blameless for the day of Christ,
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 11] filled with the fruit of righteousness that comes through Jesus Christ, to the glory and praise of God.
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 12] Now I want you to know, brothers, that my circumstances have actually served to advance the gospel.
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 13] As a result, it has become clear throughout the whole palace guard and to everyone else that I am in chains for Christ.
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 14] And most of the brothers, confident in the Lord by my chains, now dare more greatly to speak the word without fear.
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 15] It is true that some preach Christ out of envy and rivalry, but others out of goodwill.
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 16] The latter do so in love, knowing that I am appointed for the defense of the gospel.
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 17] The former, however, preach Christ out of selfish ambition, not sincerely, supposing that they can add to the distress of my chains.
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 18] What then is the issue? Just this: that in every way, whether by false motives or true, Christ is preached. And in this I rejoice. Yes, and I will continue to rejoice,
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 19] because I know that through your prayers and the provision of the Spirit of Jesus Christ, my distress will turn out for my deliverance.
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 20] I eagerly expect and hope that I will in no way be ashamed, but will have complete boldness so that now as always Christ will be exalted in my body, whether by life or by death.
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 21] For to me, to live is Christ, and to die is gain.
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 22] But if I go on living in the body, this will mean fruitful labor for me. So what shall I choose? I do not know.
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 23] I am torn between the two. I desire to depart and be with Christ, which is far better indeed.
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 24] But it is more necessary for you that I remain in the body.
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 25] Convinced of this, I know that I will remain and will continue with all of you for your progress and joy in the faith,
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 26] so that through my coming to you again your exultation in Christ Jesus will resound on account of me.
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 27] Nevertheless, conduct yourselves in a manner worthy of the gospel of Christ. Then, whether I come and see you or only hear about you in my absence, I will know that you stand firm in one spirit, contending side by side for the faith of the gospel,
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 28] without being frightened in any way by those who oppose you. This is a clear sign of their destruction but of your salvation, and it is from God.
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 29] For it has been granted to you on behalf of Christ not only to believe in Him, but also to suffer for Him,
[BSB | EN | NT | Philippians | Chapter 1 | Verse 30] since you are encountering the same struggle you saw I had, and now hear that I still have.
