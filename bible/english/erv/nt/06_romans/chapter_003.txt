[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 1] What advantage then hath the Jew? or what is the profit of circumcision?
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 2] Much every way: first of all, that they were intrusted with the oracles of God.
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 3] For what if some were without faith? shall their want of faith make of none effect the faithfulness of God?
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 4] God forbid: yea, let God be found true, but every man a liar; as it is written, That thou mightest be justified in thy words, And mightest prevail when thou comest into judgment.
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 5] But if our unrighteousness commendeth the righteousness of God, what shall we say? Is God unrighteous who visiteth with wrath? ( I speak after the manner of men.)
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 6] God forbid: for then how shall God judge the world?
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 7] But if the truth of God through my lie abounded unto his glory, why am I also still judged as a sinner?
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 8] and why not ( as we be slanderously reported, and as some affirm that we say), Let us do evil, that good may come? whose condemnation is just.
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 9] What then? are we in worse case than they? No, in no wise: for we before laid to the charge both of Jews and Greeks, that they are all under sin;
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 10] as it is written, There is none righteous, no, not one;
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 11] There is none that understandeth, There is none that seeketh after God;
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 12] They have all turned aside, they are together become unprofitable; There is none that doeth good, no, not so much as one:
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 13] Their throat is an open sepulchre; With their tongues they have used deceit: The poison of asps is under their lips:
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 14] Whose mouth is full of cursing and bitterness:
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 15] Their feet are swift to shed blood;
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 16] Destruction and misery are in their ways;
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 17] And the way of peace have they not known:
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 18] There is no fear of God before their eyes.
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 19] Now we know that what things soever the law saith, it speaketh to them that are under the law; that every mouth may be stopped, and all the world may be brought under the judgment of God:
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 20] because by the works of the law shall no flesh be justified in his sight: for through the law cometh the knowledge of sin.
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 21] But now apart from the law a righteousness of God hath been manifested, being witnessed by the law and the prophets;
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 22] even the righteousness of God through faith in Jesus Christ unto all them that believe; for there is no distinction;
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 23] for all have sinned, and fall short of the glory of God;
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 24] being justified freely by his grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus:
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 25] whom God set forth to be a propitiation, through faith, by his blood, to shew his righteousness, because of the passing over of the sins done aforetime, in the forbearance of God;
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 26] for the shewing, I say, of his righteousness at this present season: that he might himself be just, and the justifier of him that hath faith in Jesus.
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 27] Where then is the glorying? It is excluded. By what manner of law? of works? Nay: but by a law of faith.
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 28] We reckon therefore that a man is justified by faith apart from the works of the law.
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 29] Or is God the God of Jews only? is he not the God of Gentiles also? Yea, of Gentiles also:
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 30] if so be that God is one, and he shall justify the circumcision by faith, and the uncircumcision through faith.
[ERV | EN | NT | Romans | Chapter 3 | Verse 31] Do we then make the law of none effect through faith? God forbid: nay, we establish the law.
