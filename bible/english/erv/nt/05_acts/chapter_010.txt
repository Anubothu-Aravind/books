[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 1] Now there was a certain man in Caesarea, Cornelius by name, a centurion of the band called the Italian band,
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 2] a devout man, and one that feared God with all his house, who gave much alms to the people, and prayed to God alway.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 3] He saw in a vision openly, as it were about the ninth hour of the day, an angel of God coming in unto him, and saying to him, Cornelius.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 4] And he, fastening his eyes upon him, and being affrighted, said, What is it, Lord? And he said unto him, Thy prayers and thine alms are gone up for a memorial before God.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 5] And now send men to Joppa, and fetch one Simon, who is surnamed Peter:
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 6] he lodgeth with one Simon a tanner, whose house is by the sea side.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 7] And when the angel that spake unto him was departed, he called two of his household-servants, and a devout soldier of them that waited on him continually;
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 8] and having rehearsed all things unto them, he sent them to Joppa.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 9] Now on the morrow, as they were on their journey, and drew nigh unto the city, Peter went up upon the housetop to pray, about the sixth hour:
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 10] and he became hungry, and desired to eat: but while they made ready, he fell into a trance;
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 11] and he beholdeth the heaven opened, and a certain vessel descending, as it were a great sheet, let down by four corners upon the earth:
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 12] wherein were all manner of fourfooted beasts and creeping things of the earth and fowls of the heaven.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 13] And there came a voice to him, Rise, Peter; kill and eat.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 14] But Peter said, Not so, Lord; for I have never eaten anything that is common and unclean.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 15] And a voice came unto him again the second time, What God hath cleansed, make not thou common.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 16] And this was done thrice: and straightway the vessel was received up into heaven.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 17] Now while Peter was much perplexed in himself what the vision which he had seen might mean, behold, the men that were sent by Cornelius, having made inquiry for Simon’s house, stood before the gate,
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 18] and called and asked whether Simon, which was surnamed Peter, were lodging there.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 19] And while Peter thought on the vision, the Spirit said unto him, Behold, three men seek thee.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 20] But arise, and get thee down, and go with them, nothing doubting: for I have sent them.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 21] And Peter went down to the men, and said, Behold, I am he whom ye seek: what is the cause wherefore ye are come?
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 22] And they said, Cornelius a centurion, a righteous man and one that feareth God, and well reported of by all the nation of the Jews, was warned of God by a holy angel to send for thee into his house, and to hear words from thee.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 23] So he called them in and lodged them. And on the morrow he arose and went forth with them, and certain of the brethren from Joppa accompanied him.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 24] And on the morrow they entered into Caesarea. And Cornelius was waiting for them, having called together his kinsmen and his near friends.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 25] And when it came to pass that Peter entered, Cornelius met him, and fell down at his feet, and worshipped him.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 26] But Peter raised him up, saying, Stand up; I myself also am a man.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 27] And as he talked with him, he went in, and findeth many come together:
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 28] and he said unto them, Ye yourselves know how that it is an unlawful thing for a man that is a Jew to join himself or come unto one of another nation; and yet unto me hath God shewed that I should not call any man common or unclean:
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 29] wherefore also I came without gainsaying, when I was sent for. I ask therefore with what intent ye sent for me.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 30] And Cornelius said, Four days ago, until this hour, I was keeping the ninth hour of prayer in my house; and behold, a man stood before me in bright apparel,
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 31] and saith, Cornelius, thy prayer is heard, and thine alms are had in remembrance in the sight of God.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 32] Send therefore to Joppa, and call unto thee Simon, who is surnamed Peter; he lodgeth in the house of Simon a tanner, by the sea side.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 33] Forthwith therefore I sent to thee; and thou hast well done that thou art come. Now therefore we are all here present in the sight of God, to hear all things that have been commanded thee of the Lord.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 34] And Peter opened his mouth, and said, Of a truth I perceive that God is no respecter of persons:
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 35] but in every nation he that feareth him, and worketh righteousness, is acceptable to him.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 36] The word which he sent unto the children of Israel, preaching good tidings of peace by Jesus Christ ( he is Lord of all)—

[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 39] And we are witnesses of all things which he did both in the country of the Jews, and in Jerusalem; whom also they slew, hanging him on a tree.

[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 41] not to all the people, but unto witnesses that were chosen before of God, even to us, who did eat and drink with him after he rose from the dead.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 42] And he charged us to preach unto the people, and to testify that this is he which is ordained of God to be the Judge of quick and dead.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 43] To him bear all the prophets witness, that through his name every one that believeth on him shall receive remission of sins.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 44] While Peter yet spake these words, the Holy Ghost fell on all them which heard the word.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 45] And they of the circumcision which believed were amazed, as many as came with Peter, because that on the Gentiles also was poured out the gift of the Holy Ghost.
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 46] For they heard them speak with tongues, and magnify God. Then answered Peter,
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 47] Can any man forbid the water, that these should not be baptized, which have received the Holy Ghost as well as we?
[ERV | EN | NT | Acts | Chapter 10 | Verse 48] And he commanded them to be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ. Then prayed they him to tarry certain days.
