[ERV | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 1 | Verse 1] Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus through the will of God, to the saints which are at Ephesus, and the faithful in Christ Jesus:
[ERV | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 1 | Verse 2] Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.
[ERV | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 1 | Verse 3] Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who hath blessed us with every spiritual blessing in the heavenly places in Christ:
[ERV | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 1 | Verse 4] even as he chose us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and without blemish before him in love:
[ERV | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 1 | Verse 5] having foreordained us unto adoption as sons through Jesus Christ unto himself, according to the good pleasure of his will,
[ERV | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 1 | Verse 6] to the praise of the glory of his grace, which he freely bestowed on us in the Beloved:
[ERV | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 1 | Verse 7] in whom we have our redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of our trespasses, according to the riches of his grace,
[ERV | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 1 | Verse 8] which he made to abound toward us in all wisdom and prudence,
[ERV | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 1 | Verse 9] having made known unto us the mystery of his will, according to his good pleasure which he purposed in him
[ERV | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 1 | Verse 10] unto a dispensation of the fulness of the times, to sum up all things in Christ, the things in the heavens, and the things upon the earth; in him, I say,
[ERV | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 1 | Verse 11] in whom also we were made a heritage, having been foreordained according to the purpose of him who worketh all things after the counsel of his will;
[ERV | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 1 | Verse 12] to the end that we should be unto the praise of his glory, we who had before hoped in Christ:
[ERV | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 1 | Verse 13] in whom ye also, having heard the word of the truth, the gospel of your salvation,— in whom, having also believed, ye were sealed with the Holy Spirit of promise,
[ERV | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 1 | Verse 14] which is an earnest of our inheritance, unto the redemption of God’s own possession, unto the praise of his glory.
[ERV | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 1 | Verse 15] For this cause I also, having heard of the faith in the Lord Jesus which is among you, and which ye shew toward all the saints,
[ERV | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 1 | Verse 16] cease not to give thanks for you, making mention of you in my prayers;
[ERV | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 1 | Verse 17] that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give unto you a spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of him;
[ERV | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 1 | Verse 18] having the eyes of your heart enlightened, that ye may know what is the hope of his calling, what the riches of the glory of his inheritance in the saints,
[ERV | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 1 | Verse 19] and what the exceeding greatness of his power to us-ward who believe, according to that working of the strength of his might
[ERV | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 1 | Verse 20] which he wrought in Christ, when he raised him from the dead, and made him to sit at his right hand in the heavenly places,
[ERV | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 1 | Verse 21] far above all rule, and authority, and power, and dominion, and every name that is named, not only in this world, but also in that which is to come:
[ERV | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 1 | Verse 22] and he put all things in subjection under his feet, and gave him to be head over all things to the church,
[ERV | EN | NT | Ephesians | Chapter 1 | Verse 23] which is his body, the fulness of him that filleth all in all.
