[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 1] And the third day there was a marriage in Cana of Galilee; and the mother of Jesus was there:
[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 2] and Jesus also was bidden, and his disciples, to the marriage.
[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 3] And when the wine failed, the mother of Jesus saith unto him, They have no wine.
[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 4] And Jesus saith unto her, Woman, what have I to do with thee? mine hour is not yet come.
[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 5] His mother saith unto the servants, Whatsoever he saith unto you, do it.
[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 6] Now there were six waterpots of stone set there after the Jews’ manner of purifying, containing two or three firkins apiece.
[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 7] Jesus saith unto them, Fill the waterpots with water. And they filled them up to the brim.
[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 8] And he saith unto them, Draw out now, and bear unto the ruler of the feast. And they bare it.
[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 9] And when the ruler of the feast tasted the water now become wine, and knew not whence it was ( but the servants which had drawn the water knew), the ruler of the feast calleth the bridegroom,
[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 10] and saith unto him, Every man setteth on first the good wine; and when men have drunk freely, then that which is worse: thou hast kept the good wine until now.
[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 11] This beginning of his signs did Jesus in Cana of Galilee, and manifested his glory; and his disciples believed on him.
[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 12] After this he went down to Capernaum, he, and his mother, and his brethren, and his disciples: and there they abode not many days.
[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 13] And the passover of the Jews was at hand, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem.
[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 14] And he found in the temple those that sold oxen and sheep and doves, and the changers of money sitting:
[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 15] and he made a scourge of cords, and cast all out of the temple, both the sheep and the oxen; and he poured out the changers’ money, and overthrew their tables;
[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 16] and to them that sold the doves he said, Take these things hence; make not my Father’s house a house of merchandise.
[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 17] His disciples remembered that it was written, The zeal of thine house shall eat me up.
[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 18] The Jews therefore answered and said unto him, What sign shewest thou unto us, seeing that thou doest these things?
[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 19] Jesus answered and said unto them, Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up.
[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 20] The Jews therefore said, Forty and six years was this temple in building, and wilt thou raise it up in three days?
[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 21] But he spake of the temple of his body.
[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 22] When therefore he was raised from the dead, his disciples remembered that he spake this; and they believed the scripture, and the word which Jesus had said.
[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 23] Now when he was in Jerusalem at the passover, during the feast, many believed on his name, beholding his signs which he did.
[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 24] But Jesus did not trust himself unto them, for that he knew all men,
[ERV | EN | NT | John | Chapter 2 | Verse 25] and because he needed not that any one should bear witness concerning man; for he himself knew what was in man.
