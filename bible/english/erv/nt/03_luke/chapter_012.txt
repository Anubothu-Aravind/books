[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 2] But there is nothing covered up, that shall not be revealed: and hid, that shall not be known.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 3] Wherefore whatsoever ye have said in the darkness shall be heard in the light; and what ye have spoken in the ear in the inner chambers shall be proclaimed upon the housetops.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 4] And I say unto you my friends, Be not afraid of them which kill the body, and after that have no more that they can do.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 5] But I will warn you whom ye shall fear: Fear him, which after he hath killed hath power to cast into hell; yea, I say unto you, Fear him.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 6] Are not five sparrows sold for two farthings? and not one of them is forgotten in the sight of God.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 7] But the very hairs of your head are all numbered. Fear not: ye are of more value than many sparrows.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 8] And I say unto you, Every one who shall confess me before men, him shall the Son of man also confess before the angels of God:
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 9] but he that denieth me in the presence of men shall be denied in the presence of the angels of God.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 10] And every one who shall speak a word against the Son of man, it shall be forgiven him: but unto him that blasphemeth against the Holy Spirit it shall not be forgiven.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 11] And when they bring you before the synagogues, and the rulers, and the authorities, be not anxious how or what ye shall answer, or what ye shall say:
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 12] for the Holy Spirit shall teach you in that very hour what ye ought to say.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 13] And one out of the multitude said unto him, Master, bid my brother divide the inheritance with me.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 14] But he said unto him, Man, who made me a judge or a divider over you?
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 15] And he said unto them, Take heed, and keep yourselves from all covetousness: for a man’s life consisteth not in the abundance of the things which he possesseth.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 16] And he spake a parable unto them, saying, The ground of a certain rich man brought forth plentifully:
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 17] and he reasoned within himself, saying, What shall I do, because I have not where to bestow my fruits?
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 18] And he said, This will I do: I will pull down my barns, and build greater; and there will I bestow all my corn and my goods.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 19] And I will say to my soul, Soul, thou hast much goods laid up for many years; take thine ease, eat, drink, be merry.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 20] But God said unto him, Thou foolish one, this night is thy soul required of thee; and the things which thou hast prepared, whose shall they be?
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 21] So is he that layeth up treasure for himself, and is not rich toward God.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 22] And he said unto his disciples, Therefore I say unto you, Be not anxious for your life, what ye shall eat; nor yet for your body, what ye shall put on.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 23] For the life is more than the food, and the body than the raiment.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 24] Consider the ravens, that they sow not, neither reap; which have no store-chamber nor barn; and God feedeth them: of how much more value are ye than the birds!
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 25] And which of you by being anxious can add a cubit unto his stature?
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 26] If then ye are not able to do even that which is least, why are ye anxious concerning the rest?
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 27] Consider the lilies, how they grow: they toil not, neither do they spin; yet I say unto you, Even Solomon in all his glory was not arrayed like one of these.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 28] But if God doth so clothe the grass in the field, which today is, and tomorrow is cast into the oven; how much more shall he clothe you, O ye of little faith?
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 29] And seek not ye what ye shall eat, and what ye shall drink, neither be ye of doubtful mind.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 30] For all these things do the nations of the world seek after: but your Father knoweth that ye have need of these things.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 31] Howbeit seek ye his kingdom, and these things shall be added unto you.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 32] Fear not, little flock; for it is your Father’s good pleasure to give you the kingdom.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 33] Sell that ye have, and give alms; make for yourselves purses which wax not old, a treasure in the heavens that faileth not, where no thief draweth near, neither moth destroyeth.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 34] For where your treasure is, there will your heart be also.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 35] Let your loins be girded about, and your lamps burning;
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 36] and be ye yourselves like unto men looking for their lord, when he shall return from the marriage feast; that, when he cometh and knocketh, they may straightway open unto him.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 37] Blessed are those servants, whom the lord when he cometh shall find watching: verily I say unto you, that he shall gird himself, and make them sit down to meat, and shall come and serve them.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 38] And if he shall come in the second watch, and if in the third, and find them so, blessed are those servants.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 39] But know this, that if the master of the house had known in what hour the thief was coming, he would have watched, and not have left his house to be broken through.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 40] Be ye also ready: for in an hour that ye think not the Son of man cometh.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 41] And Peter said, Lord, speakest thou this parable unto us, or even unto all?
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 42] And the Lord said, Who then is the faithful and wise steward, whom his lord shall set over his household, to give them their portion of food in due season?
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 43] Blessed is that servant, whom his lord when he cometh shall find so doing.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 44] Of a truth I say unto you, that he will set him over all that he hath.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 45] But if that servant shall say in his heart, My lord delayeth his coming; and shall begin to beat the menservants and the maidservants, and to eat and drink, and to be drunken;
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 46] the lord of that servant shall come in a day when he expecteth not, and in an hour when he knoweth not, and shall cut him asunder, and appoint his portion with the unfaithful.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 47] And that servant, which knew his lord’ s will, and made not ready, nor did according to his will, shall be beaten with many stripes;
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 48] but he that knew not, and did things worthy of stripes, shall be beaten with few stripes. And to whomsoever much is given, of him shall much be required: and to whom they commit much, of him will they ask the more.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 49] I came to cast fire upon the earth; and what will I, if it is already kindled?
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 50] But I have a baptism to be baptized with; and how am I straitened till it be accomplished!
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 51] Think ye that I am come to give peace in the earth? I tell you, Nay; but rather division:
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 52] for there shall be from henceforth five in one house divided, three against two, and two against three.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 53] They shall be divided, father against son, and son against father; mother against daughter, and daughter against her mother; mother in law against her daughter in law, and daughter in law against her mother in law.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 54] And he said to the multitudes also, When ye see a cloud rising in the west, straightway ye say, There cometh a shower; and so it cometh to pass.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 55] And when ye see a south wind blowing, ye say, There will be a scorching heat; and it cometh to pass.

[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 57] And why even of yourselves judge ye not what is right?
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 58] For as thou art going with thine adversary before the magistrate, on the way give diligence to be quit of him; lest haply he hale thee unto the judge, and the judge shall deliver thee to the officer, and the officer shall cast thee into prison.
[ERV | EN | NT | Luke | Chapter 12 | Verse 59] I say unto thee, Thou shalt by no means come out thence, till thou have paid the very last mite.
