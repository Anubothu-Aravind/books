[ERV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 33 | Verse 2] And Moses wrote their goings out according to their journeys by the commandment of the LORD: and these are their journeys according to their goings out.
[ERV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 33 | Verse 3] And they journeyed from Rameses in the first month, on the fifteenth day of the first month; on the morrow after the passover the children of Israel went out with an high hand in the sight of all the Egyptians,

[ERV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 33 | Verse 15] And they journeyed from Rephidim, and pitched in the wilderness of Sinai.

[ERV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 33 | Verse 18] And they journeyed from Hazeroth, and pitched in Rithmah.

[ERV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 33 | Verse 55] But if ye will not drive out the inhabitants of the land from before you; then shall those which ye let remain of them be as pricks in your eyes, and as thorns in your sides, and they shall vex you in the land wherein ye dwell.
[ERV | EN | OT | Numbers | Chapter 33 | Verse 56] And it shall come to pass, that as I thought to do unto them, so will I do unto you.
