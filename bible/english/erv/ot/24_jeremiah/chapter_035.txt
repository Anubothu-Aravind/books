[ERV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 35 | Verse 1] The word which came unto Jeremiah from the LORD in the days of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah, king of Judah, saying,
[ERV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 35 | Verse 2] Go unto the house of the Rechabites, and speak unto them, and bring them into the house of the LORD, into one of the chambers, and give them wine to drink.
[ERV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 35 | Verse 3] Then I took Jaazaniah the son of Jeremiah, the son of Habazziniah, and his brethren, and all his sons, and the whole house of the Rechabites;
[ERV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 35 | Verse 4] and I brought them into the house of the LORD, into the chamber of the sons of Hanan the son of Igdaliah, the man of God, which was by the chamber of the princes, which was above the chamber of Maaseiah the son of Shallum, the keeper of the door:
[ERV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 35 | Verse 5] And I set before the sons of the house of the Rechabites bowls full of wine, and cups, and I said unto them, Drink ye wine.
[ERV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 35 | Verse 6] But they said, We will drink no wine: for Jonadab the son of Rechab our father commanded us, saying, Ye shall drink no wine, neither ye, nor your sons, for ever:
[ERV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 35 | Verse 7] neither shall ye build house, nor sow seed, nor plant vineyard, nor have any: but all your days ye shall dwell in tents; that ye may live many days in the land wherein ye sojourn.
[ERV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 35 | Verse 8] And we have obeyed the voice of Jonadab the son of Rechab our father in all that he charged us, to drink no wine all our days, we, our wives, our sons, nor our daughters;
[ERV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 35 | Verse 9] nor to build houses for us to dwell in: neither have we vineyard, nor field, nor seed:
[ERV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 35 | Verse 10] but we have dwelt in tents, and have obeyed, and done according to all that Jonadab our father commanded us.
[ERV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 35 | Verse 11] But it came to pass, when Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon came up into the land, that we said, Come, and let us go to Jerusalem for fear of the army of the Chaldeans, and for fear of the army of the Syrians; so we dwell at Jerusalem.
[ERV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 35 | Verse 12] Then came the word of the LORD unto Jeremiah, saying,
[ERV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 35 | Verse 13] Thus saith the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel: Go, and say to the men of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, Will ye not receive instruction to hearken to my words? saith the LORD.
[ERV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 35 | Verse 14] The words of Jonadab the son of Rechab, that he commanded his sons, not to drink wine, are performed, and unto this day they drink none, for they obey their father’s commandment: but I have spoken unto you, rising up early and speaking; and ye have not hearkened unto me.
[ERV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 35 | Verse 15] I have sent also unto you all my servants the prophets, rising up early and sending them, saying, Return ye now every man from his evil way, and amend your doings, and go not after other gods to serve them, and ye shall dwell in the land which I have given to you and to your fathers: but ye have not inclined your ear, nor hearkened unto me.
[ERV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 35 | Verse 16] Forasmuch as the sons of Jonadab the son of Rechab have performed the commandment of their father which he commanded them, but this people hath not hearkened unto me;
[ERV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 35 | Verse 17] therefore thus saith the LORD, the God of hosts, the God of Israel: Behold, I will bring upon Judah and upon all the inhabitants of Jerusalem all the evil that I have pronounced against them: because I have spoken unto them, but they have not heard; and I have called unto them, but they have not answered.
[ERV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 35 | Verse 18] And Jeremiah said unto the house of the Rechabites, Thus saith the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel: Because ye have obeyed the commandment of Jonadab your father, and kept all his precepts, and done according unto all that he commanded you;
[ERV | EN | OT | Jeremiah | Chapter 35 | Verse 19] therefore thus saith the LORD of hosts, the God of Israel: Jonadab the son of Rechab shall not want a man to stand before me for ever.
