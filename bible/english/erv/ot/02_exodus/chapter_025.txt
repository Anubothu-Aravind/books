[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 1] And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 2] Speak unto the children of Israel, that they take for me an offering: of every man whose heart maketh him willing ye shall take my offering.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 3] And this is the offering which ye shall take of them; gold, and silver, and brass;
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 4] and blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine linen, and goats’ hair;
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 5] and rams’ skins dyed red, and sealskins, and acacia wood;
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 6] oil for the light, spices for the anointing oil, and for the sweet incense;
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 7] onyx stones, and stones to be set, for the ephod, and for the breastplate.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 8] And let them make me a sanctuary; that I may dwell among them.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 9] According to all that I shew thee, the pattern of the tabernacle, and the pattern of all the furniture thereof, even so shall ye make it.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 10] And they shall make an ark of acacia wood: two cubits and a half shall be the length thereof, and a cubit and a half the breadth thereof, and a cubit and a half the height thereof.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 11] And thou shalt overlay it with pure gold, within and without shalt thou overlay it, and shalt make upon it a crown of gold round about.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 12] And thou shalt cast four rings of gold for it, and put them in the four feet thereof; and two rings shall be on the one side of it, and two rings on the other side of it.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 13] And thou shalt make staves of acacia wood, and overlay them with gold.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 14] And thou shalt put the staves into the rings on the sides of the ark, to bear the ark withal.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 15] The staves shall be in the rings of the ark: they shall not be taken from it.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 16] And thou shalt put into the ark the testimony which I shall give thee.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 17] And thou shalt make a mercy-seat of pure gold: two cubits and a half shall be the length thereof, and a cubit and a half the breadth thereof.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 18] And thou shalt make two cherubim of gold; of beaten work shalt thou make them, at the two ends of the mercy-seat.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 19] And make one cherub at the one end, and one cherub at the other end: of one piece with the mercy-seat shall ye make the cherubim on the two ends thereof.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 20] And the cherubim shall spread out their wings on high, covering the mercy-seat with their wings, with their faces one to another; toward the mercy-seat shall the faces of the cherubim be.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 21] And thou shalt put the mercy-seat above upon the ark; and in the ark thou shalt put the testimony that I shall give thee.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 22] And there I will meet with thee, and I will commune with thee from above the mercy-seat, from between the two cherubim which are upon the ark of the testimony, of all things which I will give thee in commandment unto the children of Israel.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 23] And thou shalt make a table of acacia wood: two cubits shall be the length thereof, and a cubit the breadth thereof, and a cubit and a half the height thereof.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 24] And thou shalt overlay it with pure gold, and make thereto a crown of gold round about.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 25] And thou shalt make unto it a border of an handbreadth round about, and thou shalt make a golden crown to the border thereof round about.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 26] And thou shalt make for it four rings of gold, and put the rings in the four corners that are on the four feet thereof.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 27] Close by the border shall the rings be, for places for the staves to bear the table.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 28] And thou shalt make the staves of acacia wood, and overlay them with gold, that the table may be borne with them.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 29] And thou shalt make the dishes thereof, and the spoons thereof, and the flagons thereof, and the bowls thereof, to pour out withal: of pure gold shalt thou make them.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 30] And thou shalt set upon the table shewbread before me alway.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 31] And thou shalt make a candlestick of pure gold: of beaten work shall the candlestick be made, even its base, and its shaft; its cups, its knops, and its flowers, shall be of one piece with it:
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 32] and there shall be six branches going out of the sides thereof; three branches of the candlestick out of the one side thereof, and three branches of the candlestick out of the other side thereof:
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 33] three cups made like almond-blossoms in one branch, a knop and a flower; and three cups made like almond-blossoms in the other branch, a knop and a flower: so for the six branches going out of the candlestick:
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 34] and in the candlestick four cups made like almond-blossoms, the knops thereof, and the flowers thereof:
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 35] and a knop under two branches of one piece with it, and a knop under two branches of one piece with it, and a knop under two branches of one piece with it, for the six branches going out of the candlestick.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 36] Their knops and their branches shall be of one piece with it: the whole of it one beaten work of pure gold.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 37] And thou shalt make the lamps thereof, seven: and they shall light the lamps thereof, to give light over against it.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 38] And the tongs thereof, and the snuffdishes thereof, shall be of pure gold.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 39] Of a talent of pure gold shall it be made, with all these vessels.
[ERV | EN | OT | Exodus | Chapter 25 | Verse 40] And see that thou make them after their pattern, which hath been shewed thee in the mount.
