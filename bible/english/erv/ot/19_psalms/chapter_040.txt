[ERV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 40 | Verse 1] I waited patiently for the LORD; and he inclined unto me, and heard my cry.
[ERV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 40 | Verse 2] He brought me up also out of an horrible pit, out of the miry clay; and he set my feet upon a rock, and established my goings.
[ERV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 40 | Verse 3] And he hath put a new song in my mouth, even praise unto our God: many shall see it, and fear, and shall trust in the LORD.
[ERV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 40 | Verse 4] Blessed is the man that maketh the LORD his trust, and respecteth not the proud, nor such as turn aside to lies.
[ERV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 40 | Verse 5] Many, O LORD my God, are the wonderful works which thou hast done, and thy thoughts which are to us-ward: they cannot be set in order unto thee; if I would declare and speak of them, they are more than can be numbered.
[ERV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 40 | Verse 6] Sacrifice and offering thou hast no delight in; mine ears hast thou opened: burnt offering and sin offering hast thou not required.
[ERV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 40 | Verse 7] Then said I, Lo, I am come; in the roll of the book it is written of me:
[ERV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 40 | Verse 8] I delight to do thy will, O my God; yea, thy law is within my heart.
[ERV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 40 | Verse 9] I have published righteousness in the great congregation; lo, I will not refrain my lips, O LORD, thou knowest.
[ERV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 40 | Verse 10] I have not hid thy righteousness within my heart; I have declared thy faithfulness and thy salvation: I have not concealed thy lovingkindness and thy truth from the great congregation.
[ERV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 40 | Verse 11] Withhold not thou thy tender mercies from me, O LORD: let thy lovingkindness and thy truth continually preserve me.
[ERV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 40 | Verse 12] For innumerable evils have compassed me about, mine iniquities have overtaken me, so that I am not able to look up; they are more than the hairs of mine head, and my heart hath failed me.
[ERV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 40 | Verse 13] Be pleased, O LORD, to deliver me: make haste to help me, O LORD.
[ERV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 40 | Verse 14] Let them be ashamed and confounded together that seek after my soul to destroy it: let them be turned backward and brought to dishonour that delight in my hurt.
[ERV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 40 | Verse 15] Let them be desolate by reason of their shame that say unto me, Aha, Aha.
[ERV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 40 | Verse 16] Let all those that seek thee rejoice and be glad in thee: let such as love thy salvation say continually, The LORD be magnified.
[ERV | EN | OT | Psalms | Chapter 40 | Verse 17] But I am poor and needy; yet the Lord thinketh upon me: thou art my help and my deliverer; make no tarrying, O my God.
