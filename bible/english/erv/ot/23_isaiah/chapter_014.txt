[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 3] And it shall come to pass in the day that the LORD shall give thee rest from thy sorrow, and from thy trouble, and from the hard service wherein thou wast made to serve,

[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 6] that smote the peoples in wrath with a continual stroke, that ruled the nations in anger, with a persecution that none restrained.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 7] The whole earth is at rest, and is quiet: they break forth into singing.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 8] Yea, the fir trees rejoice at thee, and the cedars of Lebanon, saying, Since thou art laid down, no feller is come up against us.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 9] Hell from beneath is moved for thee to meet thee at thy coming: it stirreth up the dead for thee, even all the chief ones of the earth; it hath raised up from their thrones all the kings of the nations.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 10] All they shall answer and say unto thee, Art thou also become weak as we? art thou become like unto us?
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 11] Thy pomp is brought down to hell, and the noise of thy viols: the worm is spread under thee, and worms cover thee.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 12] How art thou fallen from heaven, O day star, son of the morning! how art thou cut down to the ground, which didst lay low the nations!
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 13] And thou saidst in thine heart, I will ascend into heaven, I will exalt my throne above the stars of God; and I will sit upon the mount of congregation, in the uttermost parts of the north:
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 14] I will ascend above the heights of the clouds; I will be like the Most High.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 15] Yet thou shalt be brought down to hell, to the uttermost parts of the pit.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 16] They that see thee shall narrowly look upon thee, they shall consider thee, saying, Is this the man that made the earth to tremble, that did shake kingdoms;
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 17] that made the world as a wilderness, and overthrew the cities thereof; that let not loose his prisoners to their home?
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 18] All the kings of the nations, all of them, sleep in glory, every one in his own house.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 19] But thou art cast forth away from thy sepulchre like an abominable branch, clothed with the slain, that are thrust through with the sword, that go down to the stones of the pit; as a carcase trodden under foot.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 20] Thou shalt not be joined with them in burial, because thou hast destroyed thy land, thou hast slain thy people; the seed of evil-doers shall not be named for ever.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 21] Prepare ye slaughter for his children for the iniquity of their fathers; that they rise not up, and possess the earth, and fill the face of the world with cities.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 22] And I will rise up against them, saith the LORD of hosts, and cut off from Babylon name and remnant, and son and son’s son, saith the LORD.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 23] I will also make it a possession for the porcupine, and pools of water: and I will sweep it with the besom of destruction, saith the LORD of hosts.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 24] The LORD of hosts hath sworn, saying, Surely as I have thought, so shall it come to pass; and as I have purposed, so shall it stand:
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 25] that I will break the Assyrian in my land, and upon my mountains tread him under foot: then shall his yoke depart from off them, and his burden depart from off their shoulder.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 26] This is the purpose that is purposed upon the whole earth: and this is the hand that is stretched out upon all the nations.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 27] For the LORD of hosts hath purposed, and who shall disannul it? and his hand is stretched out, and who shall turn it back?
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 28] In the year that king Ahaz died was this burden.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 29] Rejoice not, O Philistia, all of thee, because the rod that smote thee is broken: for out of the serpent’s root shall come forth a basilisk, and his fruit shall be a fiery flying serpent.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 30] And the firstborn of the poor shall feed, and the needy shall lie down in safety: and I will kill thy root with famine, and thy remnant shall be slain.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 31] Howl, O gate; cry, O city; thou art melted away, O Philistia, all of thee; for there cometh a smoke out of the north, and none standeth aloof at his appointed times.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 14 | Verse 32] What then shall one answer the messengers of the nation? That the LORD hath founded Zion, and in her shall the afflicted of his people take refuge.
