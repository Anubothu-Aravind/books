[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 1] Woe to the rebellious children, saith the LORD, that take counsel, but not of me; and that cover with a covering, but not of my spirit, that they may add sin to sin:
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 2] that walk to go down into Egypt, and have not asked at my mouth; to strengthen themselves in the strength of Pharaoh, and to trust in the shadow of Egypt!
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 3] Therefore shall the strength of Pharaoh be your shame, and the trust in the shadow of Egypt your confusion.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 4] For his princes are at Zoan, and his ambassadors are come to Hanes.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 5] They shall all be ashamed of a people that cannot profit them, that are not an help nor profit, but a shame, and also a reproach.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 6] The burden of the beasts of the South. Through the land of trouble and anguish, from whence come the lioness and the lion, the viper and fiery flying serpent, they carry their riches upon the shoulders of young asses, and their treasures upon the bunches of camels, to a people that shall not profit them.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 7] For Egypt helpeth in vain, and to no purpose: therefore have I called her Rahab that sitteth still.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 8] Now go, write it before them on a tablet, and inscribe it in a book, that it may be for the time to come for ever and ever.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 9] For it is a rebellious people, lying children, children that will not hear the law of the LORD:
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 10] which say to the seers, See not; and to the prophets, Prophesy not unto us right things, speak unto us smooth things, prophesy deceits:
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 11] get you out of the way, turn aside out of the path, cause the Holy One of Israel to cease from before us.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 12] Wherefore thus saith the Holy One of Israel, Because ye despise this word, and trust in oppression and perverseness, and stay thereon;
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 13] therefore this iniquity shall be to you as a breach ready to fall, swelling out in a high wall, whose breaking cometh suddenly at an instant.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 14] And he shall break it as a potter’s vessel is broken, breaking it in pieces without sparing; so that there shall not be found among the pieces thereof a sherd to take fire from the hearth, or to take water withal out of the cistern.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 15] For thus said the Lord GOD, the Holy One of Israel, In returning and rest shall ye be saved; in quietness and in confidence shall be your strength: and ye would not.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 16] but ye said, No, for we will flee upon horses; therefore shall ye flee: and, We will ride upon the swift; therefore shall they that pursue you be swift.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 17] One thousand shall flee at the rebuke of one; at the rebuke of five shall ye flee: till ye be left as a beacon upon the top of a mountain, and as an ensign on an hill.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 18] And therefore will the LORD wait, that he may be gracious unto you, and therefore will he be exalted, that he may have mercy upon you: for the LORD is a God of judgment; blessed are all they that wait for him.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 19] For the people shall dwell in Zion at Jerusalem: thou shalt weep no more; he will surely be gracious unto thee at the voice of thy cry; when he shall hear, he will answer thee.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 20] And though the Lord give you the bread of adversity and the water of affliction, yet shall not thy teachers be hidden any more, but thine eyes shall see thy teachers:
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 21] and thine ears shall hear a word behind thee, saying, This is the way, walk ye in it; when ye turn to the right hand and when e turn to the left.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 22] And ye shall defile the overlaying of thy graven images of silver, and the plating of thy molten images of gold: thou shalt cast them away as an unclean thing; thou shalt say unto it, Get thee hence.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 23] And he shall give the rain of thy seed, that thou shalt sow the ground withal; and bread of the increase of the ground, and it shall be fat and plenteous: in that day shall thy cattle feed in large pastures.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 24] the oxen likewise and the young asses that till the ground shall eat savoury provender, which hath been winnowed with the shovel and with the fan.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 25] And there shall be upon every lofty mountain, and upon every high hill, rivers and streams of waters, in the day of the great slaughter, when the towers fall.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 26] Moreover the light of the moon shall be as the light of the sun, and the light of the sun shall be sevenfold, as the light of seven days, in the day that the LORD bindeth up the hurt of his people, and healeth the stroke of their wound.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 27] Behold, the name of the LORD cometh from far, burning with his anger, and in thick rising smoke: his lips are full of indignation, and his tongue is as a devouring fire:
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 28] and his breath is as an overflowing stream, that reacheth even unto the neck, to sift the nations with the sieve of vanity: and a bridle that causeth to err shall be in the jaws of the peoples.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 29] Ye shall have a song as in the night when a holy feast is kept; and gladness of heart, as when one goeth with a pipe to come into the mountain of the LORD, to the Rock of Israel.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 30] And the LORD shall cause his glorious voice to be heard, and shall shew the lighting down of his arm, with the indignation of his anger, and the flame of a devouring fire, with a blast, and tempest, and hailstones.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 31] For through the voice of the LORD shall the Assyrian be broken in pieces, which smote with a rod.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 32] And every stroke of the appointed staff, which the LORD shall lay upon him, shall be with tabrets and harps: and in battles of shaking will he fight with them.
[ERV | EN | OT | Isaiah | Chapter 30 | Verse 33] For a Topheth is prepared of old; yea, for the king it is made ready; he hath made it deep and large: the pile thereof is fire and much wood; the breath of the LORD, like a stream of brimstone, doth kindle it.
