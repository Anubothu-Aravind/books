[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 1] Now David was old and full of days; and he made Solomon his son king over Israel.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 2] And he gathered together all the princes of Israel, with the priests and the Levites.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 3] And the Levites were numbered from thirty years old and upward: and their number by their polls, man by man, was thirty and eight thousand.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 4] Of these, twenty and four thousand were to oversee the work of the house of the LORD; and six thousand were officers and judges:
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 5] and four thousand were doorkeepers; and four thousand praised the LORD with the instruments which I made, said David, to praise therewith.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 6] And David divided them into courses according to the sons of Levi; Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 7] Of the Gershonites; Ladan and Shimei.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 8] The sons of Ladan; Jehiel the chief, and Zetham, and Joel, three.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 9] The sons of Shimei; Shelomoth, and Haziel, and Haran, three. These were the heads of the fathers’ houses of Ladan.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 10] And the sons of Shimei; Jahath, Zina, and Jeush, and Beriah. These four were the sons of Shimei.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 11] And Jahath was the chief, and Zizah the second: but Jeush and Beriah had not many sons; therefore they became a fathers’ house in one reckoning.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 12] The sons of Kohath; Amram, Izhar, Hebron, and Uzziel, four.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 13] The sons of Amram; Aaron and Moses: and Aaron was separated, that he should sanctify the most holy things, he and his sons, for ever, to burn incense before the LORD, to minister unto him, and to bless in his name, for ever.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 14] But as for Moses the man of God, his sons were named among the tribe of Levi.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 15] The sons of Moses; Gershom and Eliezer.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 16] The sons of Gershom; Shebuel the chief.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 17] And the sons of Eliezer were, Rehabiah the chief. And Eliezer had none other sons; but the sons of Rehabiah were very many.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 18] The sons of Izhar; Shelomith the chief.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 19] The sons of Hebron; Jeriah the chief, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, and Jekameam the fourth.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 20] The sons of Uzziel; Micah the chief, and Isshiah the second.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 21] The sons of Merari; Mahli and Mushi. The sons of Mahli; Eleazar and Kish.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 22] And Eleazar died, and had no sons, but daughters only: and their brethren the sons of Kish took them to wife.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 23] The sons of Mushi; Mahli, and Eder, and Jeremoth, three.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 24] These were the sons of Levi after their fathers’ houses, even the heads of the fathers’ houses of those of them that were counted, in the number of names by their polls, who did the work for the service of the house of the LORD, from twenty years old and upward.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 25] For David said, The LORD, the God of Israel, hath given rest unto his people; and he dwelleth in Jerusalem for ever:
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 26] and also the Levites shall no more have need to carry the tabernacle and all the vessels of it for the service thereof.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 27] For by the last words of David the sons of Levi were numbered, from twenty years old and upward.
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 28] For their office was to wait on the sons of Aaron for the service of the house of the LORD, in the courts, and in the chambers, and in the purifying of all holy things, even the work of the service of the house of God;
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 29] for the shewbread also, and for the fine flour for a meal offering, whether of unleavened wafers, or of that which is baked in the pan, or of that which is soaked, and for all manner of measure and size;
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 30] and to stand every morning to thank and praise the LORD, and likewise at even;
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 31] and to offer all burnt offerings unto the LORD, in the sabbaths, in the new moons, and on the set feast, in number according to the ordinance concerning them, continually before the LORD:
[ERV | EN | OT | 1 Chronicles | Chapter 23 | Verse 32] and that they should keep the charge of the tent of meeting, and the charge of the holy place, and the charge of the sons of Aaron their brethren, for the service of the house of the LORD.
