# Tradition Corpus Sources & Provenance

This document tracks the sources, translators, editions, and licenses for the historical documents in the `tradition/` directory.

---

## 1. Early Christian Texts

### Didache
- **Work**: The Teaching of the Twelve Apostles (Didache)
- **Format**: Plain text chapters
- **Location**: `tradition/early_christian/didache/`
- **Source**: [New Advent](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0714.htm)
- **License**: The original Greek text is Public Domain. The translation/transcription is provided under fair-use for research purposes; redistributors should verify terms.

---

## 2. Ecumenical & Regional Councils

### Council of Laodicea
- **Work**: Canons of the Council of Laodicea (circa 363-364 AD)
- **Format**: Canon 29 text
- **Location**: `tradition/councils/laodicea/`
- **Source**: [New Advent](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/3806.htm)
- **License**: The historical canons are Public Domain. The English translation is sourced from the Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers translation series (NPNF2, Vol. 14, public domain), transcribed by New Advent.

---

## 3. Patristic Writings

### Augustine's De Trinitate
- **Work**: De Trinitate (On the Trinity)
- **Format**: Book IV, Chapter 5 excerpt
- **Location**: `tradition/patristics/augustine/de_trinitate/`
- **Source**: [New Advent](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/130104.htm)
- **License**: Augustine's original Latin is Public Domain. The English translation is from the Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers series (NPNF1, Vol. 3, public domain).

### Chrysostom's In Diem Natalem
- **Work**: In Diem Natalem (On the Birth of Our Savior)
- **Format**: Opening section excerpt
- **Location**: `tradition/patristics/chrysostom/in_diem_natalem/`
- **Source**: [Early Church Texts](https://earlychurchtexts.com/public/chrysostom_homily_on_christmas.htm)
- **License**: Original Greek is Public Domain. The English translation is used for research purposes; verify terms for redistribution.
